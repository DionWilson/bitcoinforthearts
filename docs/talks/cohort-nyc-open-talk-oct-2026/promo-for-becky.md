## Short event blurb (for IG / email / Eventbrite-style)

Former professional ballet dancer Dion Wilson founded Bitcoin for the Arts after a career on stages from Alvin Ailey to *The Lion King*. In this Open Talk, he traces a path from living as an artist inside the fiat economy to building Bitcoin-native patronage, and asks what happens to creativity when we move from industrial-age arts systems into an information age with digitized sound money. For Gen Z and millennials coming of age at a societal hinge: how do artists and builders reclaim sovereignty, own more of what they make, and cultivate the next frontier together?

Expect a conversational presentation with three open discussion moments. Not a corporate panel. Not a hard pitch.

## Speaker bio (edited from Becky's draft)

**Recommended bio:**

Dion Wilson is a former professional ballet dancer with sixteen years on company stages and twenty-five years in the industry, including Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater, Dance Theatre of Harlem, Philadanco, and Disney's *The Lion King*. He is a teacher, personal trainer, and the creator of the fitness class DBarre. He founded Bitcoin for the Arts, a New York 501(c)(3) building Bitcoin-native patronage to support creators across visual arts, theater, dance, music, writing, storytelling, and film.
