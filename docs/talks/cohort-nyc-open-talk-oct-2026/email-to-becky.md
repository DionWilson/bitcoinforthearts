Artists at the End of an Era: Sound Money, Sovereignty, and the Information Age

**Bio** (edited from your draft)  
Dion Wilson is a former professional ballet dancer with sixteen years on company stages and twenty-five years in the industry, including Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater, Dance Theatre of Harlem, Philadanco, and Disney's *The Lion King*. He is a teacher, personal trainer, and the creator of the fitness class DBarre. He founded Bitcoin for the Arts, a New York 501(c)(3) building Bitcoin-native patronage to support creators across visual arts, theater, dance, music, writing, storytelling, and film.

**Short blurb**  
Former professional dancer Dion Wilson founded Bitcoin for the Arts after a career on stages from Alvin Ailey to *The Lion King*. This Open Talk traces a path from living as an artist inside the fiat economy to building Bitcoin-native patronage, and asks what happens to creativity as we move from industrial-age arts systems into an information age with digitized sound money. Built for Gen Z and millennials at a societal hinge: sovereignty, ownership, and cultivating the next frontier. Conversational presentation with three open discussion moments.
