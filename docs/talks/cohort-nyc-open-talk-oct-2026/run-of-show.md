## Room goals

Audience: Gen Z and millennials (creatives, founders, curious New Yorkers) looking for orientation at a societal hinge.

By the end they should be able to:
1. Connect an artist's lived money problem to monetary design (not just "hustle harder")
2. Contrast arts under the industrial age with arts under the information age
3. See Bitcoin as digitized sound money that can sit beside digitized culture
4. Leave with one personal sovereignty experiment (not a token purchase pitch)

Tone: salon, not TED. Examples from Dion's dance career and BFTA, framed as evidence for larger questions.

## The three discussion prompts (print for yourself)

1. **Where do you feel the old "artist career map" breaking down in your own life or industry?**  
2. **If artists could own more of the value they create in the information age, what would change first for you: time, rights, audience, or pay?**  
3. **What is one experiment you could run in the next 30 days to increase your sovereignty as a creator or builder?**

## Story beats to hit (do not read as script)

### Act I
- Sixteen years dancing (Ailey, DTH, Philadanco, Lion King); twenty-five years in the industry  
- Body knows value; fiat career fog: irregular pay, gatekeepers, inflation, cuts  
- Union / advocacy work (Lion King rep, Ailey AGMA) = dignity without a monetary fix  
- Bitcoin as structural question, not lottery ticket  
- Founding BFTA as "NEA of the Bitcoin Era" practice: grants, education, public culture, reserve  

### Act II
- Money shapes who creates and who owns  
- Industrial age: mass media, central gatekeepers, artist as input  
- Hinge generation: platforms, AI, collapsing trust, search for durable value  
- Information age: global distribution + new scarcity (attention, authenticity)  
- Digitization of archives is necessary; sound money completes the stack  

### Act III
- Sovereignty = agency over value, rights, continuity  
- Tool stack: Bitcoin/Lightning, direct channels, portable reputation, archives, patronage communities  
- Gen Z / millennials can cultivate the frontier, not only inherit the last era  
- BFTA as living proof, light touch, then experiment + open floor
