A two-way conversation between Dion Wilson, founder of Bitcoin For The Arts, and Kyle Huber, filmmaker and host of We Are Satoshi. Recorded in Kyle's yard in Seattle on October 16, 2026. First published on his channel. Posted here so our audience can find it too.

Kyle's episode: https://youtu.be/w5x1dTXvX4E
We Are Satoshi: https://www.youtube.com/@WeAreSatoshiPod

Dion danced for 16 years, including the national tour of Disney's The Lion King, then found Bitcoin while he was building the next chapter of his life. Kyle left client video work, came back to it in 2024, and has spent the last two years filming Bitcoin adoption, including a first trip to El Salvador and projects with My First Bitcoin. They talk about why artists get clarity when they find sound money, and why holding bitcoin is not the same thing as never spending it.

Sound money means money with a fixed supply. Bitcoin will only ever have 21 million. Dollars can be printed. A song, a painting, or a film cannot. That is the bridge this conversation is about: artists getting paid directly, and Bitcoin people actually using the money.

Kyle bought a piece by Lady Nakamoto at Bitcoin Is For Everyone in Portland. It was her first sale in bitcoin, and it was his first time buying Bitcoin art. He still looks at it every day.
