=== DESCRIPTION ===

Rami Korhonen has sung in the Finnish melodic punk band Fumble since the early nineties. One of their songs opens an episode of Law & Order: Special Victims Unit. On long business trips he found a guitar in a rented apartment and started writing songs about Bitcoin, with one rule: the songs never say the word. He calls it acoustic pleb rock. The project is The Higher Low.

In this episode, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Rami for the whole road. Seeing the Ramones in Helsinki at sixteen. Thirty years of DIY punk, including the nights with two people and a dog. Twelve songs recorded in one four-hour session. The Bitcoin Beats invitation that turned them into the album Node To Freedom. Opening Bitcoin conference stages with one voice and one guitar.

He explains why a song has to work for someone who has never heard of Bitcoin, what the name The Higher Low means, and why he misses the freedom-tech years before the treasury companies. He tells the story of Knut Svanholm jumping on the drums at Baltic Honeybadger in Riga and slowing Freedom Engine down for good, writing that song in El Zonte, handing the acoustic songs to Jesperi Mommo and the band, and crowdfunding the vinyl for Freedom In the Digital Age on Geyser.

He does not spend the sats his music earns. "One day it's going to be enough to do another album."
