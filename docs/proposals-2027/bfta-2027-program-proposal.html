Why this exists

Working artists are paid, when they are paid, in money that arrives late and shrinks. Public arts dollars spiked on temporary relief and are running off. That pattern is in our published briefs, The ARPA Cliff and The State of Arts Funding 2026. A grant that cannot be held is a cycle. We pay the artist in bitcoin, and we teach the artist how to hold it.

Work already in public

Sixteen artist stories are published on YouTube. Six of those are in the podcast feed. Artist articles are on the website. In 2026 we funded Ainsley Costello, Aksana Zasinets, and Christopher Arcella, and we presented Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit in Columbus with artists on two stages. In October 2026 we began releasing new episodes first on Fanfares, where listeners pay the creator directly, then free on YouTube and RSS. The ten artists in 2027 are the next year of this work, not a recount of 2026.
