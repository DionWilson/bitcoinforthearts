What we did in 2026

In September 2026 we presented Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit in Columbus, Ohio. Live music on the Expo Stage, a youth theatre performance, a painter working live over two days, filmmakers and a cinema program, a culture panel, and a silent auction and raffle that raised money for artists. We built it on our own display and paid for the floor ourselves. This budget is scoped from those actual invoices.
