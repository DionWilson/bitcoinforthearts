The event

A day and a half before the doors open

Bitcoin for the Arts Park bridges the Bitcoin Golf Championship on July 14 and the opening of general admission at the Bitcoin Conference on July 16. The venue partner, 6th and Peabody, is donating the use of the space. A professional stage and an in-house production team let us present artists at a level Nashville will notice.

On the campus

  • Two stages, one inside and one outside: music, theatre, film, presentations, spoken word.
  • Market row of an estimated 20 to 30 10x10 tents for Bitcoin, Nostr, and arts organizations.
  • Two food options and a distillery for dinners, tastings, and signature drinks.
  • A separate outbuilding with its own private bar, for a VIP green room.

Who is in the room

Nashville musicians and the local music industry. People who are new to Bitcoin. The Bitcoin and Nostr community arriving for conference week. The program is built for newcomers first, with the Bitcoin community showing that it works.

We have done this before

In September 2026 we opened Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit in Columbus. Nashville’s Ainsley Costello played a full set with her band. Rock ’n’ Roll Andy Breakheart played the Expo Stage. Short North Stage brought Sweeney Todd with youth performers. Shipwreck Sean painted live over two days. Filmmakers pitched, a cinema slate ran both days, and a silent auction and live-painting raffle raised money for artists.

Ways to take part

Bring your project to Nashville

Spaces are not mapped yet and pricing is not set. This sheet is an interest check. Tell us what you want to do and we will come back with a site map, pricing, and terms once they are confirmed with the venue. Vendor space will cost a fraction of conference exhibitor rates, and every dollar goes to Bitcoin For The Arts to pay the artists who come to Nashville.

01 · Vendors

Market row tent

A 10x10 space in the market row to sell, demo, and meet people on the two days before the conference opens.

An estimated 20 to 30 tents. Placement, power, and internet confirmed with the site map.

02 · Experiences

Host a tasting or a dinner

Work with OSD Distilling and the on-site kitchens on a hosted dinner, a tasting flight, a signature drink, or a signature bottle. Ticketed or invite-only.

All food and bar service is run by the venue.

03 · VIP

Host the green room

A separate outbuilding with its own private bar. Host the room where artists, sponsors, and guests meet, under your name.

One host. Format agreed with the venue and production team.

04 · Stage

Sponsor a stage or an artist

Sponsor a stage block or a headline act. Or cover one artist’s pay and travel to Nashville. The credit reads: [Artist] presented by [your company].

Every artist on the bill is paid.

05 · In kind

Gear, lodging, travel, print, media, crew

Support that is not cash still moves artists into the room. In-kind gifts are logged and credited the same way as cash support.

Recognition

Sponsors and vendors are credited on the Park map and event page, thanked from the stage, and listed in the BFTA newsletter and our public transparency report. Final recognition is set in each agreement.

Why it matters

Artists get paid. In sound money.

Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. is a New York 501(c)(3) nonprofit and the first nonprofit paying artists exclusively in Bitcoin, through micro-grants, education, and live programming. We follow a published rule for every dollar: 55 percent to artist grants, 30 percent to programs, 10 percent to operations, 5 percent to a long-term Bitcoin reserve.

Money raised at the Park pays the artists who perform. We do not ask artists to play for free, and we do not pay some and not others.
