## Pricing

### Benchmarks

| Event | 10x10 cost to a small vendor |
| --- | --- |
| Bitcoin 2026 Las Vegas, Bitcoin Bazaar | $6,000 flat, includes 2 exhibitor passes |
| Bitcoin Hong Kong, small booth | about $25,000 |
| Midwest Bitcoin Summit 2026 (Lindey, through Andrew) | $500 |

### Recommended rates (both days unless noted)

| Rate | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maker (bring your own canopy) | $250 ($150 one day) | 10x10 space, listing on the Park map and site, social shoutout |
| Brand (bring your own canopy) | $500 | Same, plus a logo on the market row sign |
| Community or nonprofit | $100, or free with 4 volunteer hours | 10x10 space, listing |
| Add-on: tent, table, and 2 chairs supplied | +$150 | We rent and set up |
| Add-on: power | +$50 | Only if the venue site map allows it |

The maker rate is half of Columbus and about 96% below the Bitcoin Bazaar. That is the line for the email: "less than Columbus, a fraction of the conference floor."

### Can we afford it?

It depends on two things: how many tents we sell, and whether vendors bring their own canopies. Market row costs that do not move much with vendor count are signage, insurance, a market row lead, and any venue fees. Rough budget for those: $2,000 to $3,000 (confirm once the venue sends terms).

| Tents sold | Average fee | Revenue | After $2,500 fixed costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | $300 | $3,000 | $500 |
| 20 | $350 | $7,000 | $4,500 |
| 30 | $350 | $10,500 | $8,000 |

- Below about 10 paid tents, the market row is a cost to BFTA, not a revenue line. That is still fine if it is framed as a program (it fills the Park and supports small Bitcoin businesses), but decide that on purpose.
- Supplied tents are pass-through. Price the add-on at or above our rental cost so it never loses money.
- If BTC Media comes in, the market row is a smaller piece of the budget and we can hold these rates easily. If they do not, hold the maker rate and lean on brand tents and sponsors.
