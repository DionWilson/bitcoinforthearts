## The One-Liner

**Art + Zap Weekend is the first global, Bitcoin-native arts fundraiser — 21 artists, 2 days, 8 hours of live programming broadcast across YouTube, X, and Nostr, culminating in a live in-person finale at Pubkey NYC.**

---

## Why Now

Federal arts funding is collapsing. The NEA has been targeted for elimination in two consecutive federal budgets, and 2025 saw mass termination of previously approved grants. At the same time, Bitcoin and the Lightning Network have made it possible — for the first time in history — for an audience anywhere in the world to send instant, micro-value payments directly to an artist on stage.

We are not waiting for institutions to decide whether artists deserve funding. We are building a parallel system that is censorship-resistant, globally accessible, and aligned with the values of the Bitcoin community.

**Art + Zap Weekend is the proof of concept.**
