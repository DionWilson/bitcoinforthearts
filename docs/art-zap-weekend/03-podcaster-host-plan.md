# Art + Zap Weekend — Podcaster Host Plan

**Goal:** Confirm at least **4 podcasters per broadcast day** (8 total across the weekend), each committing **1–2 hours** on-air. Plus 1 backup per day in case of cancellation.

The right podcaster does two things for this event:

1. **They host on-air** — they are the trusted voice that interviews artists, reads sponsor mentions, and prompts zaps.
2. **They bring their audience** — every confirmed podcaster comes with a built-in promotion channel (their show, newsletter, social, and Nostr following).

A podcaster host is worth more than a paid host, because the audience comes with the booking.

---

## Why 4 podcasters × 1–2 hours (not 2 × 4 hours)

A **lighter ask = more yeses from bigger podcasters.** Major Bitcoin podcasters will rarely commit a full 4-hour day, but most will gladly come on for an hour. This format:

- **Lowers the barrier** to a yes from podcasters whose audience you actually want
- **Allows top-tier podcasters to drop in for one segment** (~25 min, one artist) if that's all they can spare — billed as "guest co-host"
- **Builds in natural handoffs** that double as promotional moments for each show
- **Provides redundancy** — if one podcaster cancels last-minute, you're missing one hour, not the whole day

**Backups:** 1 per day, on call.

### Pattern: 5–10 minute overlaps

Each transition has a 5–10 minute overlap so the outgoing podcaster gets a chance to plug their show before signing off and the incoming one settles into the seat naturally on-camera. Producer cues the handoff.

### Anchor option: 1 podcaster who stays longer

If a particularly mission-aligned podcaster wants the anchor role and is willing to commit 2.5–3 hours, that's preferred — provides continuity. But not required. The 1–2 hour pattern works fine without an anchor.

### "Drop-in for one segment" option (for the biggest names)

For podcasters whose audience you really want but who can't spare 1–2 hours: offer a **single artist-segment co-host slot (~25 minutes)**. They come on, co-host one specific artist's interview, plug their show, leave. Billed as "Guest Co-Host" in event materials. Lowest possible ask, highest possible yes rate from major podcasters.

## Tax-Receipt Treatment for Donated Podcaster Time — IMPORTANT

You asked whether you can give a tax receipt for the value of donated podcaster time. **Short answer: no — IRS Publication 526 explicitly states that donated time and services are not tax-deductible to the donor.** Even when the service has a clear fair market value (e.g., "my normal rate is $5,000 to host a 4-hour event").

What you **CAN** do — and should — for every donating podcaster:

### 1. Issue a written acknowledgment letter (NOT a tax receipt)

This is **not a tax deduction**, but it is documentation a podcaster can use for their own records, sponsor pitches for their own show, marketing one-sheet, or year-end PR. Many podcasters value this nearly as much as a tax receipt.

**Template language** (run past your CPA before sending):

> Dear [Podcaster Name],
>
> On behalf of Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc., a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit (EIN: [EIN]), we acknowledge with deep gratitude your contribution of professional podcast hosting services to **Art + Zap Weekend** on [date(s)].
>
> Specifically, you contributed approximately **[X] hours** of on-air hosting time, plus preparation and promotion across your podcast, social media, and Nostr channels. The fair market value of equivalent professional hosting services, based on your standard rate, is approximately **$[amount]**.
>
> Per IRS guidelines (Publication 526), the value of contributed services is not tax-deductible. However, we want to formally recognize and document your generous in-kind contribution to our 501(c)(3) mission of funding artists in Bitcoin.
>
> Any out-of-pocket expenses you incurred in connection with this contribution (travel, mileage, supplies) **may be tax-deductible** — please consult your tax advisor. We are happy to issue a separate receipt for documented expenses.
>
> Thank you for helping make this event possible.
>
> Sincerely,
> Dion Wilson, Founder & Director
> Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.

### 2. Their out-of-pocket expenses ARE deductible

If a podcaster travels to NYC for the in-person finale, their travel and lodging costs are tax-deductible (subject to IRS rules). Issue a separate, itemized expense receipt.

### 3. Public recognition is the real currency

The most valuable thing you can give a podcaster is **on-air promotion of their show, their LN address, and their handles** — which they earn by hosting. The acknowledgment letter is the paperwork; the on-air promotion is the actual reward.

> ⚠️ Have your CPA approve the acknowledgment-letter template before you send the first one. The language above is consistent with published IRS guidance but should be blessed by your tax preparer for your specific situation.

## Outreach Script (Copy-Paste)

Send this via DM, Nostr, or email. Personalize the first line.

> Hey [Name] — big fan of [show/episode reference].
>
> I'm Dion Wilson, founder of Bitcoin For The Arts, the first 501(c)(3) paying artists exclusively in BTC. On [date weekend], we're producing **Art + Zap Weekend** — 2 days of live Bitcoin-native arts programming featuring 21 artists, broadcast on YouTube + Zap.Stream + X, culminating in a Monday-night live finale at Pubkey [DC/NYC] headlined by a Broadway performer.
>
> I'd love to have you co-host one of the broadcast days. The ask is genuinely light:
>
> - **1–2 hours on-air on Sat or Sun**, somewhere between 1–5 pm ET — pick the slot that works for you
> - One 30-minute prep call the week before
> - One mention on your show + 1 social post
>
> If 1–2 hours is too much, I'll happily take **just one ~25-minute artist segment** with you billed as Guest Co-Host. Whatever fits your schedule.
>
> In return: top billing as a featured host, your show's logo on the broadcast, dedicated zaps to your podcast LN address during your slot, written acknowledgment letter for your records of the contributed service value, and the artists in your audience get to see you put your weight behind them.
>
> Would Saturday or Sunday work better? Happy to jump on a 10-minute call to walk through it.

### Tone notes

- **Don't lead with the ask.** Lead with the show reference. Bitcoin podcasters get pitched constantly; the ones who succeed at outreach lead with proof of attention.
- **Make the time commitment crystal clear and small.** "1–2 hours" — not "host with us." Even better, offer the 25-minute version as a fallback.
- **Offer reciprocity.** Their podcast LN address gets zaps during their hosting block, and they get an acknowledgment letter for the value of their contributed service. Real, tangible things — not just exposure.
