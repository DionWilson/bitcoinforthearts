# Art + Zap Weekend — Podcaster Host Plan

**Goal:** Confirm at least **2 podcasters per day** as on-air hosts (4 total across the weekend), plus 1 backup per day in case of cancellation.

The right podcaster does two things for this event:

1. **They host on-air** — they are the trusted voice that interviews artists, reads sponsor mentions, and prompts zaps.
2. **They bring their audience** — every confirmed podcaster comes with a built-in promotion channel (their show, newsletter, social, and Nostr following).

A podcaster host is worth more than a paid host, because the audience comes with the booking.

Each podcaster commits to:

- 4 hours on-air on their assigned day

**That's it.** Light enough that a podcaster will say yes if they care about the mission.

## Outreach Script (Copy-Paste)

Send this via DM, Nostr, or email. Personalize the first line.

> Hey [Name] — big fan of [show/episode reference].
>
> I'm Dion Wilson, founder of Bitcoin For The Arts (the first 501(c)(3) paying artists exclusively in BTC). On [date weekend], we're producing **Art + Zap Weekend** — 2 days, 4 hours per day of live Bitcoin-native arts programming featuring 21 artists, broadcast on YouTube + Zap.Stream + X, culminating in a live finale at Pubkey NYC.
>
> I'd love to have you co-host one of the days. The ask is light:
>
> - 4 hours on-air on Sat **or** Sun, 1–5 pm ET
> - One 30-minute prep call the week before
> - One mention on your show + 1 social post
>
> In return: top billing as a featured host, your show's logo on the broadcast, dedicated zaps to your podcast LN address during your day, and the artists in your audience get to see you put your weight behind them.
>
> Would Saturday or Sunday work better for you? Happy to jump on a 10-minute call to walk through it.

### Tone notes

- **Don't lead with the ask.** Lead with the show reference. Bitcoin podcasters get pitched constantly; the ones who succeed at outreach lead with proof of attention.
- **Be specific about the lift.** "4 hours on Saturday or Sunday, 1–5 pm ET" is much easier to say yes to than "host an event with us."
- **Offer reciprocity.** Their podcast LN address gets zaps during their hosting block. That's a real, tangible thing — not just exposure.
