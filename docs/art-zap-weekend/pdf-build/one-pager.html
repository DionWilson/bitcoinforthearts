Art + Zap Weekend

A Bitcoin-funded fundraiser for Bitcoin For The Arts
Art + Zap Weekend is a Bitcoin-funded fundraiser for Bitcoin For The Arts, our 501(c)(3) that funds artists nationwide through quarterly micro-grants. 2 days of broadcast. 21 featured artists giving their time as mission supporters. Live in-person finale at Pubkey DC with a Broadway-caliber headliner. Audience donations route to BFTA and flow through our public 55/30/10/5 allocation. Featured artists also receive 100% of any optional direct tips sent to their personal Lightning addresses during their broadcast segments.

When

  • A weekend in late August through early October 2026. Saturday + Sunday, 1–5 pm ET each day, broadcast live (specific weekend confirming with venue).
  • Following Monday evening: in-person finale at Pubkey DC headlined by a Broadway performer (Broadway is dark Mondays — top NYC talent uniquely available).

Where

  • Online: YouTube + X + Nostr (Zap.Stream) + Fountain — broadcast simultaneously.
  • In person: Pubkey DC — Bitcoin-native venue with mission-aligned rates.

Who

  • Dion Wilson, Founder/Director. Actors' Equity. Former Dance Theatre of Harlem & Alvin Ailey. Lion King national tour.
  • 2–4 Bitcoin podcaster hosts per day, up to 8 total — 1–2 hours each. Each brings their own audience.
  • 21 featured artists across music, visual art, dance, theater, film, writing.

Why a sponsor cares

  • First-mover story. Sponsors written into the founding narrative of an arts-funding model built on Bitcoin rails.
  • Permanent on-brand content. Logos visible on every clip on YouTube and Nostr in perpetuity.
  • Audience small but high-signal. Bitcoin + arts overlap = high-intent, high-NW, deeply loyal to early supporters.
  • Tax-deductible through a 501(c)(3) with public governance and on-chain transparency.

Today's ask

  • A signed sponsorship at Title, Supporting, or Friend tier — cash, in-kind, or a combination.
  • A specific in-kind contribution (A/V, hardware wallets, catering, silent-auction items).
  • A warm introduction to one company or person you believe should sponsor this event.
