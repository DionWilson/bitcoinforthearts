# Volunteer Outreach — Featured Artist Invitations

**For BFTA volunteers reaching out to artists about Art + Zap Weekend.**

This is the volunteer-facing version of `07-artist-outreach.md`. The Dion-voice founder template is at `07`; use this one when **anyone other than Dion** is doing artist outreach.

---

## Read this first — what's different about volunteer outreach

You are reaching out **on behalf of Bitcoin For The Arts** as a volunteer. That changes a few things from how Dion would write the same email:

1. **You can't promise things only the Director can promise.** Specifically: dates that aren't locked, sponsor commitments that aren't signed, honoraria amounts beyond the published model, finale slot guarantees, anything that requires a contract.
2. **Your job is to surface interest, not to close.** A good volunteer outreach ends in either "yes, I'd love to talk to Dion about being part of this" OR "no thanks." Either is a win.
3. **You're allowed to say "I'll have to check with Dion."** That's not weakness — it protects the artist from miscommunication and protects BFTA from over-promising.
4. **Always cc or bcc Dion** on initial outreach so he can pick up the thread when an artist says yes.

## What you CAN tell artists

- Bitcoin For The Arts is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 41-2642260) that funds artists nationwide through quarterly Bitcoin micro-grants
- Art + Zap Weekend is BFTA's flagship Bitcoin-funded fundraiser, targeting **a weekend in late August through early October 2026** (specific weekend confirming with venue)
- Format: Saturday + Sunday live broadcast (1–5 pm ET each day), Monday in-person finale at Pubkey DC
- 21 featured artist slots across music, dance, theater, visual art, film, writing, storytelling
- This is a **fundraiser FOR BFTA**, not a real-time direct-patronage event. Audience donations route to BFTA and feed our quarterly grant cycle nationwide.
- Featured artists participate as **mission supporters** — donating their time so other artists get funded. **No honorarium.** The artist's personal Lightning address shows on-screen during their segment as an optional tip jar (direct tips = 100% theirs).
- 5 format options the artist can choose from (live performance / live interview + showcase / pre-recorded + live chat / visual showcase / Monday in-person)
- BFTA will issue a written acknowledgment letter for the artist's records (NOT a tax receipt — services aren't deductible per IRS rules — but useful for their own marketing)

## What you CANNOT promise

- ❌ A specific event date (still confirming with venue)
- ❌ Any honorarium (the model is no honorarium for broadcast artists; **the Broadway-caliber Monday headliner is the one paid exception, NOT a slot to dangle in front of regular outreach**)
- ❌ Any post-event "surprise gratitude payment" — this is internal-only, never disclosed in advance, would recreate quid pro quo
- ❌ Auction proceeds or donor-direction promises
- ❌ Sponsor connections or guaranteed press
- ❌ Specific slot times or formats (Dion finalizes the schedule)

If an artist asks about anything in this list, the honest answer is: **"That's a great question. Let me check with Dion and follow up — I'd rather get you the right answer than guess."**

## What to do when an artist says YES

1. **Reply within 24 hours** confirming receipt and that you're looping in Dion
2. **Email Dion** with the artist's name, contact, format choice (if stated), and any specific questions they raised
3. **Add the artist to `tabs/artists.csv`** with status `Verbal yes`
4. **Let Dion send the confirmation email** — he handles slot assignment, tech check scheduling, and the ongoing relationship

The handoff to Dion should look like:

> Hey Dion — [Artist Name] said yes to Art + Zap Weekend. They're interested in [format option, if known]. They asked about [any specific questions].
>
> Email: [artist email]
> Other handles: [Nostr / X / IG]
> Their work: [link]
>
> Want me to make the formal intro to confirm the slot, or are you taking it from here?

---

## What to do when an artist says NO

1. **Reply graciously and ask for one referral** — even one warm intro to an artist they think would be a fit is gold
2. **Update `tabs/artists.csv`** with status `Declined` and a 1-line note on why (helps avoid re-asking the same artist next event)
3. **Move on quickly** — no convincing, no follow-up pressure

A good "no" reply:

> Hey [Artist] — completely understand. Thank you for considering it and for being honest about the timing. Quick ask: is there an artist friend of yours who you think would be a great fit for this? A warm intro from you to one person who'd care about the BFTA mission would be a huge help. No pressure if not.
>
> Either way, we'd love to keep you in mind for the next one. Thanks again.

## Tracking outreach

Use `tabs/artists.csv` (already in this folder). Status values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not contacted | Identified but no outreach sent |
| Contacted | Outreach sent, awaiting reply |
| In conversation | Replied, scheduling a call or trading details |
| Verbal yes | Said yes, format being decided — **handoff to Dion** |
| Confirmed | Slot locked by Dion, format chosen, prep call scheduled |
| Tech check done | Pre-event tech check completed |
| Declined | Pass — log and move on |
| Backup | Said yes but as backup only |

**Aim for 30+ outreaches in the first 2 weeks** to land 21 confirmations + 5 backups. With 5 format options on the table, conversion should be reasonable, but the no-honorarium model means expect a 30–50% decline rate, so volume matters.

## Outreach priority order — who to contact first

Outreach order should be (warmest first, coldest last):

1. **Existing BFTA artist network** — anyone BFTA has already paid through micro-grants, anyone in BFTA's existing community
2. **Bitcoin-native artists** already producing work (NostrArt, Bitcoin musicians on Wavlake/Fountain, BTC visual artists) — they don't need orange-pilling
3. **Sovereign-aligned artists** in the broader arts community who don't need to be Bitcoiners but resonate with the freedom/independence theme
4. **Members of Dion's AEA / AGMA / Broadway / Dance Theatre of Harlem / Ailey networks** — these are warm intros and they trust him; ask Dion before reaching out cold to anyone on these lists
5. **Cold outreach to artists whose work fits the mission** — only after the warmer tiers are tapped

For the **Broadway-caliber Monday finale headliner**, **do NOT do volunteer outreach** — Dion handles that personally using his AEA + Broadway-dancer network.

## When to escalate to Dion

Escalate immediately if an artist:

- Asks about an honorarium beyond what's in this template
- Asks about specific dates / slot times you can't confirm
- Asks about contracts, riders, or formal agreements
- Asks about Broadway finale slot eligibility
- Says yes and is a high-profile name (tech-press attention level — let Dion handle the handoff personally)
- Asks anything about sponsors, donations, or fundraising mechanics beyond what's in this template
- Pushes back on the no-honorarium model and tries to negotiate
- Asks about tax-deductibility of their contributed services

The honest answer to any of these is always: **"Great question — let me loop in our founder Dion who can give you a definitive answer. I'll have him reply within 24 hours."** Then email Dion right away.

## Tone notes

- **Be honest about the no-honorarium model up front.** Burying it sets up a worse conversation later. Artists who would only say yes for cash will say no immediately, which is fine — that's not the audience this event needs.
- **Lead with their work, not the ask.** A specific reference to their work in the first line is the difference between a yes and an ignore.
- **Don't oversell.** Phrases like "this will be huge" or "you'd be missed" or "you have to be part of this" sound desperate and untrue. Honest understatement converts better.
- **Make it easy to say no.** Artists who feel cornered into saying yes will flake later. A clean "no" now is better than a soft "yes" that becomes a flake in July.
