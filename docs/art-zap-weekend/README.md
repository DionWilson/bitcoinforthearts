# Art + Zap Weekend — Sponsor Package

Complete planning package for the Art + Zap Weekend fundraiser:
**2 days of broadcast (Sat + Sun, 1–5 pm ET) + Monday-night in-person finale (Broadway-headlined),
21 artists, broadcast on YouTube + X + Nostr (Zap.Stream),
hosted by 4 podcasters per day at 1–2 hours each,
held at Pubkey DC (primary) or Pubkey NYC / NYC alternative.**

## Quick start for the Tuesday pitch

1. Open `00-one-page-pitch.md` and fill in `[DATE]`, `[LN address]`, `[BTC address]`, `[npub]`, `[EIN]`. Print 5 copies.
2. Skim `02-sponsor-tiers-and-budget.md` so you can:
   - Defend the $5K – $25K range and the 9-slot cap
   - Explain how event costs flow through the 30% Programs bucket without breaking the published 55/30/10/5 model

4. Update the Google Sheet using `04-spreadsheet-update.md` and the CSVs in `tabs/`.

## Format decisions baked into this package

- **Saturday + Sunday broadcast**, 1–5 pm ET (4 hrs each, 8 hrs total)
- **Monday-night in-person finale** with a **Broadway-caliber headliner** (Broadway is dark on Mondays = top NYC talent uniquely available)
- **21 artists**, 10 + 11 split across the broadcast days, with up to 4 also performing at the Monday finale
- **4 podcaster hosts per broadcast day** committing 1–2 hours each (8 + 2 backups), with **25-min drop-in slots** available for big-name podcasters who can't spare a full block
- **9 sponsor slots maximum** ($25K Title + 3 × $10K Supporting + 5 × $5K Friend)
- **Pubkey DC** as primary in-person venue; **Pubkey NYC / NYC alternative** as backup; an **NYC venue contributed in-kind** is itself one of the strongest sponsorship opportunities
- **Producer staffing scales with funding**: solo at floor, hired professional at target, full team at stretch
- **55/30/10/5 model honored** — event costs sit naturally inside the 30% Programs allocation; no policy change required

## Important policy notes

- **Donated podcaster time is NOT tax-deductible** to the donor (IRS Pub 526). BFTA issues a written **acknowledgment letter** documenting the FMV — see `05-podcaster-acknowledgment-letter-template.md`. Have your CPA approve the template before sending.
- **Pubkey DC exists** at https://www.pubkey.bar/dc/home — primary venue option for this event.
- **BTCPay / Lightning infrastructure** is **not** listed as an in-kind ask because BFTA already runs its own BTCPay Server. This is a strength to highlight, not a gap to fill.
