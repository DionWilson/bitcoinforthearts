## Common Objections & How to Handle Them

### "How big is the audience?"

> "Honest answer: we're in our first year and don't have prior numbers to point to. What we can promise is high-intent, high-net-worth, deeply engaged Bitcoin + arts overlap, and **permanent on-platform content** — every clip lives forever on YouTube and Nostr with your branding visible. Sponsors here are buying the founding-narrative moment, not a CPM."

### "How do I know my money goes to artists?"

> "BFTA publishes its allocation: 55% to direct artist grants, 30% to programs, 10% ops, 5% to a permanent Bitcoin reserve. Every dollar follows that model. Each of the 21 featured artists gets a flat $500 BTC honorarium directly from the 55% bucket — guaranteed regardless of funding level. Anything left over in the 55% bucket beyond honoraria seeds our Q3 nationwide micro-grant cycle. Production costs come from the 30% Programs bucket. So at our target funding, your sponsor dollar funds **21 featured artists plus roughly 25 additional micro-grants** for artists nationwide. Board minutes and financials are public."

### "Is this tax-deductible?"

> "Yes — BFTA is a registered 501(c)(3). EIN is on the leave-behind. We provide proper donation receipts. Have your finance team check the deductibility against your local rules; it's straightforward."

### "Can we just give in-kind?"

> "Absolutely — and we love in-kind. NYC venue contribution, A/V production, hardware wallets for artists, catering at the finale, photography. We credit in-kind dollar-for-dollar at the corresponding tier. There's a full menu in the leave-behind."

### "We can't commit at the conference, we'd need a board approval."

> "Totally understood. Best path then is a 30-min call with our founder this week so you have everything you need to take to your board. Can I get you connected?"

### "What's your relationship to [BTC company X]?"

> "We're an independent 501(c)(3) — no commercial ties to any single Bitcoin company. We use BTCPay Server self-custodial for all donations, paid out in BTC to our artists. We work with the whole ecosystem, not for any one company."

### "Why Pubkey and not [other venue]?"

> "Pubkey is Bitcoin-native and offers mission-aligned rates. We're working with **Pubkey DC** as the primary venue, with **Pubkey NYC** or another NYC venue as the backup. Open to NYC venue contributions from sponsors as in-kind — actually one of our highest-value asks."

### "I'm not a Bitcoiner / our company isn't crypto. Why would we sponsor?"

> "Honest answer: maybe you wouldn't. But if you're an arts brand, a financial services brand reaching forward-thinking high-net-worth audiences, or a company that wants to be on the right side of the next decade of arts patronage — this is the founding event of that movement. Worth a 30-min call to find out."
