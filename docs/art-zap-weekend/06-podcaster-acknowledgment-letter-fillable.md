# Podcaster Acknowledgment Letter — Fillable

> **Send to donating podcasters within 30 days of the event.** This is an *acknowledgment*, not a tax receipt — donated services are not tax-deductible per IRS Pub 526. For full rationale, IRS-compliance notes, and a worked example, see `05-podcaster-acknowledgment-letter-template.md`.
>
> ⚠️ **Have your CPA approve the language below before sending the first one.**

Print this on Bitcoin For The Arts letterhead. Replace every `[bracketed]` field. Sign in ink. Scan and email a PDF to the podcaster, plus mail a hard copy if they have a US address.

---

[BFTA letterhead with logo, address, EIN, website]


[Date]


[Podcaster Name]
[Podcast / Show Name]
[Address line 1]
[Address line 2]
[City, State, ZIP]


Dear [Podcaster First Name],

On behalf of **Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.**, a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization (EIN: **[EIN]**), I want to formally acknowledge with deep gratitude your contribution of professional podcast hosting services to **Art + Zap Weekend**, our flagship Bitcoin-native arts fundraiser broadcast on **[date(s) of broadcast]**.

Specifically, you contributed:

- Approximately **[X]** hours of on-air hosting time on **[broadcast date]**
- Pre-event promotion across your podcast, social media, and Nostr channels
- A **[30-minute]** technical preparation call

The fair market value of equivalent professional hosting services, based on your standard rate of **$[hourly or project rate]**, is approximately **$[total dollar amount]**.

Per IRS guidelines (Publication 526), the value of contributed services is **not tax-deductible** to the donor. However, we want to formally recognize and document your generous in-kind contribution to our 501(c)(3) mission of funding artists exclusively in Bitcoin.

Any **out-of-pocket expenses** you incurred in connection with this contribution — including travel, mileage, lodging, or supplies — **may be tax-deductible**. Please consult your tax advisor. We are happy to issue a separate, itemized expense receipt for documented expenses; please send copies to **donate@bitcoinforthearts.org**.

Thank you for helping make Art + Zap Weekend possible, and for putting your platform behind sovereign artists. Bitcoin For The Arts wouldn't exist without partners like you.

Sincerely,



______________________________
**Dion Wilson**
Founder & Director
Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.
donate@bitcoinforthearts.org
bitcoinforthearts.org
