## Why this email is structured this way

- **The pitch deck link is the first thing in the body**, before any logistics — the deck is the most important deliverable, so it leads. Logistics serve the deck, not the other way around.
- **Reply-with-list** for hotels/handles/panels — gives them a clear, scannable thing to do in 60 seconds rather than an open-ended "let me know your plans"
- **One shared meal per day, not three** — reasonable ask that doesn't require them to clear their entire calendar; they likely have other meet-ups already
- **Sunday dinner as the kickoff** — this is the most important meal of the trip. Building rapport before the chaos starts means Day 1 hallway work goes 3x better.
- **Working lunch Monday, drinks Tuesday** — energy matches what's actually possible at a conference. Working lunch is utilitarian; closing drinks is celebratory.
- **Three "don'ts" + one "do"** at the end — keeps them from over-functioning. Most volunteers at a conference try to close, accept, or memorize too much. Constraining what they're responsible for is how you actually empower them to do their part well.
