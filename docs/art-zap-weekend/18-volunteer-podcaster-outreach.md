# Volunteer Outreach — Podcaster Host Invitations

**For BFTA volunteers reaching out to Bitcoin podcasters about hosting Art + Zap Weekend.**

This is the volunteer-facing podcaster outreach template. The podcaster strategy and structure are documented in `03-podcaster-host-plan.md` — read that first if you haven't.

---

## Read this first — what's different about volunteer podcaster outreach

You are reaching out **on behalf of Bitcoin For The Arts** as a volunteer. Specifically:

1. **Your job is to surface interest, not to book.** A good outreach ends in either "yes, please loop me in with Dion" or "no thanks." Either is a win.
2. **You can't promise specific slot times.** Slot scheduling happens after Dion confirms the podcaster has bandwidth.
3. **You CAN promise the published model:** 1–2 hour slots, light prep, on-air promotion of their show, FMV acknowledgment letter.
4. **Always cc or bcc Dion** on initial outreach so he can pick up the thread when a podcaster says yes.

---

## What you CAN tell podcasters

- Bitcoin For The Arts is a 501(c)(3) (EIN 41-2642260) that funds artists nationwide through quarterly Bitcoin micro-grants
- Art + Zap Weekend is BFTA's flagship Bitcoin-funded fundraiser
- Targeting **a weekend in late August through early October 2026** (specific weekend confirming with venue)
- 4 podcaster hosts per broadcast day (Sat + Sun), each committing **1–2 hours** on-air
- **25-minute "drop-in" slots** are available for big-name podcasters who can't spare a full block — they co-host a single artist segment, plug their show, leave
- One 30-minute prep call the week before
- One mention on the podcaster's own show + one social/Nostr post before the event
- BFTA broadcasts on YouTube + X + Nostr (Zap.Stream) + Fountain
- The podcaster's own LN address gets shown on-screen during their hosting block — direct zaps from the audience to their podcast wallet are 100% theirs
- BFTA issues a written acknowledgment letter for the podcaster's records documenting the FMV of contributed services (NOT a tax receipt — services aren't deductible per IRS rules — but useful for their own marketing and PR)

## What you CANNOT promise

- ❌ A specific date / specific slot time (Dion finalizes)
- ❌ Audience numbers or exposure guarantees
- ❌ Honoraria for podcasters (the model is donated time, like featured artists)
- ❌ Sponsor partnerships with the podcaster's show beyond what's in the published runner
- ❌ Custom segment content beyond what's standard (hosting one or more artist segments)

If a podcaster asks about anything in this list, the honest answer is: **"Let me check with Dion and follow up — I'd rather get you the right answer than guess."**

## What to do when a podcaster says YES

1. **Reply within 24 hours** confirming receipt and that you're looping in Dion
2. **Email Dion** with the podcaster's name, show, slot preference (if stated), and any specific questions
3. **Add the podcaster to `tabs/podcaster-hosts.csv`** with status `Verbal yes`
4. **Let Dion send the confirmation email** — he handles slot assignment, the prep call, and the ongoing relationship

The handoff to Dion should look like:

> Hey Dion — [Podcaster Name] from [Show Name] said yes to hosting Art + Zap Weekend. They want [slot preference: 1hr / 2hr / 25-min drop-in], on [Sat / Sun / either]. They asked about [any specific questions].
>

> Other handles: [Nostr / X]

## What to do when a podcaster says NO

1. **Reply graciously and ask for one referral**
2. **Update `tabs/podcaster-hosts.csv`** with status `Declined` and a 1-line note on why
3. **Move on quickly**

Sample "no" reply:

> Hey [Name] — completely understand. Thank you for considering it. Quick ask: is there a Bitcoin podcaster friend of yours who you think would be a great fit? A warm intro from you to one host who'd care about the BFTA mission would be a huge help. No pressure if not.
>

## Tracking outreach

Use `tabs/podcaster-hosts.csv` (already in this folder). Status values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not contacted | Identified but no outreach sent |
| Contacted | Outreach sent, awaiting reply |
| In conversation | Replied, scheduling a call or trading details |
| Verbal yes | Said yes, slot being decided — **handoff to Dion** |
| Confirmed | Slot locked by Dion, prep call scheduled |
| Backup | Said yes but as backup only |
| Declined | Pass — log and move on |

**Aim for 12+ outreaches** to land 8 confirmations + 2 backups. Lighter ask than artist outreach (1–2 hours vs full mission-supporter commitment), so conversion should be higher.

## When to escalate to Dion

Escalate immediately if a podcaster:

- Asks about honorarium or fee for hosting (the model is donated time, but they'll want this confirmed by Dion before committing)
- Asks for a custom slot time or format that's outside the standard 1–2 hr or 25-min options
- Has a co-host or production partner they want to bring (multi-person hosting needs Dion's call on slot logistics)
- Wants to do something custom with their LN address / zap routing during the broadcast
- Is a high-profile / mega-show host (let Dion handle the relationship personally — these matter too much for handoff)
- Asks about sponsorship integration with their own show (separate conversation Dion should lead)
- Pushes back on the no-honorarium model

Honest answer in any of these cases: **"Great question — let me loop in our founder Dion who can give you a definitive answer. I'll have him reply within 24 hours."** Then email Dion immediately.

## Tone notes

- **Lead with the show, not the ask.** A specific reference to their show in the first line is non-negotiable. Bitcoin podcasters get pitched all day; pattern-recognition kicks in fast.
- **Make the time commitment crystal clear and small.** "1–2 hours" is the headline number. The 25-minute drop-in option is the safety net for the biggest names.
- **Be honest that this is a fundraiser FOR BFTA.** Bitcoin podcasters care about the v4v ethos. Saying "fundraiser FOR our org" up front signals respect; obscuring it would feel like a bait-and-switch when they realize the on-screen QR isn't the artist's.
- **Their LN address on-screen is the killer feature.** Don't bury it. Bitcoin podcasters know what direct zaps to their wallet are worth, and "your podcast LN address shown on-screen during your hosting block" is a real, tangible benefit.
- **Don't oversell audience numbers.** This is a first-year event. Honest understatement ("first-year event, niche audience, high signal") converts better with sophisticated podcasters than inflated claims.
- **Lighter podcasters first, mega-shows last.** Don't waste your warmest ammunition on the biggest names — they're the hardest yes. Land 4–6 mid-tier confirmations first; then approach big names with momentum.
