# Artist Outreach — Templates and Scripts

How to invite the 21 broadcast artists (and the 4 Monday-finale performers) to participate in Art + Zap Weekend.

Goal: each artist commits to **one of four format options** that fits their work and their schedule. Lighter ask = higher yes rate.

---

## The four format options to offer every artist

Always lead with this menu. Let the artist pick what works for them — most will pick whichever feels lowest-effort.

| # | Format | Best for | Time commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Live performance** | Musicians, dancers, spoken-word, theater | ~25 min on-camera segment | 1 prep call (15 min) + 25 min live |
| **2. Live interview + showcase** | Visual artists, filmmakers, writers — anyone whose work is shown, not performed | ~25 min interview where you discuss the work as it's screen-shared | 1 prep call (15 min) + 25 min live |
| **3. Pre-recorded video / performance + live conversation** | Artists who can't be live (timezone / nerves / production quality) | They send a 5–15 min recording in advance; we play it on-air; they join live for a 10-min conversation after | 1 prep call (15 min) + ~10 min live |
| **4. Visual showcase only (no on-camera)** | Visual artists / poets / writers uncomfortable on camera | Their work is screen-shared with a host narrating, optional voiceover provided | 1 brief email exchange + 0 live time |

For the **Monday in-person finale at Pubkey**, separately offer:

| Format | Best for | Time commitment |
|---|---|---|
| **5. Live in-person performance at Pubkey** | NYC/DC artists able to perform at the venue Monday night | 1 in-person rehearsal + 15-min performance slot |

## Outreach Email — Template (Copy-Paste)

Send via email or DM. Personalize the first paragraph with something specific you've seen of their work.

---

**Subject:** Invitation: Art + Zap Weekend — [Artist Name], [date]

Hi [Artist First Name],

[1–2 sentences referencing specific work of theirs you admire. Example: "I caught your set at [venue] last spring — your piece [title] has been on my mind ever since." This is non-optional. Generic outreach gets ignored.]

I'm Dion Wilson, founder of **Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.**, the first 501(c)(3) nonprofit paying artists exclusively in Bitcoin. We're putting together our first global event — **Art + Zap Weekend** — and I want you to be one of our 21 featured artists.

**The event:**
- **Saturday + Sunday, [DATE]** — 2 days of live broadcast, 1–5 pm ET each day, on YouTube + X + Nostr (Zap.Stream) + Fountain
- **Monday evening, [DATE]** — in-person finale at **Pubkey [DC / NYC]** with a Broadway-headlined performance + silent art auction
- **21 featured artists** across music, visual art, dance, theater, film, writing, and storytelling
- Hosted by Bitcoin podcasters with combined audiences in the high tens of thousands
- Every viewer can send you Bitcoin **directly during your segment** via Lightning zaps — no platform fees, no waiting

**What we're asking from you (pick whichever fits your schedule):**

1. **A live performance** — ~25 minutes on-camera from your studio/home/wherever, with a host conversation
2. **A live interview + showcase** — we screen-share your work and you talk about it
3. **A pre-recorded performance + live conversation** — send us a 5–15 min video in advance, then join live for a 10-min chat after we play it
4. **A visual showcase only** — your work shown on-screen with a host narrating; you don't have to be on camera at all
5. **(Optional)** Perform live in person at the Monday finale at Pubkey

If this is a yes (or a "maybe — tell me more"), I'd love to jump on a 15-minute call this week.

Looking forward,

Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.

## Tracking artist outreach

Use `tabs/artists.csv` (already in this folder). Status values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not contacted | Identified but no outreach sent |
| Contacted | Outreach sent, awaiting reply |
| In conversation | Replied, scheduling a call or trading details |
| Verbal yes | Said yes, format being decided |
| Confirmed | Slot locked, format chosen, prep call scheduled |
| Tech check done | Pre-event tech check completed |
| Declined | Pass — log and move on |
| Backup | Said yes but as backup only |

Aim for **30 outreaches in the first 2 weeks** to land 21 confirmations + 5 backups. With 5 format options on the table, conversion should be high.

## Who to prioritize first

Outreach order should be:

1. **Existing BFTA artist network** — anyone you've already paid through micro-grants, anyone in your existing community
2. **Bitcoin-native artists** already producing work (e.g., NostrArt, Bitcoin musicians on Wavlake/Fountain, BTC visual artists) — they don't need orange-pilling
3. **Sovereign-aligned artists** in the broader arts community who don't need to be Bitcoiners but resonate with the freedom/independence theme
4. **Members of your AEA / AGMA / Broadway / Dance Theatre of Harlem / Ailey networks** — these are warm intros and they trust you
5. **Cold outreach to artists whose work fits the mission** — only after the warm tiers are tapped

For the **Broadway-caliber Monday finale headliner**, lead with your AEA + Broadway-dancer network — the warmest possible intro for the highest-stakes slot.

## Sensitive note: pay structure language

When an artist asks "what's the honorarium?" — answer honestly with the range. **Do not** promise a specific number until sponsors are confirmed. Suggested language:

> "Honoraria depend on what we raise from sponsors. Floor scenario is around $650 per artist; target is $1,200+; stretch could go higher. I'll confirm your specific honorarium amount **2 weeks before the event** at the latest. On top of the BFTA honorarium, **100% of audience zaps during your segment go directly to your LN address — BFTA takes nothing from those.** That's typically where the upside is."

This is honest, anchors expectations correctly, and keeps the long-tail story (zaps as the upside) front and center.
