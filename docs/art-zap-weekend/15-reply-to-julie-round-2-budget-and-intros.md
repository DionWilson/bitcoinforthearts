## Strategic context for future reference

This exchange is a model of how board feedback should work in a small org:

| Round | Julie's contribution | Dion's response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Don't underestimate the live music tech lift; split the role" | Adopted the structural change explicitly; asked for cost intel + names with concrete anchors |
| 2 | "$1K + travel; Steven Ishuzu; Trey at BTC DC; Greg's limits at Pubkey" | Locked $1K as non-negotiable budget line; accepted the Steven intro; surfaced the Trey-vs-Thomas timing question openly |

The pattern: **specific board input → specific decision in the same beat → specific next ask.** Each round of exchange moves the plan forward. This is what board engagement looks like when it's working.

Two things to NOT do in board comms — both of which would have killed this thread:

- ❌ "Let me think about it and get back to you" (kills momentum, requires Julie to remember to follow up)
- ❌ "Let's discuss at the next board meeting" (defers a decision that doesn't need a meeting — also signals her individual input wasn't trusted enough to act on)

The right response treats Julie as a collaborator with judgment, not a board member to be managed.
