## Slide 1 — The Problem We're Solving

- Federal arts funding is collapsing. The NEA has been targeted for elimination in two consecutive federal budgets. NEA mass-terminated grants in May 2025.
- Bitcoin and the Lightning Network now make instant, global, micro-value patronage possible for the first time in history.
- BFTA already operates in this space — but we lack a flagship, public-facing event that puts the mission in front of new audiences.
- **Art + Zap Weekend is that event.**

---

## Slide 2 — Why the Original Plan Was Re-Scoped

The 7-day plan had real concerns the board raised:

- **Single-producer burnout risk** across 21 hours of live broadcast
- **Audience attrition** — small/new brand cannot sustain 7 days of attention from a niche audience
- **Operational load** of coordinating 7 different podcaster hosts across 7 separate days
- **No clear culminating moment** — the event ended on a Wednesday with no in-person draw

The revised plan addresses each of these without losing what made the original strong.

---

## Slide 3 — The Revised Format

**Saturday + Sunday — 2 days of live broadcast** (1:00–5:00 pm ET)
- 4 hours per day, 8 hours total
- 21 artists across the two days (10 Saturday + 11 Sunday)
- **4 podcaster hosts per day**, each committing **1–2 hours** (8 podcasters total + 2 backups)
- Time window optimized for **Americas + Europe + Africa simultaneous reach**
- Broadcast simultaneously to YouTube + X + Nostr (Zap.Stream) + Fountain

**Monday evening — In-person finale at Pubkey**
- Broadway is dark on Mondays → **top-tier NYC performance talent uniquely available**
- ~7:00 pm doors → cocktail + sponsor reception
- ~8:30 pm live performances by 3 supporting artists from the broadcast
- ~9:30 pm **Broadway-caliber headliner**
- ~10:00 pm silent art auction with **Title Sponsor's Named Lot** as featured piece
- ~10:30 pm closing
- **Pubkey DC** as primary venue (mission-aligned rates); **Pubkey NYC / NYC alternative** as backup

---

## Slide 4 — Why This Format Wins

| Concern | How the revision addresses it |
|---|---|
| Producer burnout | 8 broadcast hours over 2 days vs 21 hours over 7 — and **producer staffing scales with sponsor funding** (solo at floor, hired professional at target, full team at stretch) |
| Audience attrition | Weekend = best global availability. "This weekend" is a clean marketing message vs. "tune in every day for a week" |
| Host coordination | 4 podcasters × 1–2 hrs is a much lighter ask than 7 podcasters × full days. Higher conversion rate from podcaster outreach. **25-min "drop-in" slots** available for the biggest-name podcasters who can't spare a full block. |
| No culminating moment | Monday finale at Pubkey with a Broadway headliner is the biggest possible exclamation point. Sunday broadcast hypes "tomorrow night at Pubkey" → drives RSVPs |
| Single-shot risk | If Saturday has a tech issue, Sunday is a recovery window. If broadcast underperforms, the in-person finale still delivers visibility. |

## Slide 5 — Sponsorship Structure

**Three tiers, 9 slots maximum, $80K stretch / $45K realistic target.**

| Tier | Cash | Slots |
|---|---|---|
| **Title Sponsor** | $25,000 | 1 |
| **Supporting Sponsor** | $10,000 | 3 |
| **Friend of the Arts** | $5,000 | 5 |

Plus **in-kind sponsorships** credited at the corresponding tier — NYC venue contribution, A/V, photo/video, catering, hardware wallets for artist gifts, silent auction items.

### Why the $5K–$25K range works

- $25K Title is the level a Bitcoin-native company's CMO can sign off on without a board meeting (after-pilot we can scale to $50K)
- $10K Supporting matches NYC arts gala table rates and Bitcoin Park sponsorship slots
- $5K Friend captures small Bitcoin businesses (wallets, podcasts, hardware vendors) that wouldn't bite at $10K
- Below $5K, sponsor-management cost exceeds revenue — push smaller donors to Sovereign Circle memberships

## Slide 6 — Funding the Event Without Breaking the 55/30/10/5 Model

**This was a board concern. Here's the resolution.**

The 30% **Programs bucket** in our published policy is *defined* as workshops, residencies, co-productions, and **live productions**. **Art + Zap Weekend IS a program.** Event hard costs flow naturally through the 30% bucket. The 55% to direct artist grants includes the 21 featured-artist honoraria as the first tranche.

**Sponsor-facing language:**

> "55% of every sponsor dollar goes to direct artist grants — 21 artists this event get their share. 30% goes to programs, including the production of this event. 10% to operations. 5% to the HODL Vault endowment."

**No policy change required. No suspension of transparency.** Restricted gifts (Option B) and a board resolution (Option C) are available as fallbacks but should not be needed if in-kind asks land — see `02-sponsor-tiers-and-budget.md` for full rationale.

### Artist honorarium policy (NEW — board direction)

- **Flat $500 BTC honorarium** to each of the 21 broadcast artists, guaranteed at every funding scenario
- Sized intentionally low + uniform: aligned with BFTA's standard $500–$2,000 micro-grant range; uniform = no negotiation, no parity disputes; **$500 is meaningful but doesn't crowd out the broader micro-grant pipeline**
- Excess in the 55% Direct Artist Grants bucket flows to **Q3 nationwide micro-grants** for non-event artists
- Broadway-caliber Monday finale headliner gets a separate $2,500 honorarium **paid from the 30% Programs bucket** (it's a venue-specific finale draw, not a broadcast slot)

### Three budget scenarios

| Scenario | Sponsor revenue | 55% Artist Grants | 21 honoraria ($500 ea) | Excess → Q3 micro-grants | 30% Programs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | $25,000 | $13,750 | $10,500 | $3,250 (~5 micro-grants) | $7,500 (solo producer; needs full in-kind) |
| **Target** | $45,000 | $24,750 | $10,500 | $14,250 (~25 micro-grants) | $13,500 (hired pro producer + Broadway) |
| **Stretch** | $80,000 | $44,000 | $10,500 | $33,500 (full Q3 cycle funded) | $24,000 (full team + premium) |

### Audience zap / donation policy (NEW — board direction)

- **On-screen donation QR is BFTA's Lightning address for the entire 8 hours of broadcast.** All zaps default to BFTA's mission.
- Each artist's personal Lightning address is shown briefly in a secondary lower-third labeled "Tip [Artist] Directly" during their ~25-min segment as an optional tip jar
- Direct tips to the artist's personal LN address are 100% theirs (not counted against BFTA revenue)
- This policy is **honest with both audiences:** donors see one default ("support BFTA") with an optional path ("tip the artist"); artists know upfront they get a guaranteed $500 + tips; sponsors know this is a real fundraiser FOR BFTA

## Slide 7 — In-Kind Asks (Where the Real Leverage Is)

In-kind sponsorship is the difference between "great event" and "great event that *also* funds 21 artist grants." Top asks:

| Need | Approx. value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **NYC venue** for Monday finale | $5,000–$10,000 | One of the strongest in-kind opportunities — frees up cash for artist honoraria |
| Pubkey DC venue partnership | $5,000 | Mission-aligned rates; primary path |
| Beverage / catering at finale | $2,000–$5,000 | Friend-tier credit |
| A/V at finale | $2,000–$4,000 | Friend-tier credit |
| **Hardware wallets for 21 artists** as gifts | $1,000–$3,000 | Mission-aligned; ideal for hardware vendors |
| Photographer + videographer | $2,000–$4,000 | Multi-month content yield |
| Travel for out-of-area artists incl. Broadway headliner | $1,500–$5,000 | Critical for finale draw |

> **What we're explicitly NOT asking for in-kind: BTCPay Server hosting or Lightning routing.** BFTA already runs its own BTCPay infrastructure on `donate.bitcoinforthearts.org` with full custody of donations. This is a strength to highlight to sponsors, not a gap to fill.

## Slide 8 — Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsorship under-fills | Medium | Three-tier structure means even partial fills (1 Title + 1 Supporting = $35K) hits floor + most of target |
| Broadway headliner falls through | Low–Medium | Backup performers from the broadcast lineup; Director's AEA/AGMA network provides depth |
| Tech failure during broadcast | Medium | StreamYard auto-records; backup producer at target funding; Sunday is a recovery window if Saturday breaks |
| Pubkey DC unavailable for Monday | Medium | Pubkey NYC and 3 NYC backup venues identified |
| Podcaster cancels day-of | Medium | 2 backup podcasters on standby + 25-min drop-in slot can cover gaps |
| Donor flow underperforms | Low | Three concurrent paths: cash sponsorship, audience zaps, post-event grant matching |
