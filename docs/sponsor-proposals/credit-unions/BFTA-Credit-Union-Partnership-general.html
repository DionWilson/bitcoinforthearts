The opportunity

Members are asking about bitcoin.
Who will they learn it from?

More members hold bitcoin or are curious about it every year. When their credit union isn't part of that conversation, they learn from exchanges, apps, and social media, and the relationship moves with them. A credit union that teaches sound money plainly and responsibly becomes the trusted place members turn to.

01 · Who we serve

Independent working artists

Freelancers, small-business owners, and creative entrepreneurs with irregular income, chronically underserved by traditional arts funding and by retail banking.

02 · What they need

Real financial education

How money works, how to manage income that comes in bursts, how to protect savings, and how new payment rails like bitcoin and Lightning work.

03 · Why a credit union

Shared values

Member-owned, community-first, and built to serve people the big institutions overlook. That is the same community Bitcoin For The Arts was built to serve.

Why now

Bridging credit unions into the
sound money ecosystem

Credit unions don't need to become bitcoin companies to lead here. They need members who understand what bitcoin is, how to stay safe, and how it fits beside their savings, credit, and retirement planning. That understanding is what Bitcoin For The Arts teaches.

The NCUA has confirmed that federally insured credit unions may work with third-party providers of digital asset services, within applicable law and with sound risk management (Letter to Credit Unions 21-CU-16). Education is the first, lowest-risk step.

01

Education first. Members learn the fundamentals from a 501(c)(3), with no products sold and no investment advice.

02

Trust stays home. The credit union is the name on the classroom, not an exchange.

03

A younger, entrepreneurial audience. Creative freelancers are the members most credit unions want and rarely reach.

04

Ready when you are. If the credit union later explores digital asset services, its members already have the foundation.

Credit union expertise on our board. Kyle Shirkness spent 19+ years in credit unions, most recently as Director of Operational Excellence and Innovation at Citadel Credit Union, and presented "Bitcoin: The Credit Union Infrastructure Upgrade" at EDGE 2026.

Built for compliance

How we protect your brand

We know a credit union's compliance and risk teams will review any bitcoin partnership. These terms are built into every tier.

  • Education, not advice. Our classes are financial education. We give no investment advice and sell no products.
  • No product endorsements. We recognize sponsors by name and logo. We don't promote rates, products, or comparisons.
  • Your review. Co-branded materials and co-hosted class content are shared with your team in advance.
  • Artist privacy. We never sell or share artist or member data. Any member offer is opt-in by the artist.
  • Full transparency. Public board minutes, governance policies, and financial records, plus a written impact report for each sponsor.
  • Charitable gift. Sponsorships are gifts to a 501(c)(3) and are receipted with the value of any benefits stated.
