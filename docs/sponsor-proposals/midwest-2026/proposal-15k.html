A B O U T   B I T C O I N   F O R   T H E   A R T S

Bitcoin for the Arts, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit empowering artists with Bitcoin micro-grants, education, and sovereign funding tools. We are the first nonprofit paying artists exclusively in Bitcoin. Our work spans podcast journalism, original publishing, micro-grant deployment, an Artist Directory, and on-the-ground cultural programming at major Bitcoin conferences.

Our public footprint is small but compounding. We are a startup nonprofit operating with limited resources, building the patronage model that the arts and the Bitcoin movement both need.

T H E   O P P O R T U N I T Y

The Midwest Bitcoin Summit returns to the Greater Columbus Convention Center on September 23 and 24, 2026. Bitcoin for the Arts has secured a dedicated 30'-wide footprint on the Expo Hall floor and access to the Secondary Stage for lunch-hour performances on both days. We are calling this footprint Bitcoin Arts Park.

Inside the BFTA footprint we will run a curated film cinema (silent-disco headphones, no audio bleed), a Living Room brand and demo zone with a Lightning wallet activation, a Gallery wall with peer-to-peer Lightning silent auction, and a merchandise and donation counter. On the Secondary Stage, two days of programmed live performances by Ainsley Costello and other working artists on a Bitcoin standard. The whole footprint is BFTA-programmed end to end.

“Culture is not a luxury. It is essential civic infrastructure. And the rail it runs on matters more than the headlines admit.” Bitcoin for the Arts · Issue #14 · June 2026

This $15,000 sponsorship covers two linked components: a live Dirty Coin presentation by Alana Mediavilla in Columbus, and the operational backbone of BFTA's Bitcoin Arts Park footprint. Together, they put a proven Bitcoin documentary in front of a heartland audience and give an artist-first 501(c)(3) the resources to build a cultural room that lives up to the mission.
