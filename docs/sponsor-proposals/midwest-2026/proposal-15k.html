W H O   W E   A R E

Bitcoin for the Arts is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit empowering artists with Bitcoin micro-grants, education, and sovereign funding tools. We are the first nonprofit paying artists exclusively in Bitcoin.

Our work spans original podcast journalism, weekly editorial publishing, an 8-part educational webinar series for artists, micro-grant deployment, a public Artist Directory, and on-the-ground cultural programming at major Bitcoin conferences. We are a startup nonprofit operating with limited resources, building the patronage model that the arts and the Bitcoin movement both need.

T H E   O P P O R T U N I T Y

The Midwest Bitcoin Summit returns to the Greater Columbus Convention Center on September 23 and 24, 2026. BFTA has secured a dedicated 30'-wide footprint on the Expo Hall floor and access to the Secondary Stage for lunch-hour performances on both days. We call this footprint Bitcoin Arts Park.

Inside the footprint: a curated film cinema, a Living Room brand and Lightning wallet demo zone, a Gallery wall with peer-to-peer Lightning silent auction, and a merchandise and donation counter. On the Secondary Stage, two days of programmed live performances by Ainsley Costello and other working artists on a Bitcoin standard. And, anchoring the cinema, a live presentation and Q&A of Dirty Coin, the most-screened Bitcoin documentary in the world, by director Alana Mediavilla.
