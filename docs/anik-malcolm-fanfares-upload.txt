DESCRIPTION (paste into Fanfares Description)

Classically trained in London, disillusioned with the arts establishment in Reykjavik, and now living with his wife and children in the mountains of central Italy, Anik Malcolm is producing some of the most rigorous fine art anywhere in the Bitcoin space today.

His practice carries a two-word tagline: Depicting Bitcoin. His most consequential work is a monumental oil painting titled The Whole Entire Universe, which debuted at Bitcoin 2026 in Las Vegas after more than 900 hours of labor. Twenty-one million beads. A cube of 276 by 276 by 276. Each face yields exactly 4,096 squares. The halving pattern appears in the image itself, and it only works cleanly at 21 million.

In this episode, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Anik to go back to the beginning: classical training, the gallery-world moment that broke the spell, the move to Italy, how Bitcoin became not just a subject but the subject of his entire practice, collecting and exhibiting outside the museum circuit in places like Lugano and Roatan, fatherhood inside a sound-money household, and what a renaissance in serious craft looks like when it happens outside MoMA.

This Fanfares edition uses the YouTube version audio with intro clips and the closing animation walkthrough. The RSS audio version does not have the intro.

IN THIS EPISODE:
Knowing he would spend his life making things since childhood. Classical fine art training in London and pattern recognition. Disillusionment in London and Reykjavik. The gallery meeting that felt like fiat across the table. Moving to central Italy. How Bitcoin clicked after selling a Reykjavik flat. Folding an identity into Depicting Bitcoin. The Whole Entire Universe math and Las Vegas debut. Patronage geography: Lugano, Las Vegas, planned Roatan sculpture. Artistic household and kids. Next five to ten years.
