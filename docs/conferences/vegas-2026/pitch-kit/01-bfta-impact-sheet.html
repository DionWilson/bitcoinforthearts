Our Mission

Bitcoin For The Arts funds independent creators across visual arts, theater, music, dance, writing, film, and storytelling — through quarterly Bitcoin micro-grants, education, and live programming. We are building a parallel arts-funding system that doesn't depend on government grants, corporate sponsors with strings, or institutional donors with conditions. Sound money for sovereign creators.

Why Bitcoin

Federal arts funding is collapsing. The NEA has been targeted for elimination in two consecutive federal budgets. State arts appropriations are projected to drop 7.7% nationally for FY2026. Institutional arts funding can no longer be relied on.

Bitcoin and the Lightning Network make a non-state-dependent arts funding model possible for the first time:

  • Instant, global, peer-to-peer payments with no platform fees, no permission, no third party between donor and artist
  • Fixed supply (21 million) — endowments denominated in Bitcoin do not lose purchasing power across decades the way fiat endowments do
  • On-chain transparency — every donation in, every grant out, verifiable by anyone, forever

We are not waiting for institutions to fix themselves. We are building the alternative.
