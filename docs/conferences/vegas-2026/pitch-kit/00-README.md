## Print specs

- **Quantity:** 50 copies
- **Paper:** Letter-size (8.5 × 11), color
- **Format:** Single-sided (the sheet is designed to fit on one side; if it overflows on your printer, switch to double-sided with the third-page content on the back)
- **Stock:** Heavier than standard copy paper if you have it (24 lb is fine; 32 lb feels more substantial). Matte finish is more readable than glossy under conference lighting.

## Honest framing baked in (read this before printing)

The Impact Sheet is **NOT** a "look at our funded grants" piece — BFTA hasn't delivered grants yet. It's framed as **"Impact + Trajectory"**: what BFTA has built (the org, the model, the artists already in the community, the upcoming flagship event) plus what's coming (Q3 micro-grant cycle, Art + Zap Weekend in September).

This framing is more honest AND more compelling than fabricating grant numbers. Sophisticated Bitcoin-conference attendees will respect "we are building this — here's the proof so far" more than "look at how big we already are."

## What it does at the booth / hallway conversation

- Hand to anyone who shows interest beyond a 30-second pitch
- Reinforces the verbal pitch with concrete proof points
- Includes the artist stories that make BFTA *feel* like a real community, not a one-person operation
- Has the QR code + sponsor email so the recipient can act on their own time
