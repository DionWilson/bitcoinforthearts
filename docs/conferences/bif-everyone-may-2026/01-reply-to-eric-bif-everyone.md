- **Lead with the yes, not the gratitude.** Eric made a real offer; the most respectful response is a fast confirmation, not a thank-you novel.
- **Answer his 3 questions directly and in his order.** Treating his structure as the structure of the reply signals you read the email carefully.
- **Surface the BFTA reciprocity items proactively.** Eric explicitly asked "anything else you can provide beyond promotion." Listing the newsletter feature, live coverage, post-event recap, and mutual booking interest treats him as a partner, not a vendor.
- **"BFTA pays directly" is non-negotiable** — protects Beth from any out-of-pocket exposure. Make it crystal clear in writing.
- **"Beth has my full backing on any operational decision" empowers Beth** to make calls in real time without having to chase Dion. Critical because Dion will be at the BFTA Vegas conference April 27–29 and may have other commitments before May 22.
- **Domain acknowledgment** closes a loop Eric raised. Demonstrates attention to detail.
- **Treat his "send the discount code when you have it" as a gentle prompt** — surfaces the deliverable without being pushy about it.
