## Why the email is structured this way

- **Beth's voice, not Dion's.** Continues her existing thread with Eric (4 emails in already). Switching to Dion's voice would feel jarring — Eric has been speaking with Beth since April 12.
- **Lead with the yes** — Eric made a real offer; the most respectful response is fast confirmation, not throat-clearing.
- **"BFTA pays the $300 directly" up front, before any other ask.** This protects Beth from any out-of-pocket exposure and gives Eric the clarity he needs to invoice correctly. Surface the financial mechanic immediately so it doesn't get buried.
- **Answer Eric's 3 questions in his order.** Treating his structure as the structure of the reply signals he was read carefully.
- **Beth's authority delineation at the bottom** — "operational decisions = me, sponsorship structure = Dion" — gives Eric clear escalation paths and prevents Beth from being put on the spot for anything contractual.
- **Reciprocity items are concrete, not vague.** Newsletter feature, live coverage, transparency report mention, mutual booking interest — each is a specific deliverable Eric can hold us to.
