## The 30-second BFTA pitch (memorize this)

When someone walks up and asks "what is this?":

> "I'm Beth Fletcher, an artist. This booth is for **Bitcoin For The Arts** — we're the first 501(c)(3) nonprofit paying artists exclusively in Bitcoin, through quarterly micro-grants we fund nationwide. Our flagship event is **Art + Zap Weekend** in September — two days of live broadcast, 21 featured artists, in-person finale at Pubkey DC. We're looking for sponsors, hosts, and audiences who care about a non-state-dependent arts funding model. **What's pulling you to this booth — the art, the Bitcoin angle, or both?**"

That last question is the magic move — it lets the conversation go where the visitor's actual interest is.

## Then branch the conversation based on what they say:

### Branch 1: "I'm an artist / I love the art"
- Show them your work first
- Mention the BFTA grant program and your own story as a featured artist
- Invite them to apply for a grant: `bitcoinforthearts.org/grants`
- Capture: name + email + their work, hand them a printed BFTA general one-pager

### Branch 2: "I work at [Bitcoin company / wallet / hardware]"
- This is a sponsor lead — light up
- Ask what they do, listen
- Pitch Art + Zap Weekend: "We're confirming sponsors for September — three tiers from $5K to $25K, plus in-kind for hardware vendors. Want me to connect you with our founder Dion this week?"
- Capture: name + role + company + best contact (email or Signal). Hand them the printed Art + Zap Weekend one-pager.

### Branch 3: "I'm a Bitcoiner who supports the arts"
- This is a donor lead — also light up
- Show them BFTA's transparent allocation (55% to artists, 5% to HODL Vault, public on-chain)
- Point at the donation QR: "If you want to back the model, that's our Lightning address. If you want a deeper relationship, the Sovereign Circle membership starts at $5/month."
- Capture: name + email if they're interested in joining the Sovereign Circle

### Branch 4: "I just like the booth"
- Ask if they want to sign up for the BFTA newsletter — easy yes
- Tell them about Art + Zap Weekend in September
- Capture: email for newsletter

## Money handling at the booth

### For YOUR art sales and LN tips
- 100% yours
- Phone wallet keeps the sats; if any cash sales, you keep the cash
- Document each sale (piece + price + buyer if known) for your own records — useful for next year's tax

### For BFTA donations
- Lightning donations to BFTA's address go directly to the BFTA wallet — they don't pass through you
- If anyone wants to give cash specifically to BFTA, the cleanest move is to ask them to pay via Lightning instead (point at the QR). If they insist on cash — accept it, document amount + name, mail it in to BFTA after the event with a note.

### For the booth fee ($300)
- BFTA pays Eric directly. **You don't front this.** If Eric asks you about it at the door, "Dion is handling that on our side — invoice goes to sponsor@bitcoinforthearts.org" is the answer.

### For your travel
- BFTA covers up to **$500** in travel/lodging expenses. Save every receipt (hotel, flight, gas, tolls, parking). After the event, send the receipts to `donate@bitcoinforthearts.org` and BFTA reimburses you in BTC.
- If you spend more than $500 — that overage is your contribution; BFTA doesn't cover it. Plan accordingly. (Most options should land well under $500: e.g., one night at a moderate Portland hotel + flight or drive should fit.)

## What Beth is NOT responsible for

To keep your weekend manageable:

- ❌ **Closing sponsors on the spot** — your job is to surface interest and capture leads. Dion closes via follow-up calls.
- ❌ **Promising specific deliverables to sponsors** — anything beyond what's in the printed one-pager, escalate to Dion. Honest answer: "Great question — let me have Dion follow up with the specifics within 24 hours."
- ❌ **Discounting the sponsor tiers** — tiers are tiers. If someone tries to negotiate, "Dion handles all tier conversations directly — let me get you on his calendar."
- ❌ **Speaking to the post-event surprise gratitude payment** for Art + Zap artists — that's INTERNAL only, never disclosed.
- ❌ **Booking artists for Art + Zap Weekend on the spot** — refer interested artists to `bitcoinforthearts.org/grants` and the Art + Zap Weekend page; Dion or the volunteer artist coordinator follows up.
- ❌ **Speaking on behalf of BFTA's official policy** beyond what's in the printed materials. If you don't know, say so and refer to Dion. Honesty beats guessing.
