## The 30-second BFTA pitch (memorize this)

When someone walks up and asks "what is this?":

> "I'm Beth Fletcher, an artist. This booth is for **Bitcoin For The Arts** — we're the first 501(c)(3) nonprofit paying artists exclusively in Bitcoin, through quarterly micro-grants we fund nationwide. Our flagship event is **Art + Zap Weekend** in September — two days of live broadcast, 21 featured artists, in-person finale at Pubkey DC. We're looking for sponsors, hosts, and audiences who care about a non-state-dependent arts funding model. **What's pulling you to this booth — the art, the Bitcoin angle, or both?**"

That last question is the magic move — it lets the conversation go where the visitor's actual interest is.

## Then branch the conversation based on what they say:

### Branch 1: "I'm an artist / I love the art"
- Show them your work first
- Mention the BFTA grant program and your own story as a featured artist
- Invite them to apply for a grant: `bitcoinforthearts.org/grants`
- Capture: name + email + their work, hand them a printed BFTA general one-pager

### Branch 2: "I work at [Bitcoin company / wallet / hardware]"
- This is a sponsor lead — light up
- Ask what they do, listen
- Pitch Art + Zap Weekend: "We're confirming sponsors for September — three tiers from $5K to $25K, plus in-kind for hardware vendors. Want me to connect you with our founder Dion this week?"
- Capture: name + role + company + best contact (email or Signal). Hand them the printed Art + Zap Weekend one-pager.

### Branch 3: "I'm a Bitcoiner who supports the arts"
- This is a donor lead — also light up
- Show them BFTA's transparent allocation (55% to artists, 5% to HODL Vault, public on-chain)
- Point at the donation QR: "If you want to back the model, that's our Lightning address. If you want a deeper relationship, the Sovereign Circle membership starts at $5/month."
- Capture: name + email if they're interested in joining the Sovereign Circle

### Branch 4: "I just like the booth"
- Ask if they want to sign up for the BFTA newsletter — easy yes
- Tell them about Art + Zap Weekend in September
- Capture: email for newsletter
