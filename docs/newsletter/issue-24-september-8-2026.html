The Dispatch

CINEMA AT BITCOIN ARTS PARK.

Dear sovereign renaissance,

Two weeks to Midwest. This week we put names on the screen.

Bitcoin Arts Park will run a film cinema on the Expo floor: Bitcoin documentaries, sovereignty stories, and two trailers with live pitches from people connected to the work. Not a hotel-ballroom film fest. A working cultural booth where the audience can watch, ask, and tip.

Two people will pitch films in person at the booth: Nadia Vaeh and Jason R. Johnston. The rest of the slate screens as cinema. We are also featuring IndeeHub as a resource for filmmakers and creators who want value-for-value rails for their work.

   

◆ The cinema

The films.

Documentaries on the screen. Two pitches at the booth.

Dirty Coin

Directed by Alana Mediavilla (Campo Libre). The Bitcoin mining documentary filmed across four continents. From Texas to Malawi to Finland, it follows miners, energy systems, and communities, and it has become one of the most-screened Bitcoin films in the world.

Watch on IndeeHub →

Finding Home

From filmmaker and host Avi Burra. Two stories of our time collide: immigrants seeking a new home, and visionaries seeking a new money. A cinematic search for sovereignty and community, served one meal at a time. Episodic documentary.

Watch on IndeeHub →

Hummingbird: The Bitcoin Jungle Story

Directed by DirectorHodl, produced by paulinthejungle, presented via Simply Bitcoin. In Uvita, Costa Rica, an organic farmers market and Bitcoin Jungle sit inside a larger story of trust, colonialism, the IMF and World Bank, and the eagle-and-condor prophecy.

Watch on YouTube →

Bigger Than Bitcoin

Directed by Kyle Huber with My First Bitcoin. Episode One goes to Kenya: Nairobi, Kibera, Machakos, and the grassroots educators, merchants, youth, and women-led projects turning open-source Bitcoin education into local sovereignty.

Watch on YouTube →

Till My Last Breath  ·  Trailer + booth pitch

Writer, producer, and director Courtney Lee Simpson (Shine Your Light). Faith-based family drama inspired by a true story: a proof-of-concept short shot in three days with a nine-person crew, aiming toward the feature. Nominated at CONTENT2026 for directing and storytelling.

Courtney will not be in Columbus. Representing the film at Bitcoin Arts Park: Jason R. Johnston, CEO and Lead Creative of Fifty Oars Motion Pictures, and the film’s executive producer, cinematographer, editor, colorist, and sound designer (also nominated for Best Cinematography at CONTENT2026). Jason will pitch the project at our booth.

Watch the trailer →

One Poop Scoop Away  ·  Trailer + booth pitch

Nadia Vaeh, award-winning recording artist and filmmaker, brings a teaser, talk, and Q&A around the indie feature she stars in. Trailer landing this week. At Midwest she pitches the film in person and helps hold the Bitcoin Arts Park presence.

Watch for the trailer drop. Meet Nadia at the booth in Columbus.

◆ Share Your Bitcoin Journey

Episode 15. Ainsley Costello.

Value for value. Gen Z. Bitcoin music. Opening Bitcoin Arts Park.

Ainsley Costello · Share Your Bitcoin Journey Episode 15

Ainsley Costello is a Nashville pop-rock artist, band leader, and one of the clearest Gen Z voices in Bitcoin music. First artist on Wavlake to hit one million sats with Cherry On Top. Berklee Music Business, magna cum laude, at 19. Co-founding Epoch Music. Opening day at Bitcoin Arts Park on September 23.

In Episode 15 we get into Spotify math vs value for value, human-made music in the age of AI, Lightning rails for artists, and what showing up in Bitcoin rooms actually unlocks.
