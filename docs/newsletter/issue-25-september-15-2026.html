◆ Share Your Bitcoin Journey

Episode 16. Nadia Vaeh.

Music as a lifeline. Indie grind. One Poop Scoop Away. Midwest booth.

Nadia Vaeh · Share Your Bitcoin Journey Episode 16

Nadia Vaeh is an award-winning recording artist and filmmaker. Atlanta native. Owner of Galactic Cat Records. After more than a decade of independent touring, writing, and side hustles, she turned the camera on what it actually means to make it. The indie feature is One Poop Scoop Away (directed by Josh Bedsole).

In Episode 16 we talk music as a lifeline, streaming math versus patronage, songwriter rights, getting paid in Bitcoin, and why she is bringing a teaser, talk, and Q&A to Bitcoin Arts Park next week.

◆ Cinema partners

Liberty International.

Solution to Poverty · three shorts on the Bitcoin Arts Park screen.

Liberty International · Solution to Poverty series

Liberty International (International Society for Individual Liberty, Inc.) is a Dallas-based 501(c)(3) and one of the longest-running freedom advocacy networks in the world. Founded in the lineage that began in 1969, today it works across 80+ countries through education, leadership training, conferences, translations, and storytelling. Mission, in their words: finding and expanding freedom in an unfree world through education, leadership, and networking.

We have been in conversation with their team, including leadership and strategy. For Midwest they offered documentary options that fit our cinema narrative. We are screening the first selection: the Solution to Poverty series, three short episodes on why poverty persists, how licensing laws block wealth creation, and how wealthy countries keep poorer nations poor.

Sovereignty, incentives, and human dignity on screen. That sits cleanly next to Bitcoin Arts Park.
