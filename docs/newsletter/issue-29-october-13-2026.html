◆ Share Your Bitcoin Journey  ·  Episode 17

The Higher Low

Rami Korhonen has sung in the Finnish punk band Fumble since the early nineties. A few years ago, alone on business trips with a guitar he found in a rented apartment, he started writing songs about Bitcoin with one rule: the songs never say the word. He calls it acoustic pleb rock.

We talk about seeing the Ramones at sixteen, recording twelve songs in a single four-hour session, the campfire test every song has to pass, and the night Knut Svanholm jumped on the drums in Riga and changed a song for good.

One more thing: Rami has never spent the sats his music earns. "One day it's going to be enough to do another album." That is exactly the kind of support this release is built to test.

◆ Why we're releasing it this way

Showing artists the path.

Streaming pays almost nothing unless you are at the top of the pyramid. Rami says it plainly in the episode, and every working artist knows it. Value for value is the alternative: the audience pays the artist directly, in sats, with no label, platform, or bank in the middle.

It is still early, and most artists have never tried it. We think the fastest way to change that is to go first, with our own work, and publish what happens. We are partnering with the new tools of sovereignty, testing them on our own releases, and showing artists exactly how to use them to unlock their creative freedom.

◆ How it works

01 Fanfares sells the episode. You pay 21 sats, a tiny fraction of a dollar, and the episode unlocks. The audio is encrypted until you pay, and your sats go straight to Bitcoin For The Arts to fund artists. Any creator can sell audio this way.
02 Listeners who share get paid. Once you've bought the episode, Fanfares gives you your own referral link. When someone buys through it, you earn half of that sale.
03 AQSTR pays people to spread the word. We funded a campaign on AQSTR that pays sats on Nostr for reposting, quoting, replying to, and following our post. An AI check makes sure only genuine responses get paid. "gm" doesn't count. Something you actually heard in the episode does.
04 Next week, it goes free. The full episode lands on YouTube and our podcast feed. The exclusive week rewards the people who showed up first, and it doesn't lock anyone out.

The creator gets paid for the work. The listener gets paid for sharing it. Nobody in the middle takes the margin. That is the value-for-value loop, and this week we are running it end to end. We'll publish the numbers when the week is done.
