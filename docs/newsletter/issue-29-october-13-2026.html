◆ What we do

Bridging art and sound money.

Bitcoin for the Arts is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We do three things.

Grants. Micro-grants of $500 to $2,000, paid in Bitcoin, for the real cost of making the work. This month that meant a band on a conference stage, a hand-embroidered piece, and a feature film on its way to a theater.

Education. We teach artists how Bitcoin works, so they can get paid, save, and own their money. We also put freedom technology in their hands: the open protocols being built so artists can publish, get paid, and keep their audience with no gatekeeper in the middle.

Showcases. Our podcast, Share Your Bitcoin Journey, and Bitcoin Arts Park put artists and their work in front of people, so the work gets seen, collected, and paid for.

Where we are going. Partnerships with galleries, art institutions, and arts organizations on exhibits, live programs, and Bitcoin Art Nights. The goal is simple: pay every artist we feature. We are not there yet. Your support is how we get there.

   

◆ Featured projects

Two projects we want to fund next.

A micro-grant gets an artist into the room. These are what comes after: full budgets, line by line, for work that takes months instead of a weekend. Both artists stood on our stage in Columbus.

Painting  ·  One year, $100,000

Shipwreck Sean: One year of rooms

At Bitcoin Arts Park, Sean carried finished paintings into the room and then made another one over two days while people watched. The proposal is a year of that: eight Bitcoin exhibitions, four in the United States and four abroad. He arrives with work, paints on site, and sells peer to peer.

A weekend shows what he can do. A year takes a salary, materials, crates, freight, and travel. The budget is public.

Music  ·  One album, $40,000

Ainsley Costello: The next record

Our micro-grant put Ainsley and her band on the Expo Stage in Columbus. That was one weekend. The next record is ten songs with the same band, twelve days in a working Nashville room, a producer, a mix, and a master.

The first line in the budget is her time. That is the point.
