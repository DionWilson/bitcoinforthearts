Dear sovereign renaissance,

The first class is one week from today.

Monday, November 9, at 7:00 PM Eastern. The class is called What Is Money? It is live, about an hour, and there is no fee. We need you in the room, and we need you to bring someone with you.

There are four classes. Every one of them is a Monday at 7:00 PM Eastern. They are online, with time for questions. One session will also meet in person in New York. The address goes to everyone who signs up, as soon as the room is set.

Sound money is a new idea for most people. It means money that no one can print more of, so the value of your work holds over time. Bitcoin is that money. Only 21 million will ever exist. We say "sound money" because those words open the conversation. What we teach is Bitcoin.

◆ Fall 2026  ·  Mondays at 7:00 PM Eastern

Four classes. One hour each.

Arts schools teach the craft. Almost none of them teach why the dollar buys less every year, or how a creator gets paid directly. These four sessions do that, in order, in plain English. You do not have to be an artist to take a seat. You do not have to own any Bitcoin.

01 What Is Money?
Monday, November 9, 2026, at 7:00 PM Eastern. Why the dollar buys less when more of it is printed, what sound money means, and why Bitcoin was built so no one can make more of it.
02 Unlocking New Creative Freedom.
Monday, November 23, 2026, at 7:00 PM Eastern. How a fan or a collector pays an artist directly, with no platform taking the margin.
03 Bitcoin in Practice.
Monday, December 7, 2026, at 7:00 PM Eastern. A wallet, a first payment, a clear price for your work, and records you can keep next to a bank account.
04 Protecting Your Savings: Security and Self-Custody.
Monday, December 14, 2026, at 7:00 PM Eastern. How to hold what you earn, recovery words, and the habits that keep long-term savings safe.
