The Dispatch

Three grants. Then a classroom.

Dear sovereign renaissance,

This week we are awarding three artists. The grants are small on purpose. The work is not. Together they are $3,900: a band on a conference stage, a hand-embroidered piece, and a feature film that still has to reach a theater.

After the grants, a classroom. The Bitcoin lessons for artists are already on the site. We are opening them as live webinars. The signup is live.

   

◆ Music  ·  $2,500

Ainsley Costello

Nashville. Pop-rock. One of the clearest younger voices putting Bitcoin inside a working music career. She does not bolt sound money onto a set. She gets paid in it, and she says so in public.

The $2,500 went into the real cost of showing up at Bitcoin Arts Park: the band, food, lodging, and a full Expo Stage set, plus the culture panel. That is the use case this organization exists to fund. An artist, paid, in the room, with the work in front of people.

Her dossier →

   

◆ Textile  ·  $400

Aksana Zasinets

Warsaw. Also known as 5Ksana. She has practiced hand embroidery as Bitcoin physical art since 2017. The needle is the argument: sovereignty you can hold, made slowly enough that a machine cannot fake it.

Embroidery for Freedom is the project. The $400 funds the hours and the workspace for a dedicated piece for Bitcoin for the Arts, then a public record of the process for artists who will never stand in a conference hall.

Her dossier →

   

◆ Film  ·  $1,000

Christopher Arcella

Miami. The Bitcoin Executor is a narrative feature made outside Hollywood, funded by Bitcoiners, aimed at people who will never sit through a technical talk. The cut is in late post-production, about 110 minutes.

A finished cut is not a released film. The $1,000 supports the part audiences actually meet: a theatrical plan, marketing and posters, and ticketing that can settle in Bitcoin. We are awarding it now. Settlement follows the signed agreement. Bitcoin for the Arts will be credited in the end titles.

◆ Education

Live webinars for artists.

Grants pay for the work. Education is how the next artist learns to hold the payment. We are offering online webinars for artists, taught from the curriculum already published on the site. The decks stay open. The signup is for the live room.

The path, in order:

What Is Money? Bitcoin for Artists. Bitcoin in Practice for Artists. Protecting Your Bitcoin. Sound Money, Better Art. Then three sessions on the creator economy: the incentive, the audience, and the tools that let an artist get paid directly.

Sign up with your name and email. We will write when the next live session is set. You can read the presentations while you wait.
