The Dispatch

FROM THE HEART OF AMERICA.

Dear sovereign renaissance,

Columbus is behind us. The Midwest Bitcoin Summit is complete. And Bitcoin Arts Park, our first public appearance as a cultural footprint on an Expo floor, did what we hoped it would do: it gave people a room they will remember, and a clear look at why Bitcoin for the Arts exists.

The feedback has been generous. Attendees thanked us for what we brought to the conference. More than once, people said they are already looking forward to what we do next. That lands differently when you know what it took to get there.

We are a new organization with very little funding. The artists still came. They donated time, energy, and creativity. They made real sacrifices to be in the room because they believe culture belongs on a Bitcoin and sound-money standard, and that grants, resources, and education are how we keep that work alive.

Bringing our first public appearance to the heart of America was a remarkable beginning. This issue is a thank you, a credit list, and an invitation to help us keep going.

◆ Why it mattered

Culture is how sound money becomes a world.

We believe culture is what is needed to cultivate the world on a sound-money standard. At Midwest we showcased artists and their talent so people in attendance could feel that, not only hear about it.

We want to create the next renaissance in art. Midwest was a glimpse of what we can do when artists are visible, paid in Bitcoin where we can, and backed by a serious patronage stack: grants, education, resources, and rooms like Bitcoin Arts Park.

If you believe in culture and love artists, we need your support so we can fund this work and keep artists' time preference low enough to keep building on a Bitcoin standard.

◆ What's next

Q4: relationships. Then Nashville.

Fourth quarter we focus on building relationships and partnerships with other arts institutions: bringing sound-money education into those rooms, and offering our Bitcoin education webinars to artists and organizations ready to learn.

The main Bitcoin Conference returns to Nashville, July 15–17, 2027. We want to be there. We are looking at what is possible. We need support to plan it.

If you liked what we did at Midwest, imagine what we could do in Nashville with your help.
