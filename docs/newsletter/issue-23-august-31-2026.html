◆ Share Your Bitcoin Journey

Episode 14. MadMunky2140.

Wood, privacy, and the 2140 movement. Out now.

MadMunky2140 · Share Your Bitcoin Journey · Episode 14

MadMunky2140 is a woodworker, privacy advocate, and founding voice of the 2140 movement. He builds physical Bitcoin art from oak, epoxy, and hard wax oil, sells on a Bitcoin standard, and carries cypherpunk values into art, music, and culture.

In Episode 14 we talk craft and signal: why wood holds a message that lasts, privacy as artistic freedom, Nostr, peer-to-peer culture, and what it looks like to sell work without asking a platform for permission.
