◆ Share Your Bitcoin Journey

Episode 14. Mad Munky.

Wood, privacy, and the 2140 movement. Out now.

Mad Munky · Share Your Bitcoin Journey · Episode 14

Mad Munky is a woodworker, privacy advocate, and founding voice of the 2140 movement. He builds physical Bitcoin art from oak, epoxy, and hard wax oil, sells on a Bitcoin standard, and carries cypherpunk values into art, music, and culture.

In Episode 14 we talk craft and signal: why wood holds a message that lasts, privacy as artistic freedom, Nostr, peer-to-peer culture, and what it looks like to sell work without asking a platform for permission.

◆ How it works

Bid now. Pick up in Columbus.

Advance bids are open on every lot page. Place a binding bid in sats from home. Staff will write the high advance bid onto the paper sheet before doors open. Anyone in the room can still outbid you on the Expo floor.

On site: stand in front of the work, read the vinyl card, scan the QR for artist links and contact, and sign the sheet. Every lot shows an opening bid in sats with an “about $” guide for orientation, and the same 21,000-sat minimum increase. Bids are in sats. Highest valid bid at close wins.

Pickup is in person only at Bitcoin Arts Park (Greater Columbus Convention Center, September 23-24). No shipping. If you cannot be there to collect the work or book, please do not place an advance bid.

Passes: midwestbtc.com · Arts Park hub: bitcoinforthearts.org/midwest · Auction lots: bitcoinforthearts.org/midwest/auction

   

◆ For donors

Something for the auction table?

We have four silent-auction lots live so far: three visual artworks (CA Danner, Lady RedHorns, Shipwreck Sean) and a Timechain Mag Genesis Edition. If you are an organization, institution, collector, high-net-worth friend of Bitcoin, merchant, or anyone with something of real value to give, we would like to consider an in-kind donation for the Bitcoin Arts Park auction table at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit.

Think broadly across the Bitcoin ecosystem: original art or editions (displayed on an easel or at the table as space allows), conference or event tickets, hardware wallets, round-trip airfare or hotel nights for a Bitcoin conference, gift cards from Bitcoin-accepting merchants, tech products, classes, seminars, experiences, discounts, or other hospitality and travel offers. Art is welcome. So is almost anything else worth bidding on that advances culture, access, or sound money.

Table space and curation are limited. Not every offer will fit the program. Sending a proposal does not guarantee a lot. It starts a conversation about whether the gift belongs on the table.

Email a short description, estimated value, photos if relevant, and how the winner claims the gift to hello@bitcoinforthearts.org with the subject line Midwest auction table donation for consideration. We will reply as we review.
