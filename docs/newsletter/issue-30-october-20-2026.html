The Dispatch

Four classes on sound money.

Dear sovereign renaissance,

In November and December we are teaching our first four classes. Each one is about an hour. We will walk through the first series of our education courses on live webinars, and we will hold one session in person in New York. We are still finalizing the room for the in-person class, and we want a real headcount now so we book the right space.

Yes, we teach Bitcoin. We start one step earlier: what money is, why your purchasing power keeps shrinking, and how sound money and new payment rails bridge the gap between the traditional system and the digital age.

We built these classes for working artists, and they are open to anyone who wants a clear, plain-English start in sound-money economics. You do not have to be an artist to take a seat. Below: the four lessons, the class signup, a link to our 2027 education proposal, and the full video release of Episode 17 on YouTube.

   

◆ Fall 2026  ·  About 60 Minutes Each

From the old system to new rails.

Arts schools teach the craft. Almost none of them teach how the money works, why studio rent and tour costs outrun wages, or how a creator can be paid directly without a platform taking the margin. These four sessions cover the foundations in order.

01 What Is Money?
Monday, November 9, 2026. Why prices rise when the money supply expands, how sound money works, and why saving in a currency that holds its value changes how you plan a career or a household.
02 Unlocking New Creative Freedom.
Monday, November 23, 2026. How direct patronage and open payment rails connect traditional work to the digital age, with no gatekeeper between the person who makes the work and the person who values it.
03 Bitcoin in Practice.
Monday, December 7, 2026. Setting up a wallet, receiving a payment over Lightning, pricing work or services clearly, and keeping clean records alongside a bank account.
04 Protecting Your Savings: Security and Self-Custody.
Monday, December 14, 2026. How to hold what you earn without leaving it on an exchange, recovery words, and simple habits that keep long-term savings safe.

Format: Live online webinars, about one hour each, with time for questions. One session will also meet in person in New York; the address goes to everyone on the list as soon as the room is set.
