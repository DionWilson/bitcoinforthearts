◆ Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit

The lineup.

Two days at the Greater Columbus Convention Center. Two lunch-hour shows. Visual art on the walls, a live Bitcoin mining feed on the monitor, and partners on every side of the room. Here is who is confirmed on each day so far.

Wednesday, September 23

Ainsley Costello anchors the Wednesday lunch-hour show, our Nashville-based rock performer and one of the youngest artists building a career on a Bitcoin standard. Sharing the day with her: Short North Stage, Columbus's premier regional theater, performing an excerpt from their upcoming production of Stephen Sondheim's Sweeney Todd, brought to us by their professional cast. It is the first time Broadway-scale musical theater and Bitcoin have shared a stage in Columbus, and quite possibly anywhere.

Thursday, September 24

Andy Burghardt of Ann Arbor takes the Thursday lunch-hour slot with his one-man band show, a live-looping, foot-stomping performance you have to see to understand. Sharing the day: Short North Stage's high school internship program, with student singers and performers, plus our live children's book reading of Bitcoin: A Treasure to HODL by Lindey Magee for Columbus-area homeschool families and their kids.

Across both days

Shipwreck Shawn's original paintings hang in the Bitcoin Arts Park gallery for the duration of the summit, five pieces from his growing Bitcoin-adjacent body of work. And on the monitor at the center of the Park, Proof of Print's mining printer runs live for both days, streaming the mining feed in real time so every attendee can watch Bitcoin's security budget being minted, block by block, from the floor of the show.

More confirmations coming in the weeks ahead. Get your pass and follow the full lineup at midwestbtc.com.

◆ New on Artist Stories

No Good Kid.

Building culture around Bitcoin and Nostr  ·  Designer of Top 10 Bitcoin F*ck Ups, the Dark Forest zine, and Tamers of Entropy  ·  Gran Canaria

A new long-form artist story is live on the BFTA site. No Good Kid is a masked British designer, art director, and creative producer based in Gran Canaria. Fifteen years in branding and marketing, then a coaching client asked him to help with a manuscript about “something called Bitcoin” in 2020, and everything changed. Today he shapes the covers, packaging, and visual language of some of the most important books and zines in the Bitcoin and Nostr world: Top 10 Bitcoin F*ck Ups with Dušan Matuška and AmityAge, the Dark Forest zine with Peter Horváth and bitpunk.fm, and Tamers of Entropy with juraj & lisa.

His thesis, and the reason we asked him to be featured: “Today I’m most interested in building culture around Bitcoin and Nostr that puts people before technology.”
