Episode 20 is out. Our guest is Jered Tanner. He records as Eon Jred. He and Dion Wilson toured North America together in Disney's The Lion King, where Jered covered Timon, Zazu, and Pumbaa. Before the tour, seasons at the Utah Shakespeare Festival. After it, Merlin at Tournament of Kings in Las Vegas.

From the outside, that looks like making it. On the episode he talks about what the money looked like from inside: four-month contracts, years between jobs, and a good paycheck that still left no room to make anything new. He came to Bitcoin through GameStop and AMC, and through a coin he built so he could see how that world works.

The line to send to an artist who thinks the boat already left: "You did not miss the boat. We are early."
