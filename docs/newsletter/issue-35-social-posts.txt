Why is money essential for human action?

Picture life without it. Every person would have to grow their own food, build their own house, and sew their own clothes. That is incredibly hard and slow.

The solution is specialization. Money lets you focus on what you do best, whether that is painting, cooking, coding, or building, and trade for everything else. Ludwig von Mises called this economizing: using your limited time, energy, and resources in the smartest way you can.

It applies to artists too. Three months on a public art commission are three months you could have spent on another project. That is opportunity cost, and it is why knowing the value of your time matters.
