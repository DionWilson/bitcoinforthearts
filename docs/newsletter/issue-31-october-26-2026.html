◆ Grant report  ·  Embroidery for Freedom

Aksana is at the hoop.

Aksana Zasinets, also known as 5Ksana, works in Warsaw. Her grant is $400 for Embroidery for Freedom: one handmade piece for Bitcoin for the Arts, made slowly enough that a machine cannot fake it.

Her first report is in. The stands are on the table. A new piece is on the hoop. What remains of the grant buys thread and materials, then sends the finished embroidery to the United States.

◆ Share Your Bitcoin Journey  ·  Episode 18

The artist creates first.

Episode 18 is on YouTube. Fzero is a designer, an illustrator, and co-founder of Ninefold, the studio he runs with Ivy Lumi. Their line is stories that make you build.

One of those stories is $BLEEP, an animated series about an alien trying to understand people through Bitcoin. He says a story teaches what a lecture cannot, because you live through the character's eyes. And he is plain about the order of the work: the artist creates first. Everyone else is there to serve the artist.
