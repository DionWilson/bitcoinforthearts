(d) Alter, modify, degrade, distort, animate, recolor, retrace, redraw, or otherwise change the appearance of the Licensed Marks in any manner inconsistent with the Brand Guidelines;
(e) Combine the Licensed Marks with any other trademark, name, or design in a manner that could create a composite or hybrid mark, or that could suggest that the Licensed Marks are owned or co-owned by a party other than BFTA;

(g) Use the Licensed Marks in a manner that violates any applicable law;

## Section 10. Term and Termination

**10.1 Term.** This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues in effect for the Term set forth in Schedule A, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section 10.

**10.2 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice to the other Party if:

(a) The other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of the breach (except where the breach is not subject to cure under Section 4.3);
(b) The other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of an involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;
(c) The other Party ceases to conduct its business in the ordinary course; or
(d) In the case of BFTA as the terminating Party, BFTA reasonably determines that continued licensing of the Licensed Marks to the Licensee would materially harm BFTA's reputation, jeopardize BFTA's tax-exempt status under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or violate applicable law.

**10.4 Termination for Convenience.** Neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience except by mutual written agreement of both Parties.

**10.5 Immediate Suspension.** BFTA may, upon written notice and without terminating this Agreement, suspend the Licensee's rights under this Agreement pending investigation of an actual or suspected material breach of Section 4.1(c), (f), (g), or (h). A suspension shall not exceed thirty (30) days without either termination for cause under Section 10.2 or restoration of the Licensee's rights.

## Section 11. Effect of Termination

**11.1 Cessation of Use.** Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Licensee shall immediately cease all use of the Licensed Marks, subject only to the wind-down provisions in Section 11.2.

**11.2 Wind-Down.** The Licensee shall have a wind-down period of thirty (30) days from the effective date of termination, or such shorter period as BFTA may reasonably require in the case of a Section 4.3 termination, during which the Licensee may (i) exhaust existing physical inventory of already-produced materials bearing the Licensed Marks, (ii) allow existing digital communications and archival materials referring to the historical relationship to remain in place, and (iii) inform its own contacts and audience of the termination of the licensing relationship. No new materials bearing the Licensed Marks may be produced during the wind-down period.

**11.3 Return or Destruction of Materials.** Within sixty (60) days of the end of the wind-down period, the Licensee shall, at BFTA's election, either destroy or return to BFTA any remaining unused physical materials bearing the Licensed Marks, and shall remove the Licensed Marks from the Licensee's website, social media, and other digital properties, other than historical archival references made in a manner that does not suggest a continuing relationship.

**11.4 No Right to Reference Former Relationship as Ongoing.** After termination, the Licensee shall not refer to itself as a current licensee, chapter, ambassador, partner, sponsor, or grantee of BFTA, or otherwise suggest an ongoing relationship. The Licensee may make truthful historical references to the prior relationship (for example, "former ambassador of Bitcoin for the Arts, 2026-2027"), provided such references are consistent with this Agreement.

**11.5 Survival.** Sections 6 (Ownership and Reserved Rights), 7.1 and 7.2 (notice of infringement and cooperation in enforcement), 8.4 (Disclaimer), 9 (Indemnification), 11 (Effect of Termination), and 12 (Miscellaneous) survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## Operator Notes (Internal, Not Part of the Executed Agreement)

*The following operator notes are for BFTA's internal use when preparing an Agreement for execution with a specific Licensee. Delete this section before delivering the Agreement to the Licensee.*

**When to use this template.** Use this template to grant a specific counterparty the right to use BFTA's brand assets. This template is designed to work under BFTA's current common-law trademark rights and to accommodate future federal registration without a redraft.

**When not to use this template.** This template governs brand use only. It does not govern:

- The formation of a BFTA chapter (use the Charter Policy instead, and reference this Agreement as a companion).
- The terms of a sponsorship agreement (use the sponsorship agreement instead, and either reference this Agreement or incorporate a short Brand Rider by reference).
- The terms of an artist grant (the grant agreement should contain its own brief brand-acknowledgment language rather than a full Brand License).
- The terms of an ambassador engagement or consulting relationship (use a separate consulting or ambassador agreement).

**Preparation checklist.**

1. Confirm the Licensee's legal name and entity form.
2. Identify the specific Licensed Marks to include in Schedule A.
3. Define the Permitted Uses specifically. Be concrete. "May use the Bitcoin For The Arts word mark and main lockup on the Licensee's website landing page, in the Licensee's monthly email newsletter, and on printed program materials for the [specific event or program]" is better than "may use the Marks on all Licensee materials."
4. Define the Territory. If the Licensee operates in a specific geography, state it. If worldwide, state that explicitly.
5. Define the Term. Fixed-term agreements are cleaner than perpetual licenses. Recommend 1 to 3 years for most Licensees, with a written renewal at the end of the Term.
6. Specify the BFTA approval contact. This is the person the Licensee will email when they want to run new brand uses by BFTA under Section 5.2.
7. Attach the current Brand Guidelines PDF as an exhibit or provide the URL.
8. Route the executed Agreement to BFTA's Corporate Secretary for filing in BFTA's permanent records under Section 14 of the Gift Acceptance Policy (governance recordkeeping) or equivalent.

**When a federal trademark registration issues.** When BFTA obtains federal registration for a mark that is already included in Schedule A of an executed Agreement, the Executive Director may issue a written notice to the Licensee amending Schedule A to add the USPTO registration number. Under Section 12.2 of the Agreement, this amendment does not require the Licensee's countersignature. Save the notice in the same file as the executed Agreement.

**Template version control.** The current version of this template is v1.0. Amendments to the template require a two-thirds vote of the Board (Section 12.2). Template revisions apply to future executions and do not modify Agreements already in effect at the time of the revision.
