=== DESCRIPTION ===

In this deeply personal episode, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Lindey Magee — Mississippi mother, homeschool advocate, and accidental children's book author — for a conversation about faith, family, and the Bitcoin decision that saved her family.

Lindey's son Bennett tried to orange-pill her and her late husband Chuck back in 2012 — at age 12. They didn't listen. Twelve years later, after 25 years of marriage, Chuck passed away from cancer. Sitting with a life insurance check and no traditional income, Lindey felt a conviction she couldn't shake: put it into Bitcoin.

She studied Bitcoin like it was her full-time paid job, made the decision, and today she and her 17-year-old daughter Palyn live on a Bitcoin standard — Strike Bill Pay for bills, the Coinbase debit card for daily spending, the rest in cold storage.

That same Bitcoin funded her children's book — "Bitcoin: A Treasure to HODL" — a Seuss-style rhyming introduction to sound money that debuted at Bitcoin 2025 in Las Vegas and is now nationally distributed through Ingram.
