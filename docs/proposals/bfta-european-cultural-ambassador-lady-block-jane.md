## The one-paragraph pitch

Bitcoin For The Arts is building the first nonprofit paying working artists exclusively in Bitcoin. Our audience, our storytelling, and our grant pipeline sit almost entirely inside the United States today. The most important Bitcoin-native art community in the world, however, lives in Europe: Plan B Lugano, BTC Prague, Bitcoin Ireland, the Bitcoin Block Association in Switzerland, Locras Bräu, Satoshigallery, and the growing circular economies in Cape Town and Africa that Europe organizes and finances. **Lady Block Jane (Yena) is one of the most connected artists working across that entire scene.** We are inviting a Bitcoin-aligned sponsor to underwrite a three-month engagement that formalizes BFTA's European presence through her, ships a curated collection of European Bitcoin artworks into the United States for exhibition, and puts BFTA in the room at every meaningful cultural moment on the continent for the rest of 2026.

---

## Why now

Three windows are converging in the second half of 2026 that make this program timely rather than optional.

**1. The Midwest Bitcoin Summit, September 23 to 24, Columbus.** BFTA is producing Bitcoin Arts Park, our dedicated cultural footprint on the Expo Hall floor. Bringing a European artist and a shipment of European Bitcoin artworks physically into the room creates an intercontinental cultural bridge on our biggest event of the year, at zero incremental cost to the event budget.

**2. The V4V (Value 4 Value) conference in Chicago.** Immediately adjacent to the Midwest Summit calendar and directly relevant to the Nostr, Lightning, and creator-economy audience BFTA needs in front of. Yena travels through Chicago on her way to Columbus.

**3. Plan B Forum Lugano, BTC Prague post-event circuit, and the Bitcoin Ireland Conference (early 2027).** Yena is an exhibiting artist across all three. A three-month engagement lets BFTA plant a flag at each without BFTA staff attending in person.

Miss the window and we spend the next twelve months building this bridge from a standing start. Take the window and we ship the flywheel.

## About the ambassador

**Yena, professionally known as Lady Block Jane**, is a Swiss-German artist based (until recently) at Yena's Tattoo Lädeli in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, where she ran a Bitcoin-accepting professional tattoo studio for two decades. She now works between Switzerland and Morocco. Her Bitcoin-native art practice includes acrylic and spray on canvas, three-dimensional works, tattoo art, and graffiti. Signature pieces include *Unbroken Chain*, *Golden Whale*, *Bitcoin Bridge*, and *Dressed in Entropy*.

**Verified presence in the Bitcoin art community:**

- Featured hour-long guest on **Plebchain Radio Episode 137** (November 2025) with Avi Burra: *Mermaids, Entropy, and Synchronicity with Lady Block Jane*.
- Exhibiting artist at **Bitcoin Ireland Conference 2026**.
- Exhibiting artist at **BTC Prague** (curated by Fractal Encrypt / Bitcoin Art Magazine).
- Exhibiting artist at the **Bitcoin Block Association 2025 (Switzerland)**.
- Collaborator on the **Plan B Forum Lugano** recycled-surfboard project with Bitko Yinowsky, Streetcyber Art, Bitcoin Ekasi, and Fairdeeds.
- Featured artist for **Locras Bräu**, a Swiss Bitcoin-native brewery.
- Founder of the **A to B project**, a community art initiative born in a Swiss tunnel.
- Active on Nostr with a Lightning address (`ladyblockjane@walletofsatoshi.com`) and a real content history.

Yena discovered Bitcoin in 2017 through a premonitory dream while on tour with a band and did her serious deep dive in 2019. She is a Bitcoin-only artist, aligned with the BFTA mission on principle rather than convenience.

She comes to BFTA on the recommendation of Shadrach, a longtime supporter of our organization who has been part of the BFTA orbit since the earliest days.

## What the program delivers

Three months, three concrete deliverable blocks. Every dollar the underwriter contributes maps to a named output.

### Block 1: BFTA at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit (September 22 to 24)

- Yena travels from Switzerland to Chicago and then to Columbus.
- BFTA hosts her as a featured artist inside Bitcoin Arts Park on the Expo Hall floor for both show days.
- She brings a curated selection of her own Bitcoin-inspired original works to display in the BFTA Gallery wall.
- She activates the peer-to-peer Lightning silent auction alongside her work, contributing at least two pieces to the auction, with proceeds routed to BFTA's Q4 artist grants pool.
- She appears on the BFTA Secondary Stage schedule for one short "Artist in Conversation" moment during the two-day program.
- She sits for one live *Share Your Bitcoin Journey* on-site podcast taping.
- On the way in, she represents BFTA at the **V4V conference in Chicago**, working the room, distributing BFTA materials, and reporting back with a named list of the strongest warm leads.

### Block 2: European Artwork Shipment to the United States

- Yena coordinates a shipment of donated European Bitcoin artworks from several artists in her network (Bitko Yinowsky, Streetcyber Art, Bitcoin Ekasi, and others) to BFTA in the United States.
- The works arrive as tax-deductible in-kind donations to BFTA, with appraisals and 8283 forms handled through BFTA's Treasurer.
- The collection is displayed under a **BFTA plaque** with the underwriter's name clearly credited at three US Bitcoin-forward venues to be confirmed in month one: candidates include **Chicago Collective, Pubkey (New York City), and Denver Bitcoin Space**.
- Every piece is available for purchase, with a portion of proceeds routed to the donating artist and the balance to BFTA's artist grants pool. All transactions are on Lightning.
- The exhibition rotates or extends into the first quarter of 2027 depending on venue commitments.

### Block 3: European Cultural Bridge and BFTA Europe Coordination

- Yena delivers a **written memo naming five to ten European Bitcoin art contacts** BFTA should build relationships with, prioritized by fit and reach.
- She provides **three warm introductions** to the highest-priority contacts on that list, personally.
- She coordinates directly with the **BFTA Europe chapter lead** (Kyle Skirton) to make sure her European work supports and does not overlap with the chapter formation already in motion.
- She supports BFTA's social media presence with **weekly contributions to the BFTA Nostr and Instagram feeds**, sharing photographs of her studio practice, exhibition moments, and behind-the-scenes footage.
- She writes **one long-form piece for the BFTA Substack** on the state of Bitcoin art in Europe, in her own voice, timed to the close of the engagement.

### Program governance

- **Reports to:** Dion Wilson, Founder and Executive Director, weekly.
- **Coordinates with:** Kyle Skirton, BFTA Europe chapter lead.
- **Payment mechanism:** All contractor payments are made in **Bitcoin via Lightning** to Yena's address `ladyblockjane@walletofsatoshi.com`. This is mission-aligned and story-worthy in its own right.

## Why this is the right first sponsored program at BFTA

Under BFTA's Pay the Artist campaign framework, every dollar we raise ships back out as either a named artist grant or a named program that produces one. This proposal fits that rule cleanly:

- **The ambassador is paid in Bitcoin.** Yena becomes one of the first named recipients of BFTA-managed Bitcoin compensation from an underwritten program.
- **The exhibition supplies the Q4 grants pool.** Silent-auction and sales proceeds route directly into BFTA's grants budget for the next cycle. The underwriter's contribution effectively pays for both the ambassador and the seed of the next four to six micro-grants.
- **The story compounds.** Three months of European artwork visible at US Bitcoin venues, one Substack piece, one long-form podcast, three warm-intro records, and one European contact memo become the artifacts BFTA uses to raise the next $50,000.

This is a $10,000 spend that either lands a European bridge for BFTA and returns the flywheel two turns forward, or lands the same lessons for $10,000 and shows us how to structure the next one. Either way we ship it publicly, we tell the story honestly, and we build.
