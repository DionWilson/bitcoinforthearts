Bitcoin For The Arts is the first US 501(c)(3) paying working artists exclusively in Bitcoin. Our audience and grant pipeline sit almost entirely inside the United States today, but the most important Bitcoin-native art community in the world lives in Europe. Lady Block Jane (Yena) is one of the most connected artists working across that entire scene. We are inviting a Bitcoin-aligned underwriter to fund a three-month engagement that plants BFTA physically inside the European Bitcoin art community, ships a curated case of European artworks to the United States for exhibition, and returns the story that funds the next twelve months of BFTA grants.

Block 1 · Midwest Summit and V4V (September). Yena travels from Switzerland to Chicago (V4V conference) and on to Columbus for the Midwest Bitcoin Summit, Sept 23 and 24. Featured artist inside BFTA’s Bitcoin Arts Park, contributes work to the peer-to-peer Lightning silent auction with proceeds routed to BFTA’s Q4 grants pool, one live Share Your Bitcoin Journey podcast taping on-site, Secondary Stage program appearance.

Block 2 · European artwork shipment to US venues (October and November). A curated case of Bitcoin-inspired artworks donated by leading European artists (Bitko Yinowsky, Streetcyber Art, Bitcoin Ekasi, and more) shipped as tax-deductible in-kind donations to BFTA. Installed at three US Bitcoin venues (candidates: Chicago Collective, Pubkey NYC, Denver Bitcoin Space) under a BFTA plaque with the underwriter credited at every venue. Sale and Lightning-auction proceeds route to BFTA’s artist grants budget.

Block 3 · European cultural bridge (across three months). Written memo of five to ten European Bitcoin art contacts BFTA should build. Three warm introductions delivered personally. Weekly BFTA social contributions. One long-form Substack piece on the state of Bitcoin art in Europe.
