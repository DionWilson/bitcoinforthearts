Proposal
BitcoinDay Naples
January 16, 2027
$5,000

One day in Naples. Ainsley Costello live, and one work on the table.

Prepared for John Drew, BitcoinDay. This is the smaller of two ways to do the same day. Bitcoin For The Arts is the presenter. If it is accepted, there is one agreement with Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.

Half of this offer is the performance. The other half keeps CLCT on the show in a smaller role: Kyle Knight curates, one artist is paid to take part, and one work is shipped. There is no multi-piece install. Kyle will not be in Naples. Dion's travel and lodging are not in this budget.

What BitcoinDay gets

  • A live performance by Ainsley Costello.
  • One work, chosen by CLCT, with the artist paid to be included.
  • Dion Wilson as the host. He can set a single piece on a table. This offer does not build a display or ship a group of works.

The $10,000 proposal is the version with several artists, a shipped show, and a built table. The two offers are alternatives. They are not added together.
