The program

Something holders can see, hear, and pay

New technology reaches the mainstream through culture. Napster pulled millions online by making digital music cool. The iPod turned a music library into a statement of taste. The automobile spread because it became a statement of status and freedom. Bitcoin needs the same pull, and music and art are the fastest way to create it. Holders already understand the network. What they need are real places to spend bitcoin and real reasons to care about the people on the other end of it.

Giving at BitcoinDay

  • Give bitcoin, not cash. For bitcoin held more than a year, a donor can generally deduct fair market value and avoid capital gains tax on the gain, subject to income limits. Gifts over $5,000 need a qualified appraisal.
  • Two windows. Give by December 31 for the 2026 tax year, or at BitcoinDay for 2027.
  • Donor-advised funds can give where the fund allows it.

Why it matters

Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. is a New York 501(c)(3) and the first nonprofit paying artists exclusively in bitcoin, through micro-grants, education, and live programming. Every dollar follows a published rule: 55 percent to artist grants, 30 percent to programs, 10 percent to operations, 5 percent to a long-term bitcoin reserve.
