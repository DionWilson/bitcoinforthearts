BitcoinDay Naples
January 16, 2027
$10,000

One day in Naples. A live performance, and a curated table of original work.

Prepared for John Drew, BitcoinDay. Bitcoin For The Arts is the presenter. This is one proposal, and if it is accepted, one agreement with Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.

CLCT keeps its own name and is paid for four services: shipping the work, scheduling the artists, listing the auction on clctibles.com, and marketing the event. Kyle Knight advises Bitcoin For The Arts. He is not paid from this proposal, and he will not be in Naples. Ainsley Costello performs as an independent artist. Dion Wilson sets the table. His travel and lodging are not in this budget.

What BitcoinDay gets

  • A live performance by Ainsley Costello, the Nashville artist who opened Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit in September 2026.
  • A table of original work, shipped in by CLCT, with the auction listed on clctibles.com.
  • Marketing for the art program, and a host on the table. Dion Wilson sets it up and stays with it.

In September 2026, Bitcoin For The Arts presented Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit, and CLCT curated the gallery there. This is that partnership, scaled to a one-day room of about 200 people.
