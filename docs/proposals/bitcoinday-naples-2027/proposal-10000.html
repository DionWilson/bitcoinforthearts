Proposal
BitcoinDay Naples
January 16, 2027
$10,000

One day in Naples. A live performance, and a curated table of original work.

Prepared for John Drew, BitcoinDay. Bitcoin For The Arts is the presenter. This is one proposal, and if it is accepted, one agreement with Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.

CLCT, led by Kyle Knight, curates the visual art and keeps its own name. Ainsley Costello performs as an independent artist. Dion Wilson sets the table at the event. Kyle will not be in Naples. Dion's travel and lodging are not in this budget.

What BitcoinDay gets

  • A live performance by Ainsley Costello, the Nashville artist who opened Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit in September 2026.
  • A table of original work, selected by CLCT, in the room with the people attending BitcoinDay.
  • The artists paid to take part, and the work shipped to Naples and back.
  • A host on the table for the day. Dion Wilson sets it up and stays with it.

In September 2026, Bitcoin For The Arts presented Bitcoin Arts Park at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit, and CLCT curated the gallery there. This is that partnership, scaled to a one-day room of about 200 people.
