Why we are reaching out

Bitcoin for the Arts is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that supports artists with Bitcoin grants, education, and sovereign funding tools. This fall we launched a four-class sound money series, starting with What Is Money? It begins with the principles of money and barter, then leads into Bitcoin.

We believe this education reaches the most people when it is taught by many trusted voices, each in their own community. Our first facilitator, artist Monika Bravo, recorded What Is Money? in her own voice and opened it with her own introduction. That recording is now our model for every facilitator who teaches with us.

Your work shows why this matters. You already teach Bitcoin to health students, from lecture halls of 40 to 50 people to evening sessions. You see Bitcoin as a health promotion tool, and you are building a health systems innovation hub. Your article, The Invisible Health Tax, explains how our money affects our health. Artists and health workers need the same thing: a clear way to understand sound money, and to explain it to the people who trust them.

Five ways to work together

Now · November and December 2026Easy to start this month
01
Teach What Is Money? in your own voiceFollowing Monika Bravo's model, you record the class in your own voice, open it with your own introduction, and share it with your students and community. Bitcoin for the Arts lists you as a facilitator. The materials are licensed CC BY 4.0, so you can adapt them for a health audience with credit to Bitcoin for the Arts.
02
Join us on Share Your Bitcoin JourneyAn episode of our interview show on how you brought Bitcoin into public health. It introduces you to our audience before you teach them, and it gives your students something to share.
Winter · January to March 2027Build something together
03
A guest class: The Invisible Health TaxYou adapt your lecture for artists, and we host it as a session in our education series. Most artists have never heard Bitcoin explained as a health issue. Your perspective adds a layer no one else in our program can.
04
Art that helps health students explain BitcoinYour students find it hard to explain Bitcoin to patients and their communities, and art can be the starting point. We pair an artist from our network with your students to create visuals they can use in the clinic and the classroom. Bitcoin for the Arts would look to support the artist as a program project, with scope and budget set together.
Spring and summer · 2027Grow with your innovation hub
05
A sound money module in your summer programWhen your health systems innovation hub launches, What Is Money? becomes a session in the summer experience, and could count toward the certificate. Bitcoin for the Arts can also write a letter of support for your 2027 grant applications describing this collaboration.
