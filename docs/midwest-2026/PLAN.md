## Money — the actual numbers

**Goal:** $80,000+ public-facing target (set on `/midwest` and in Issue #11). Internal modeling against the 8-tier menu lands at ~$90K floor / $130K aggressive ceiling if all slots fill at the higher counts. $80K+ is the public number; the upside is private.

**How it allocates:**

| Line item | Estimated cost |
|---|---|
| 8–10 performing artists × $2,500 | $20,000 – $25,000 |
| 8–15 visual artists × $500 | $4,000 – $7,500 |
| Broadcast engineer (3–4 days, fee + travel + per diem) | $5,000 – $10,000 |
| Production support (OSU students, runner, camera ops) | $2,500 – $4,000 |
| Photographer + videographer | $5,000 – $7,500 |
| BFTA panel + Lightning Lounge staffing | $3,000 – $5,000 |
| Travel + lodging for BFTA staff (Dion, Beth if attending, etc.) | $3,000 – $5,000 |
| Marketing materials, signage, swag | $1,500 – $3,000 |
| Contingency (10%) | $7,000 – $8,000 |
| **Total** | **$51,000 – $75,000** |

The $70K–$80K target gives us a small surplus that flows back to BFTA's general fund — exactly what Kyle confirmed in the meeting.

**Tier mix to hit the target:**

- 1 Presenting Sponsor at $25,000 = $25K
- 1 Curatorial Sponsor at $7,500 = $7.5K
- 1 Programming Sponsor at $5,000 (covers broadcast engineer) = $5K
- 6 Sponsor-an-Artist commitments at $2,500 = $15K
- 5 Friend-of-BFTA at $1,000 = $5K
- **Subtotal: $57,500** — that's the floor

To stretch:
- 2 additional Sponsor-an-Artist at $2,500 = $5K
- 1 additional Curatorial Sponsor at $7,500 = $7.5K
- **Stretch total: $70K**

To exceed stretch (toward $80K):
- 1 additional Presenting Sponsor at $25K (yes, two presenting sponsors is acceptable for a co-presented event) = $25K
- **Total possible: $95K**

This is the math we present internally, not externally. Externally we say "$70K–$80K target" and let sponsors pick their tier without seeing the architecture.
