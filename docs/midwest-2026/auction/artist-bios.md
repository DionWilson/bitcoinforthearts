# Visual artists (gallery / silent auction)

## CA Danner

Since 2017, CA Danner’s work has been devoted to Bitcoin, Lightning, and Satoshi Nakamoto’s white paper. Mixed media on linen canvas: paper glued and scraped, string for network effects, layers of acrylic. Each piece is tied to a block height. MFA. Formalist paintings of peer-to-peer cash made physical.

- Site: https://www.cadanner.com
- X: @cityalley21
- Email: cadanner@protonmail.com
- Strike: cityalley@strike.me
- At Midwest: *Satoshi White Paper Series #52* (Block Height 770067) - silent auction LOT-01

## Lady RedHorns

Lady RedHorns is a contemporary artist exploring Bitcoin through expressive, gestural painting. Her Angels of Freedom and related series treat orange light, angels, and awakening as visual language for sound money. Red Horns Gallery.

- Bitcoin store: https://redhornsbtc.store
- Contemporary: http://redhornsart.store
- Linktree: https://linktr.ee/ladyredhorns
- X: @LRedhorns
