## 5. Delivery & risk

1. Artist will ship the Work to the Midwest Bitcoin Summit **advance warehouse**, using the official warehouse label provided by BFTA / show management, to arrive **September 20, 21, or 22, 2026** (or another window BFTA confirms in writing). Artist’s planned mid-September ship date should target that arrival window.
2. Artist will send tracking information to BFTA promptly after shipment.
3. Risk of loss remains with Artist until the Work is received at the advance warehouse; thereafter BFTA will exercise reasonable care while the Work is in BFTA’s custody on-site. BFTA is not an insurer of the Work.
4. If the Work sells, the buyer is responsible for packing, shipping, or hand-carry from the venue unless otherwise agreed in writing.
5. Post-Event handling of unsold Work follows the option selected in Section 4.
