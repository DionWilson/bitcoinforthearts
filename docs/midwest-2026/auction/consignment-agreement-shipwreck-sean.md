## 1. Purpose

Artist consigns the works listed below to Bitcoin for the Arts, Inc. (“BFTA”) for exhibition and sale facilitation at Bitcoin Arts Park during the Event, on the terms stated for each work. Unless a work’s section says otherwise, BFTA may display, photograph, and market the works at the Event and online for Event purposes, with credit to **Shipwreck Sean**.

Artist represents that Artist is the sole owner of each Work, that each Work is free of liens, and that Artist has full authority to consign, sell, auction, or raffle each Work on the terms below.

## 3. Fixed-price sales (Works #1, #2, #4)

Applies to *The Volatility Blues*, *A Slice of History*, and *Cold Storage*.

1. BFTA will offer each Work for sale at Bitcoin Arts Park at the listed price (USD and/or BTC as stated). Informational USD/BTC pairings are as provided by Artist; the accepted tender and Event-day conversion practice are posted by BFTA on-site.
2. Of the cleared sale price, after any payment-processor fees actually incurred on that payment: **100% to Artist**.
3. BFTA will remit Artist’s sale proceeds within **thirty (30) days** of cleared payment, to the payout instructions above (or as later updated in writing).
4. If a Work does not sell by the end of the Event, Artist reclaims it. Artist is responsible for return shipping / pickup cost and timing after the Event, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.
5. Buyer is responsible for packing, shipping, or hand-carry from the venue unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 5. Live painting raffle (Work #5)

1. Artist will paint Work #5 **live** at Bitcoin Arts Park during the Event (Bitsby to appear; remaining content open to audience ideas, as promoted by BFTA).
2. The finished Work is the **raffle prize**. It is not sold by fixed price or silent auction. The winner is announced by **3:00 PM Eastern Time on Thursday, September 24, 2026** (or as BFTA announces on-site).
3. **Raffle proceeds** means the net funds actually collected from raffle entries for this prize (after any payment-processor fees actually incurred), not the “sale price” of the painting.
4. Artist initials **one** split of raffle proceeds:

| Initials | Option |
| --- | --- |
| _____ | **A. Artist keeps 100%.** 100% of net raffle proceeds to Artist. |
| _____ | **B. Half / half.** 50% to BFTA · 50% to Artist. |
| _____ | **C. One-third / two-thirds.** 1/3 to BFTA · 2/3 to Artist. |
| _____ | **D. Full donation.** 100% of net raffle proceeds to Bitcoin for the Arts, Inc. |

5. If Option A, B, or C includes an Artist share, BFTA will remit that share within **thirty (30) days** of cleared raffle funds, to the payout instructions above.
6. Title to the finished live painting transfers to the raffle winner upon award, subject to Event raffle rules posted by BFTA. Artist grants BFTA the right to award the Work as the raffle prize under this Agreement.
7. BFTA will administer the raffle consistent with its nonprofit practices and applicable Event / venue requirements. Entry mechanics (ticket price, how to enter) will be posted at the booth.
