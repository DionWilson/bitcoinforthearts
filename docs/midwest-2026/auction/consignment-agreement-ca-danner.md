## 1. The Work

Artist consigns the following original artwork (the “Work”) to Bitcoin for the Arts, Inc. (“BFTA”) for silent auction during the Event:

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Satoshi White Paper Series #52, Block Height 770067 |
| Artist | CA Danner |

| Medium | Mixed media on linen canvas |
| Dimensions | 22 × 28 inches |

Artist represents that Artist is the sole owner of the Work, that the Work is free of liens, and that Artist has full authority to consign and sell (or donate) the Work on the terms below.

---

## 2. Auction terms

1. The Work will be offered in a **peer-to-peer silent auction** at Bitcoin Arts Park during the Event.
2. **Opening bid:** **150,000 sats** (about **$125**).
3. **Minimum bid increment:** **21,000 sats**.
4. Bidding closes **Thursday, September 24, 2026 at 3:00 PM Eastern Time**, unless BFTA announces an earlier or later close on-site for operational reasons.
5. The highest valid bid at close is the **hammer price**. The winning bidder must complete payment before the Work is released.
6. Payment may be accepted in **USD and/or Bitcoin / Lightning**, at BFTA’s posted conversion practice for the Event day.

---

## 3. Proceeds split

Of the hammer price (winning bid), after any payment-processor fees actually incurred on that payment:

- **One-third (1/3)** to **Bitcoin for the Arts, Inc.** as a charitable contribution supporting its mission  
- **Two-thirds (2/3)** to **Artist**

BFTA will remit Artist’s share within **thirty (30) days** of cleared payment, to:

- Strike: `cityalley@strike.me`  
- and/or email payout instructions at: `cadanner@protonmail.com`  

(Artist may update payout instructions in writing before the Event.)

---

## 4. No sale

If the Work does not sell by close of auction (no winning bidder, or winning bidder defaults and no successive bidder completes purchase), **Artist donates the Work in full to Bitcoin for the Arts, Inc.** Title transfers to BFTA upon written confirmation of no sale. No further shipping cost is owed by Artist for that donation outcome, except as the parties otherwise agree in writing.

---

## 5. Delivery & risk

1. Artist will ship the Work to the Midwest Bitcoin Summit **advance warehouse**, using the official warehouse label provided by BFTA / show management, to arrive **September 20, 21, or 22, 2026**.
2. Artist will send tracking information to BFTA promptly after shipment.
3. Risk of loss remains with Artist until the Work is received at the advance warehouse; thereafter BFTA will exercise reasonable care while the Work is in BFTA’s custody on-site. BFTA is not an insurer of the Work.
4. If the Work sells, the buyer is responsible for packing, shipping, or hand-carry from the venue unless otherwise agreed in writing.
5. If the Work is donated to BFTA under Section 4, BFTA is responsible for post-Event transport of the Work from Columbus.

---

## 6. Marketing & credit

Artist grants BFTA a non-exclusive license to photograph and reproduce images of the Work for Event signage, website lot pages, newsletter, social, and archival documentation, with credit to **CA Danner**. Artist may likewise reference the auction and BFTA collaboration.

---

## 7. Relationship

This Agreement is a consignment for auction (and contingent donation). It does not create employment, partnership, or an obligation for BFTA to guarantee a sale. Artist is responsible for Artist’s own tax treatment of auction proceeds or charitable donation outcomes; BFTA may issue customary acknowledgments consistent with its 501(c)(3) practices where applicable.

---

## 8. Entire agreement

This document, together with the public lot page at  
`https://www.bitcoinforthearts.org/midwest/auction/satoshi-white-paper-52`,  
is the entire agreement for this Work. Changes must be in writing (email sufficient) and acknowledged by both parties.
