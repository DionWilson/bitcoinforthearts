## Bottom line

As a 501(c)(3), BFTA **can legally conduct a raffle in Ohio**. Under current law (**ORC 2915.092**), eligible charities **do not need a state raffle/bingo license**.

We still must follow **Ohio Revised Code Chapter 2915**, keep required records, and check **local (Columbus / Franklin County)** requirements. **Fully online raffles remain restricted** under current AG interpretation.

---

## What this means for our Summit raffles

| Rule | Practical takeaway |
| --- | --- |
| State license | **Not required** for a 501(c)(3) raffle under ORC 2915.092 |
| Who operates | **BFTA** must be the clear operator — not a for-profit running it “on behalf of” BFTA |
| How winner is chosen | **Physical draw** from a receptacle containing stubs (or detachable sections) for **every ticket sold**. Pure RNG / fully digital selection is **not** authorized |
| Ticket sales | Prefer **in-person** at the event (or limited pre-sale that still produces physical stubs) |
| Online raffles | High risk / restricted today. HB 476 (online authorization) was not settled law as of mid-2026 research |
| Proceeds (501(c)(3)) | Net profit may be retained for charitable purposes |
| Records (ORC 2915.10) | Keep **≥ 3 years**: itemized gross receipts; itemized expenses with receipts; prize list; name/address/SSN for anyone winning **$600+**; how net profit was used |
| Federal tax | W-2G / withholding rules when prizes hit IRS thresholds |
| Local | Confirm Columbus charitable solicitations / event permitting; confirm BFTA’s Ohio **Chapter 1716** charitable solicitation registration is current |

---

## Safest structure for Midwest 2026

1. Sell tickets **in person** at Bitcoin Arts Park (physical stubs).
2. Draw the winner **on site** from a receptacle holding stubs for all tickets sold.
3. Keep BFTA as operator; document proceeds and expenses.
4. Separate the **silent auction** (peer-to-peer bids / consignment) from any **raffle** (chance drawing) in messaging, money handling, and records.
5. Do **not** sell or draw fully online until written AG/local confirmation says otherwise.
