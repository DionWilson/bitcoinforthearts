# Ohio Chapter 1716 note — NY org

**Clarification:** “Chapter 1716” is Ohio’s Charitable Organizations Act (solicitation registration statute). It is not a local chapter of BFTA. BFTA is a **New York** 501(c)(3) and does **not** currently maintain a separate Ohio 1716 registration file.

---

## How we are treating this after the AG raffle reply (Aug 2026)

Dion’s Aug 19 email to Charitable Law specifically asked whether a 501(c)(3) needs **any form of registration, permit, or notice with the Attorney General’s office** before conducting this in-person raffle.

Charitable Law replied that eligible orgs need **no raffle/bingo license**, must follow Chapter 2915 / ORC 2915.10, and:

> “The summary of your event is well researched, and I do not see anything of concern if you follow the plan you have laid out.”

They did **not** direct BFTA to complete Chapter 1716 registration, file a notice, or pause until solicitation registration was active.

**Operational reading (board / ED):** For this Midwest Summit raffle plan as written, BFTA is relying on that written AG reply. If 1716 registration had been required for the activity described, we would have expected Charitable Law to say so in response to Question 1.

This is still **not legal advice**. It is BFTA’s reasonable reading of the written guidance we requested and received.
