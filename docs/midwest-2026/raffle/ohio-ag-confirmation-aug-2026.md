# Ohio AG Charitable Law confirmation — Midwest Summit raffle

**Date of AG reply:** on or about August 20-21, 2026 (reply to Dion’s Aug 19 request)  
**From:** Charitable Law, Office of Ohio Attorney General  
**To:** Dion Wilson, Bitcoin for the Arts, Inc.  
**Contact noted:** 614-466-3181 · Charitable_Law@OhioAGO.gov · [Charitable Gaming](https://charitable.ohioago.gov/Charitable-Gaming)

**Status:** Written confirmation received. Proceed with the **in-person + physical-draw** plan as described. This memo is an internal ops record, not legal advice.

---

## What they confirmed

1. **BFTA is an eligible raffle operator** as a 501(c)(3) under ORC 2915.092 (eligible categories listed: 501(c)(3), (4), (6), (7), (8), (10), (19)).
2. **No state raffle/bingo license** is required for eligible organizations.
3. Raffles must still comply with **Chapter 2915**, including **ORC 2915.10 recordkeeping**.
4. On BFTA’s proposed plan (primarily in-person ticket sales, physical drawing from stubs for all tickets sold, 100% net proceeds to charitable purposes, 3-year records):  
   > “The summary of your event is well researched, and I do not see anything of concern if you follow the plan you have laid out.”

---

## What they also flagged

- **Alcohol prizes:** If raffling beer, wine, liquor, or other alcohol, ORC 4301.58 applies (purchase from Ohio liquor permit holders; receipts; for spirituous liquor, file with Ohio Department of Commerce Liquor Control). **BFTA’s current artwork raffle plan does not involve alcohol.** Keep it that way unless you deliberately adopt those procedures.
- They pointed to the Charitable Gaming page for ongoing resources.

---

## What was not newly authorized

- They did **not** write a separate green light for **fully online** ticket sales or a remote digital draw.
- Stay on the plan you described: **in person (or stubs that still feed a physical on-site draw)** and a **physical receptacle draw**.
- If you later want meaningful online pre-sale, send a **follow-up email** with that specific structure before selling those tickets.
