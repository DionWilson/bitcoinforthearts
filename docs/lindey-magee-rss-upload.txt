=== DESCRIPTION ===

Mississippi mother Lindey Magee never planned to become a Bitcoin author. In August 2024, her husband Chuck lost his battle with cancer after twenty-five years of marriage, leaving her a widow with two children and no traditional income. Twelve years earlier, her then-twelve-year-old son Bennett had tried to teach her about Bitcoin. In grief, that conversation came back.

In this hour-long episode of Share Your Bitcoin Journey, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Lindey Magee, author of the Seuss-style rhyming children's book Bitcoin: A Treasury to HODL, which debuted at Bitcoin 2025 in Las Vegas and is now in the Ingram catalog for nationwide bookstore, library, and school distribution.

Lindey walks through her journey from grieving widow to Bitcoin advocate. She talks about the Holy Spirit conviction that led her to put Chuck's life insurance into Bitcoin, the New Year's Eve she sat down at her kitchen table and wrote most of the book by three in the morning, the offset print run of ten thousand copies she committed to on her own, and the thirty thousand dollars she has invested in the book while raising her teenage daughter on a Bitcoin standard, currently from inside a twenty-six foot Winnebago after mold destroyed their home.
