=== DESCRIPTION ===

Fzero is a designer, illustrator, node runner, and co-founder of Ninefold, the studio he runs with Ivy Lumi. Their line is "stories that make you build." He started by copying manga as a kid. He studied economics, finance, and marketing, then opened stores and ran the floor for big fashion brands. The work looked like a career. It did not feel like one. On December 25, still under 30 and home with his family, he had a stroke. He already knew Bitcoin. He had been stacking sats for years. The scare settled the question. Time is the currency you cannot count, and he wanted to leave something that helps people after he is gone.

In this episode, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson talks with Fzero about the moment Bitcoin stopped being an idea and became a node in the house, about getting banned from X and staying on Nostr, and about pricing work once the payment is peer to peer. His GENESIS 113 pins are 21,000 sats on Take My Sats. He describes the first drawing that sent people to the Bitcoin white paper, the day a friend wore his alien as a tattoo at Nostriga, and GENESIS 113, the painting that treats Bitcoin as frequency: a tower of light rising out of noise. He made the painting. Timechain Art Magazine is featuring it in Edition One. It hung at The Space in Denver. The pins, made with Modeotec, went to BTC Prague and then to Plebeian Market.

He also tells the story of $BLEEP, the retro sci-fi series about an extraterrestrial trying to understand humanity through Bitcoin. The character is him, with the awkwardness left in. Season 1 is wrapped. Season 2 arrives in 2027. And he is plain about rooms and galleries. The artist creates first. Everyone else is there to serve the artist. The profit comes from serving them well.

This episode ran first on Fanfares, where 21 sats unlocked it and the sats went to Fzero and Bitcoin For The Arts. It is now free here.
