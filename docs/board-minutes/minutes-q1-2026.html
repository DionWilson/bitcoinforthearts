4.Advisory board

The Chair introduced the advisory board and described its role: non-binding guidance on fundraising, grant applications, and partnerships. Advisors shared their perspectives, and the board noted the value of the group in expanding the organization's network and expertise.

5.Policies

The Chair confirmed that all board and advisory members received the following policies by email before the meeting: Program Policy, Donor Pipeline, Donation Policy, CSR Policy, Endowment Management Policy, and Code of Ethics Policy. No detailed review was conducted. The floor was opened for questions and amendments, and none were proposed.

The Granting Guidelines and donor perks were identified as needing further review. The Secretary will record any future feedback for the next meeting.

6.Fundraising strategy

The Chair presented the fundraising strategy and the donor pipeline for the next quarter:

  • A phased donor approach (easy, sought-after, and major), with at least three active tiers each quarter.
  • Awareness through X and Nostr posts, webinars, and events.
  • Grant applications after tax-exempt status, for example the NEA and the New York Community Trust.
  • Identifying emerging artists for initial funding whose work aligns with Bitcoin themes such as sovereignty and censorship resistance.
  • Monthly donor tiers at bitcoinforthearts.org/donate/monthly, and a New York showcase or fundraiser tied to a July 1 launch.
  • Partnerships with AEA and AGMA for webinars, and Bitcoin events for major-donor introductions.

The board reviewed metrics, including return targets and recurring revenue from tiers such as the $51 Sovereign Stacker. Suggestions included A/B testing pitches and using advisory networks for major-donor introductions. The board agreed to prioritize Bitcoin-native tactics (about 60 percent) while using Stripe for fiat donations.

Decision
Approve the fundraising strategy, with Q1 focused on recruiting lower-tier donors.
Result Approved by consensus

7.Multi-signature wallet and board wallets

The board agreed on the importance of a secure multi-signature setup for the endowment and operations. The Treasurer gave a brief overview of the process and its compliance with the Endowment Management Policy.
