2.Call to order

Dion Wilson called the meeting to order at 4:30 PM Eastern.

3.Welcome and introductions

Dion Wilson welcomed everyone and introduced Valerie Love as a new Advisor, and Kenneth Burris as Artist Advocate. Kenneth will help write and document artist stories and the organization's journey. All participants introduced themselves.

4.Q1 progress

  • Continued growth of the Share Your Bitcoin Journey podcast series and the artist database, with four to five interviews completed.
  • New artist stories and articles published on the website.
  • Launch of the newsletter on Beehiiv, and a Substack account for longer written work.

5.Strike account

The organization opened a Strike account to purchase bitcoin. The Treasurer and the Executive Director will continue to manage treasury operations.

6.Funding applications

Applications have been submitted to Block, OpenSats, Start Small, and the Human Rights Foundation. Updates will be reported as responses arrive.

7.Wallet setup and board activation

All directors were reminded to schedule a short session with Dion Wilson to activate their Bitcoin wallets.

8.Newsletter, Substack, and YouTube

The first newsletter has launched. The board discussed growing the audience through consistent writing on Substack and further development of the podcast on YouTube.

9.Bitcoin Conference

Dion Wilson will represent BFTA at the upcoming Bitcoin Conference. Advisors attending were asked to mention the organization in their conversations and to arrange informal meetings with Dion to introduce potential donors and make the ask.

10.Branding and logo

Ahmed Klink reported that his production company, Sunday Afternoon, is leading the new logo and branding project. The board reviewed initial concepts and gave feedback on direction.

11.Board transition

Dion Wilson stated that he will remain a director for now, and plans to step down as a director once a new Chair is appointed. He proposed that Phindi Mkhize-Wilson be elected to replace him as a director at that time.

12.Strategic focus for Q2

The board discussed refocusing on a stronger foundation. Priorities:

  • Increasing donations and supporters.
  • A/B testing for outreach and visibility.
  • Fundraiser event ideas, venues, and a timeline for a first event in July or August.
  • An 18-month plan for visibility at art events.

13.Open discussion and new business

  • Partnerships with local arts organizations and institutions were discussed as a key growth area.
  • Idealist.org for volunteer recruitment and Candid.org for nonprofit resources were noted.
  • All members were encouraged to share leads and ideas.

14.Adjournment

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 6:20 PM Eastern. Next meeting: Tuesday, July 7, 2026.

These minutes record the discussions and decisions of the meeting.
