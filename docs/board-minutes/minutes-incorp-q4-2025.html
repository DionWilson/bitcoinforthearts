Minutes of the Organizational Meeting of the Board of Trustees

DateSunday, November 16, 2025
Time4:00 PM to 4:25 PM Eastern
FormatVideo conference (Zoom). Unanimous written consent in lieu of meeting authorized.

1.Attendance and quorum

StatusName and role
PresentDion Wilson, Founder and Chair
Avinash Burra, Trustee
Cheryl McGinnis, Trustee

All three initial trustees were present. Quorum was established under Bylaws Article V, Section 5.04.

2.Call to order

The Chair called the meeting to order at 4:00 PM Eastern.

3.Adoption of bylaws

Resolution
RESOLVED, that the Bylaws of Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc., dated November 16, 2025, as presented, are adopted in full as the governing document of the Corporation.
Vote Unanimous, 3 to 0
Result Adopted

4.Certificate of incorporation

Resolution
RESOLVED, that the Certificate of Incorporation of Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc., as prepared and signed by Incorporator Dion Wilson, is ratified and approved for filing with the New York Department of State. FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Chair is authorized to submit the Certificate with the DOS-1511-f cover sheet and the $75 filing fee, to request expedited processing if needed, and to receive and file the certified copy on return.
Vote Unanimous, 3 to 0
Result Adopted

5.Election of officers

Resolution
RESOLVED, that the following officers are elected to serve until the next annual meeting: Chair, Dion Wilson. Treasurer, Avinash Burra. Secretary, Cheryl McGinnis.
Vote Unanimous, 3 to 0
Result Adopted

6.Banking and bitcoin operations

Resolution
RESOLVED, that the Treasurer is authorized to open a business checking account at Mercury in the name of the Corporation; to establish a BTCPay Server instance at btcpay.bitcoinforthearts.org; and to create a 3-of-5 multi-signature HODL Vault using Sparrow Wallet, with keys held by the Chair, the Treasurer, the Secretary, and two additional trustees. The public address will be published after the 501(c)(3) determination. Key-holder device details are kept in the corporate record.
Vote Unanimous, 3 to 0
Result Adopted

7.IRS and compliance

Resolution
RESOLVED, that the Chair is authorized to obtain an EIN from the IRS (Form SS-4), and to file Form 1023-EZ with the $275 fee, attaching the stamped Certificate of Incorporation and the adopted Bylaws, under NTEE code A25.
Vote Unanimous, 3 to 0
Result Adopted

8.Adjournment

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:25 PM Eastern.

Filed with: corporate records; IRS Form 1023; New York Attorney General CHAR410.
Next meeting: February 3, 2026 (annual).

These minutes accurately reflect the actions taken. Approved by unanimous written consent in lieu of meeting, under Bylaws Article V, Section 5.08.
