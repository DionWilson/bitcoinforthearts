=== DESCRIPTION ===

In this hour-long conversation, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Anik Malcolm, the classically trained multimedia artist who walked away from the London and Reykjavik art establishments and now lives with his wife and children in the mountains of central Italy, producing some of the most rigorous fine art anywhere in the Bitcoin space today.

Anik's practice carries a two-word tagline: Depicting Bitcoin. His most consequential work to date is a monumental oil painting titled The Whole Entire Universe, which debuted at the Bitcoin 2026 conference in Las Vegas. It took over 900 hours to complete. His work is being collected and exhibited in the emerging citadels of Bitcoin: Lugano Plan B in Switzerland, The Venetian in Las Vegas, the Bitcoin Museum and Gallery in Nashville, and a planned monumental public sculpture in Roatán.

This conversation goes back to the beginning: classical training in London, the specific Reykjavik gallery meeting that broke his faith in the fiat art system, the withdrawal to central Italy that unlocked a career surge for both him and his wife Una, and the mathematics of The Whole Entire Universe. How 21 million beads fit into a cube of 276. How each face gives up 4,096 squares, exactly 64 by 64. How the resulting halving pattern of 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2 turns out to be an accidental visual signature of Bitcoin itself, one that only works cleanly at 21 million and one billion, and at no round number in between.
