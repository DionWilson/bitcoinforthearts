=== DESCRIPTION ===

In this hour-long conversation, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Anik Malcolm, the classically trained multimedia artist who walked away from the London and Reykjavik art establishments and now lives with his wife and children in the mountains of central Italy.

His practice carries a two-word tagline: Depicting Bitcoin. His most consequential work is a monumental oil painting titled The Whole Entire Universe, which debuted at Bitcoin 2026 in Las Vegas and took over 900 hours to complete. His work is being collected and exhibited in the emerging citadels of Bitcoin: Lugano Plan B, The Venetian, the Bitcoin Museum and Gallery in Nashville, and a planned monumental public sculpture in Roatán.

The mathematics: 21 million beads fit into a cube of 276. Each face gives up 4,096 squares, exactly 64 by 64. The resulting halving pattern of 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2 turns out to be an accidental visual signature of Bitcoin itself, and it only works cleanly at 21 million.
