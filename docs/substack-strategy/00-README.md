# BFTA Substack Strategy — Growth, Engagement, and Cadence

Strategic plan for growing Bitcoin For The Arts' Substack as a sustained content channel for arts-funding journalism, BFTA mission storytelling, and Art + Zap Weekend audience building.

## The problem we're solving

BFTA's existing Substack has 2 published longform articles ("The Lights Are Going Out" + "Is War Good for Bitcoin?") that demonstrate the editorial voice works. The next step is **building a publishing rhythm** that:

1. **Newsjacks** the daily stream of arts-funding crisis stories so BFTA shows up where the conversation is happening
2. **Tells the BFTA mission story** in installments — what we're funding, who we're funding, why Bitcoin matters
3. **Drives Art + Zap Weekend awareness** in the months leading to the September event
4. **Grows the audience** from current subscriber count to something that can be referenced in sponsor pitches with a straight face

## The honest constraint

Dion is one person running a 501(c)(3) plus an event plus sponsor outreach plus board management. **Two longform articles per week, written solo, is unsustainable.** Most successful one-founder nonprofit Substacks settle at one longform per week. The way to hit 2/week sustainably is **a contributor system** — board members, advisors, featured artists, and external Bitcoin/arts writers each contribute 1–2 articles per month.

## What success looks like in 12 weeks

By the time Art + Zap Weekend happens in September:

- **Subscriber growth:** 3–5x current count (specific target depends on starting baseline — fill in once Dion checks Substack analytics)
- **Publishing consistency:** 12+ longform articles published, no missed Wednesdays
- **Contributor engagement:** at least 4 contributor articles published (board members + advisors + at least 1 featured artist)
- **Cross-promotion:** at least 3 reciprocal cross-posts with other Bitcoin or arts Substacks
- **Art + Zap Weekend funnel:** measurable lift in event landing page traffic + sponsor inquiry emails directly attributable to Substack mentions
- **Audience quality:** at least 5% of subscribers convert to a Sovereign Circle membership signup OR an Art + Zap Weekend sponsor inquiry

## What success does NOT mean

- Going viral. Not the goal. A focused 500-person Substack of Bitcoin + arts decision-makers is worth more than a 50,000-person Substack of strangers.
- Publishing for the sake of publishing. Every post should advance one of: newsjacking + commentary, mission storytelling, Art + Zap awareness, or community building.
- Replacing the Beehiiv newsletter. Substack is for longform editorial commentary; Beehiiv stays for direct community updates, donation asks, and operational news. The two complement, they don't compete.
