# Art + Zap Weekend — 12-Week Substack Arc

A focused content arc designed specifically to drive Art + Zap Weekend awareness, sponsor inquiries, and audience signups for the September event. Designed to layer with the general content calendar in `01` — these are the Art + Zap–specific posts plus how to time them for maximum impact.

## The arc structure

The arc has 4 phases:

| Phase | Weeks | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — Quiet build** (May) | Weeks 1–4 | Get cadence working before announcing the event publicly. Small mentions only. |
| **Phase 2 — Announcement** (early June) | Weeks 5–6 | Public reveal of the event with Pubkey DC date confirmed. Big push. |
| **Phase 3 — Sponsor + artist roster reveals** (June–July) | Weeks 7–11 | Steady drip of news as sponsors close and artists confirm. Each reveal is a content moment. |
| **Phase 4 — Final sprint** (Aug + early Sept) | Weeks 12–17 | Daily countdown energy. Live Notes from production. Day-of coverage. |

---

## Phase 1 — Quiet build (Weeks 1–4, May)

**Goal:** Don't blow the announcement until Pubkey DC date is confirmed and the landing page is fully ready. Get readers comfortable with the BFTA voice before the big push.

**Substack content:**
- Mention Art + Zap Weekend casually in 1–2 articles' CTAs ("save the date — September 2026, more soon")
- Notes that hint at the work: "headed to Vegas to find sponsors for our biggest event ever"
- Beth's Friday article (Week 1) ends with: "If you want to support BFTA's biggest year, watch this space."

**Don't yet:**
- Publish a dedicated Art + Zap Weekend longform
- Announce the September date publicly
- Push hard for sponsor inquiries

**Why hold:** Until Pubkey DC writes back with date confirmation and the landing page is fully populated, every public mention is risk. One date change after public announcement is a credibility hit; saying "September 2026, date locking imminent" privately to sponsors is fine and we already do it.

## Phase 2 — Announcement (Weeks 5–6, early June)

**Goal:** Big public reveal once the date is locked. Coordinated push across all channels.

**Trigger event:** Pubkey DC confirms a specific Monday (likely Sept 14 per Trey's recap).

**Substack content:**

- **Wednesday Week 5 longform:** "Announcing: Art + Zap Weekend — September 2026 at Pubkey DC"
  - Hook with a specific, vivid image of what the event will be (Broadway-caliber headliner, 21 artists, live broadcast, in-person finale)
  - Explain the Bitcoin-funded fundraiser model (Kenneth-corrected positioning)
  - Sponsor opportunity callout with sponsor@bitcoinforthearts.org and the landing page URL
  - End with a specific ask: "Want to be involved? Sponsor, host, attend, share."

- **Friday Week 5 longform (contributor — Julie):** "What Live Music on Bitcoin Sounds Like"
  - Technical-side perspective on broadcasting + production
  - Naturally drives credibility for the event being well-produced

- **Notes through the week:** Pull quotes from the announcement, repost from BFTA-aligned voices reacting, "if you want to be one of the 21 artists, here's how to apply"

**Coordination beyond Substack:**
- Beehiiv newsletter same week (drives readers to the Substack announcement)
- Nostr + X campaign reposting the announcement
- Email to all Sovereign Circle members + prior Bitcoin conference contacts
- Direct text/Signal to all confirmed-or-pending sponsors with the link

## Phase 3 — Sponsor + artist roster reveals (Weeks 7–11, June–July)

**Goal:** Sustained drip of news. Each sponsor close + each artist confirmation is a content moment that drives renewed attention.

**Content rhythm:**

| Trigger | Content |
|---|---|
| First sponsor closes (verbal or signed) | Wednesday article: "Why [Sponsor] Sponsored Art + Zap Weekend" — interview-style piece with the sponsor's CMO/founder. Establishes credibility, models the "sponsorship is real" social proof. |
| First 7 artists confirmed | Wednesday article: "Profiles of the First 7 Featured Artists" — 100-word capsule profile of each, with their work + their Lightning address |
| Title sponsor closes | Standalone Wednesday article + Notes campaign + Beehiiv feature |
| Broadway headliner confirmed | If named publicly: Wednesday article about the booking. If unnamed (preferred — keep mystery for the finale): a Note teasing "We've confirmed our Broadway-caliber finale headliner. We'll share the name 2 weeks before the event." |
| Pubkey DC venue confirmed publicly | Wednesday article: "Why Pubkey DC Is the Right Home for Art + Zap Weekend's Finale" — partner credit + venue story |

**Why this works:** Steady drip means the event lives in readers' minds for 2 months. A single big announcement followed by silence loses momentum. Constant small reveals build anticipation.

**Friday contributor angle for this phase:** Featured artists writing their own pre-event reflections. "Why I said yes to participating in Art + Zap Weekend without an honorarium" — exactly the kind of authentic mission storytelling that converts readers into supporters.

## Phase 4 — Final sprint (Weeks 12–17, August + early September)

**Goal:** Daily energy. Build crescendo. Maximum sponsor + audience conversion in the final 4 weeks.

**Cadence shift:** Starting **August 4**, increase Notes frequency to daily. Wednesday + Friday longform stays the same.

**Content rhythm:**

| Week | Wednesday article | Friday article (contributor) | Notes theme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 5 | "Meet the 4 Bitcoin Podcasters Hosting Art + Zap" | A featured artist on what they're preparing | Daily Notes building anticipation |
| Aug 12 | "How the Audience Will Fund Q3's Micro-Grants — A Donor's-Eye View" | Production team perspective | Daily |
| Aug 19 | "Sponsoring Art + Zap Weekend — A Sponsor's Perspective" (with confirmed sponsor) | Featured artist | Daily |
| Aug 26 | "How We Built a Bitcoin Fundraiser Using Only Bitcoin Rails" — operational deep dive | Featured artist | Daily |
| Sept 2 | "The Pubkey DC Finale — What to Expect Monday Night" | TBD | Daily countdown |
| Sept 9 | "5 Days Out — Final Preview of Art + Zap Weekend" | TBD | Daily intensified |

**Day-of (Sept 13–15 — Saturday + Sunday broadcast + Monday finale):**

- **Live Notes** every 1–2 hours throughout the broadcast: pull quotes from artists, zap leaderboard updates, sponsor recognition shouts, behind-the-scenes photos
- Cross-post all live Notes to Nostr + X
- Saturday EOD Note: total raised so far + Day 1 highlights
- Sunday EOD Note: total raised + Monday finale teaser
- Monday EOD Note: from Pubkey DC venue with the auction results + Broadway headliner reveal photo
- **Within 7 days of event:** comprehensive longform recap with totals, photos, what was accomplished, what was funded, what's next

## After the event — Phase 5: harvest the moment (Weeks 18–22)

The event is over. Most events let momentum die here. BFTA shouldn't.

| Week | Content |
|---|---|
| Week 18 | "Recap longform" — totals, photos, named donors, named sponsors, what was funded |
| Week 19 | "Q3 Micro-Grants from the Event — Who Got Funded" — the artists nationwide who received the funds raised |
| Week 20 | Featured artists from the broadcast write follow-up pieces about what they've been working on since |
| Week 21 | Sponsor case study — what their sponsorship returned in brand value, audience engagement, etc |
| Week 22 | "What We Learned — And What's Next for BFTA" — sets up the next event arc |

**Why the post-event arc matters:** This is when the audience grew the most. Capturing them with great content keeps them subscribed beyond the event itself, builds compounding value for the next event arc.

## What "success" looks like for this arc

By the time the event happens in September:

- **Substack subscriber growth** specifically attributable to Art + Zap content: at least 30–40% of total period growth
- **Sponsor inquiries** generated from Substack readers: at least 3 (track via UTM-tagged links from articles)
- **Substack mentions** picked up by other Bitcoin or arts publications: at least 2 cross-publication features
- **Day-of broadcast viewership** with at least 20% coming from Substack-driven traffic
- **Post-event recap** is one of BFTA's highest-engagement Substack pieces ever

These are testable. Track them throughout, evaluate at the post-event Phase 5 mark.
