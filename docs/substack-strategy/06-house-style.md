# BFTA house style (writing)

Standing rules for Substack drafts, newsletter copy, board prose, and any longform written for Bitcoin for the Arts.

## No em dashes

**Do not use em dashes (—) anywhere** in BFTA-authored copy.

Also avoid en dashes (–) as punctuation. For number ranges, use a plain hyphen: `9-11 minutes`, `48-72 hours`.

Prefer commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or a short new sentence instead of a dash interrupt.

**Bad:** The fund fills gaps — it should.  
**Good:** The fund fills gaps. It should.

**Bad:** Bitcoin is not health insurance — say it twice.  
**Good:** Bitcoin is not health insurance. Say it twice.

This is a hard house rule (Dion, Aug 2026), not a soft preference.

## Related voice notes (from recent Substack drafts)

- Complete a news story; do not rebut the journalist when the facts are right
- Define money terms in plain language before leaning on them
- Do not oversell Bitcoin as a cure for healthcare, healthcare policy, or institutional failure
- Prefer specific names, numbers, and cites over generic nonprofit language

## Founder bio (canonical)

Use this wording unless Dion updates it:

> He spent **sixteen years as a professional dancer** with Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater, Dance Theatre of Harlem, Philadanco, and Disney’s *The Lion King* on Broadway, and **twenty-five years in the industry**.

Do not write “twenty-five years as a professional dancer.” That collapses performing years and total industry years.
