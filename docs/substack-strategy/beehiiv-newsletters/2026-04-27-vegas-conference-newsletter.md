Greetings from the BFTA team —

A lot to share this week. We have a new visual identity. We have a refreshed website. We're at the Bitcoin conference in Las Vegas. And our flagship event in September is coming together fast. Quick run through it all.

---

### Our new brand is live

Bitcoin For The Arts has a new visual identity. The logo is built from "Bitcoin for the" set in **Neue Haas Grotesk** — a modernist Swiss sans-serif chosen for its clarity and timeless balance — and "Arts" set in a modified script. The words are arranged to **form a frame**, alluding directly to the work the organization does: enabling patronage.

The color palette pairs **Bitcoin orange** with **neon green**, balanced by **cream** and **black** — saturated colors held in tension by neutrals, energetic but elegant. The system is built to come alive through bold layering, high-contrast combinations, and dynamic compositions where graphic elements overlap and interact.

Our refreshed website is live now at **bitcoinforthearts.org** — the new identity is in place across the homepage, About, Donate, Sovereign Circle, and our flagship Art + Zap Weekend event page. Take a look.

This isn't decoration. A serious nonprofit needs a serious visual identity, and we wanted ours to feel like the work — modern, deliberate, built to last.

---

### Why Vegas

The Bitcoin community in 2026 is the same community that funds — or doesn't fund — the next decade of independent artists. We're showing up because the people who are going to make Bitcoin For The Arts work are at this conference. Some of them are sponsors. Some of them are podcasters who'll co-host our flagship event in September. Some of them are artists who don't yet know there's a 501(c)(3) paying creators exclusively in Bitcoin. Some of them are donors who care about the arts and want a Bitcoin-native way to act on that.

We're walking the floor. We're not exhibiting. We're not on a panel. The work this week is hallway work — short conversations, warm intros, follow-up calls back home next week.

If you're at Bitcoin Vegas this week and reading this — please reply to this email, or DM us on Nostr (`npub15rnn220qfdyhpgv9apjt38nadc0dzj8a7zpcrd2q4spq5apvvt2suswnaw`). We'd love to grab a coffee. The whole point of being there is to find each other.

---

### What's coming — Art + Zap Weekend, September 2026

We want to update you on the event we've been quietly building toward all spring.

**Art + Zap Weekend** is BFTA's flagship Bitcoin-funded fundraiser. The format:

- **Two days of live broadcast** — Saturday and Sunday, 1–5 pm ET each day, broadcast simultaneously to YouTube, X, and Nostr (Zap.Stream)
- **21 featured artists** across music, dance, theater, visual art, film, writing, and storytelling — each contributing their time as mission supporters, with their own Lightning addresses on-screen during their broadcast segments as optional tip jars
- **Hosted by 2–4 Bitcoin podcasters per day** (up to 8 across the weekend)
- **A Monday-evening in-person finale** at **Pubkey DC** with live performances, a silent art auction, and a Broadway-caliber headlined performance (Broadway is dark on Mondays, which makes top NYC talent uniquely available)

We are targeting a Monday evening between **late August and early October 2026** for the finale. We'll lock the specific weekend publicly the moment Pubkey DC writes back with their availability — likely within the next week or two.

**It's a fundraiser FOR Bitcoin For The Arts.** Audience donations during the broadcast route directly to BFTA and feed our nationwide quarterly micro-grant cycle. At our target funding level, this single event seeds roughly **49 Bitcoin micro-grants for artists nationwide** — far more than the 21 artists on the broadcast itself. Every sponsorship dollar follows our public 55/30/10/5 allocation model.

The full event page lives at **bitcoinforthearts.org/art-zap-weekend** — sponsor inquiries to **sponsor@bitcoinforthearts.org**.

Mission supporters welcome — no honorarium, but you keep 100% of any audience tips during your broadcast segment, and we'll feature your work to a global Bitcoin-arts audience.

| "Bitcoin-funded fundraiser FOR Bitcoin For The Arts" | Locked positioning per board input from Kenneth — explicitly NOT "Bitcoin-native, direct-to-artist" framing. Kept consistent across all BFTA materials. |
