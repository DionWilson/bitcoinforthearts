# Content Pillars + 12 Evergreen Angles + Newsjacking Radar

## The 4 content pillars

Every BFTA Substack article should map to one of these. If a draft doesn't fit any, kill the draft.

### Pillar 1 — Newsjacking the arts-funding crisis (~40% of content)

**The premise:** Federal, state, and city arts funding is collapsing. Every collapse is a story. BFTA shows up with informed commentary the day or week the news breaks.

- Federal NEA cuts and elimination attempts
- State-level arts agency budget reductions (NASAA tracks these)
- City-level arts cuts (San Diego, Minneapolis, Missouri are recent examples)
- Foundation grant program closures or pivots
- Specific institutional layoffs / closures
- Arts-press coverage of the broader crisis (NYT, Hyperallergic, ARTnews)

### Pillar 2 — Mission storytelling (~30% of content)

**The premise:** People donate to stories, not statistics. Tell the BFTA story in installments.

- Profile pieces on featured artists (one per month minimum)
- "What [$X] funds" — concrete examples of grant impact
- Inside-the-org pieces — board decisions, transparency reports, allocation math
- Founder narrative — Dion's path from Lion King → Dance Theatre of Harlem → BFTA
- Sovereign Circle member spotlights (with consent)

### Pillar 3 — Bitcoin thesis for arts funding (~20% of content)

**The premise:** Make the technical Bitcoin argument accessible to the arts world AND the artistic argument accessible to the Bitcoin world. Sit at the intersection.

- Why fixed supply matters for endowments
- Lightning Network basics for artists who've never received Bitcoin
- The HODL Vault concept and why it's different from a traditional endowment
- Sound money + low time preference as creative philosophy
- Bitcoin-as-property for tax-deductible donations (IRS Notice 2014-21 explainers)

### Pillar 4 — Art + Zap Weekend (~10% of content, scaling up to 30% in last 8 weeks)

**The premise:** Drive awareness, sponsor inquiries, and audience signups for the September event.

- Event format announcements
- Featured artist profiles tied to the event
- Sponsor spotlights as they confirm
- Behind-the-scenes production posts
- Day-of live coverage

## Newsjacking Radar — How to Catch the Story

Newsjacking only works if you're catching the story within 48 hours of breaking. Set up these monitors:

### Daily checks (10 minutes, morning)

| Source | What to watch for |
|---|---|
| **Hyperallergic** | Daily arts-funding crisis coverage |
| **ARTnews** | Institutional layoffs, foundation news |
| **NYT Arts section** | Major arts organization stories |
| **NEA news + NASAA** | Federal + state policy moves |
| **Americans for the Arts** | Advocacy alerts on funding cuts |
| **Twitter/X search:** `arts funding cut` `NEA defunded` `museum layoffs` | Real-time news as it breaks |

### Weekly checks (30 minutes, Sunday evening for the week ahead)

| Source | What to watch for |
|---|---|
| **Inside Philanthropy** | Foundation funding shifts |
| **Nonprofit Quarterly** | Sector-wide trend pieces |
| **Bitcoin Magazine + No Bitcoin Only** | Bitcoin community news that intersects with culture |
| **The Block / CoinDesk** | Crypto regulation that affects nonprofit BTC custody |
| **State arts agency newsletters** | Quietly-announced budget cuts that don't hit national press |

### Bitcoin/Nostr-specific monitors

| Source | What to watch for |
|---|---|
| **Plebchain Radio + adjacent podcast network** | New Bitcoin-arts intersection content |
| **Wavlake / Fountain trending** | Bitcoin-funded artists gaining traction |
| **Nostr search for `art` + `bitcoin`** | Real-time Nostr conversation BFTA can join |

### Process: news → published article in <48 hours

1. **Hour 0** — story breaks; you see it on a daily monitor
2. **Hour 1** — within an hour, write a short Note on Substack reacting to the story (1–3 sentences + link). This stakes BFTA's claim on the conversation immediately.
3. **Hour 1** — same Note, cross-post to Nostr + X. Tag relevant accounts.
4. **Hour 2–24** — outline the longform article: thesis, 3–5 supporting points, BFTA mission tie-in, CTA
5. **Hour 24–36** — write the longform draft (~800 words for newsjacks; tighter is better)
6. **Hour 36–48** — edit, format, publish. Cross-post to Nostr + X with a stronger pull quote than the original Note.

The window matters because the story is dead by day 4. **Faster + shorter beats slower + more polished for newsjacking specifically.**

### Saved-search shortcuts (set these up once)

- Google Alerts: `"arts funding"` + `cut OR slash OR eliminate OR layoffs` (daily digest)
- Google Alerts: `"NEA"` (immediate)
- Google Alerts: `"state arts agency"` + `budget` (daily)
- Twitter/X saved searches: `arts funding cut`, `nonprofit Bitcoin`, `Bitcoin arts grant`

## Editorial voice guide

When in doubt about tone, model after what's already published:

- **"The Lights Are Going Out. Bitcoin Is the Only Switch Left."** — direct, declarative, confident, references specific events with specific numbers, ends with a clear thesis
- **"Is War Good for Bitcoin?"** — historical, reasoned, "this is mathematical, not political" framing, treats reader as smart

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Generic "we believe in art" platitudes
- ❌ Passive voice ("artists are being failed by the system" → say WHO is failing them)
- ❌ Cypherpunk jargon without translation (define "low time preference," explain what "self-custody" means in context)
- ❌ Begging tone ("please donate to us!") — the work argues for itself; close with a clear next-step but never plead

**Do:**
- ✅ Use specific numbers and names
- ✅ Reference Dion's personal background as an artist when relevant
- ✅ Tie every article to either an action ("here's how to support this work") or a position ("here's what we believe BFTA should do about this")
- ✅ Close with a clear next-step CTA
- ✅ Use Substack's footnote feature for sources rather than inline links — keeps reading flow clean
