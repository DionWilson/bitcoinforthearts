## Bitcoin For The Arts

Bitcoin For The Arts is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to helping working artists understand sound money and use it. This Substack is where we make the case.

Every working artist in America is being reshaped, quietly, by a monetary system almost none of them were ever taught to see. Budgets shrink. Rents climb. Health benefits erode. Discretionary audiences vanish. Regional theaters close at a rate of two to three companies per month. Broadway musicals win Tony Awards, gross a million dollars a week, and still cannot survive the math. And in all of it, the word "inflation" almost never appears.

This publication exists to name what is actually happening, from inside the industry it is happening to.

## Who is writing

Bitcoin For The Arts was founded by Dion Wilson, a former professional dancer who spent twenty-five years on the biggest stages in the country. Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater. Dance Theatre of Harlem. Philadanco. Disney's *The Lion King* on Broadway.

He watched budgets contract, health benefits erode, and audiences vanish across all of it, and he did not understand why while it was happening. He does now. Bitcoin For The Arts is the education he wishes he had had as a young artist, and every essay in this Substack is written in service of the working artist who is where he was.

## What you get

Long-form essays on where the fiat monetary system meets the working life of the artist. Postmortems of specific cultural moments where the two collide. Companion pieces to the BFTA podcast, *Share Your Bitcoin Journey*. Original reporting on how Bitcoin, self-custody, sound money, and Value-for-Value distribution are quietly reshaping cinema, theater, dance, music, and visual art around the world.

Not evangelism. Not investment advice. Not sales.

Education. From inside the industry. Written for artists who deserve to see what is being done to them.
