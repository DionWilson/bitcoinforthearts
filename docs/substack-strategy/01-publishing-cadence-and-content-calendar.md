# Publishing Cadence + 12-Week Content Calendar

## The cadence

| Day | Output | Source | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | 1 Substack Note | Dion (or contributor) | 1–3 sentences, optionally w/ link |
| **Wednesday** | 1 longform article | **Dion (always)** | 800–1,500 words |
| **Thursday** | 1 Substack Note | Dion (or contributor) | 1–3 sentences |
| **Friday** | 1 longform article | **Contributor** (board / advisor / featured artist) | 600–1,200 words |
| **Saturday** | 1 Substack Note | Dion or auto-cross-post from a Beehiiv newsletter highlight | Short share |

**Total weekly output:** 2 longform articles + 3 Notes = exactly the cadence you wanted, but only 1 longform per week from Dion personally.

## Why this rhythm specifically

- **Wednesday longform from Dion** — mid-week is the highest-engagement day for nonprofit Substack reads. Dion writes the strategic anchor of the week.
- **Friday longform from a contributor** — end-of-week is when readers catch up. Contributor voice diversifies the publication and makes BFTA feel like a community of writers, not one founder shouting into the void.
- **Mon/Thu/Sat Notes** — Substack's algorithm rewards Notes engagement (it's their TikTok-style growth feature). Three Notes a week is the floor for the algorithm to keep recommending the publication.
- **No publishing Tuesday or Sunday** — gives Dion writing time on Tuesday for the Wednesday post, and a no-content rest day Sunday.

**News-reaction Note (Mon morning):**

## How to use this calendar in practice

- **Print this and pin it next to your desk.** The calendar is the commitment device. Without it the cadence collapses by week 4.
- **Saturday evening = next week's planning.** Spend 30 minutes Saturday reviewing the news, locking in the Wednesday topic, confirming the Friday contributor.
- **Tuesday is writing day.** Wednesday's longform should be drafted Tuesday morning, polished Tuesday evening, scheduled to publish Wednesday 9 am ET (highest open rate for nonprofit content).
- **Friday's contributor article needs to be in your hands by Wednesday EOD** so you have a full day Thursday to edit, format, and schedule. Build that deadline into every contributor invitation (see file `03`).
- **Notes are not optional.** They are what feeds the Substack algorithm. Skipping Notes is skipping growth.

## Editorial bar — what passes vs what doesn't

**Pass:**
- Has a clear thesis stated in the first 3 paragraphs
- References a specific recent event, statistic, or person (not generic)
- Includes at least one BFTA mission tie-in
- Ends with a clear next-step CTA: subscribe, become a Sovereign Circle member, sponsor Art + Zap Weekend, share this article
- Reads in under 8 minutes

**Doesn't pass:**
- Generic "Bitcoin is good for the arts" essay with no specific hook
- Pure marketing copy with no journalism / commentary value
- Anything written without naming a specific artist, sponsor, or news event
- Anything over 1,800 words (split into a 2-parter instead)
