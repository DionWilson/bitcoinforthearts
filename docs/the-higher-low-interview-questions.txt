Share Your Bitcoin Journey: The Higher Low - Interview Questions

Episode: 17 (adjust if numbering shifts)
Guest: The Higher Low / Rami Korhonen, @thehigherlow
Format: Share Your Bitcoin Journey house format

Format note (Dion only): Two beats per question. One short premise + one short
question. ~30-40 words total. Anything that doesn't fit the premise becomes a
follow-up after he answers. Same intellectual depth as the Benjamin Ella set;
just tighter delivery so the punch lands and Rami doesn't lose the thought.


HOST INTRO (spoken on camera before Q1)

Same structure as the James Kintore and Benjamin Ella episodes: constant opener,
then guest framing, then a one-line "in this conversation" tease, then the
handoff.

  Welcome to another episode of Share Your Bitcoin Journey presented by
  Bitcoin for the Arts. I am Dion Wilson, the founder and executive director
  for Bitcoin for the Arts, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit empowering artists with
  Bitcoin grants, education, and sovereign funding tools.

  Today, we have the pleasure of speaking with the Finnish Bitcoin musician
  behind The Higher Low, also known as Rami Korhonen, the singer of the
  melodic punk rock band Fumble since the nineties, and the one-person
  do-it-yourself project he calls Acoustic Pleb Rock.

  He started writing songs for The Higher Low in 2021, recorded the first
  batch over Christmas break, released LFG in January 2022, and released
  the debut album Node To Freedom in February 2023. Since then he has played
  Bitcoin stages from Amsterdam to Charlotte to Riga to Thailand, finished
  an eleven-country solo tour, and released the full-band second album
  Freedom In the Digital Age in August 2025.

  In this conversation, he shares his personal journey from Finnish punk
  singer to Bitcoin troubadour, why his songs never use the word Bitcoin,
  what the road taught him about plebs and campfires, and what changed when
  he handed the new record to a full band. His story is loud, homemade, and
  built to travel.

  Without further ado, welcome to the show, The Higher Low.

BACKGROUND & JOURNEY

1. The Higher Low, welcome to Share Your Bitcoin Journey. Before the albums,
before the conferences, before anyone in Bitcoin knew the name, there was a
singer in a Finnish punk band. Where does that story actually begin for you?

2. You have fronted Fumble since the nineties and the band is still going,
latest record in 2022. What did that band teach you about songs, crowds, and
staying power that you carried straight into the solo project?

3. You have said you wanted a solo project for years but never had the idea
until these songs. What was missing all that time, and what clicked on those
business-trip weekends in 2021?

4. The first batch came together over Christmas 2021 and LFG came out in
January 2022. Take us into that break. What did you record, and how did you
know this was not just a demo anymore?

5. Out of all that came Node To Freedom in February 2023. For listeners hearing
your name for the first time, what is that record, and what standard did you
hold those first songs to?


THE BITCOIN TURN

6. You noticed Bitcoin in 2017 and fell all the way in a few years later. Was
there a specific moment, person, or lived experience when it stopped being
interesting and became the rest of your life?

7. You describe Bitcoin's pull as freedom and opportunity breaking the legacy
elites' hold on ordinary people. As a working musician, what did you see in
the music business that made that frame land so hard?

8. In Our Minds caught Bitcoin Beats X Spaces in early 2023 and you were a
featured guest. What did that first Bitcoin audience give back that a normal
rock crowd never had?


THE RULE: SONGS ABOUT BITCOIN WITHOUT THE WORD

9. You write short punchy songs about Bitcoin's power and ban the word from
the lyric. Who is that rule for: the campfire fan who is not orange-pilled
yet, or the pleb who already knows and likes hearing it sideways?

10. Most Bitcoin music preaches to the room. Yours tries to pass the campfire
test first. When does a new song earn the Bitcoin reading, and when do you
throw it out for trying too hard?

11. Acoustic Pleb Rock is your name for it: one voice, one electro-acoustic
guitar, catchy on purpose. What does stripping the song that bare force you
to get right in the writing?


PUNK, DIY, AND OPEN SOURCE

12. You wrote in 2023 that Bitcoin is punk rock: no boss, do it yourself,
against the old order. You have lived in a punk band since the nineties.
Where does that comparison hold, and where do Bitcoiners get punk wrong?

13. You wanted The Higher Low to start grassroots DIY, the way most free and
open-source software starts, then add layers over time. What did you keep
loose on purpose in the early days so the project could grow?

14. You have said Bitcoin, Nostr, and open-source tools can make the future
brighter, and music is how you hurry it. Where does a song do something those
tools cannot?


THE ROAD

15. After Node To Freedom you played Amsterdam, Hodlween in Charlotte,
Satoshi Rockamoto in Riga, a Thailand afterparty, then eleven countries solo
in 2023 to 2024. What did a Bitcoin conference teach you that a punk club
already knew?

16. Mike Jarmuz said you kicked off the Riga party right and told bookers you
do not disappoint. What is your solo show built to do in the first three
songs: win the room, teach the room, or get the room singing?

17. You posted about jamming with Knut Svanholm. What happens when a songwriter
and a Bitcoin writer sit in the same room, and did that jam change a song?


FREEDOM IN THE DIGITAL AGE

18. After the solo tour you asked your Fumble friends and the Helsinki scene
to make a full-production record. What did you hand Jesperi Mommo, Mikko Salo,
and Henkka Huhtanen, and what did you refuse to hand over?

19. Freedom Engine was written in El Zonte in 2024: generation twenty-one,
the bright orange sun, start the freedom engine. Why did that song have to be
written next to the water in El Salvador?

20. You launched the album August 15 2025 with a full-band show at BTChel
Helsinki and crowdfunded the vinyl on Geyser in bitcoin. What did asking
plebs to pay for music teach you about value?


LOOKING FORWARD

21. Five years from now: The Higher Low, Fumble, Rami personally. Where do you
want to be, and how does Bitcoin sit inside that picture?


THE CLOSING QUESTION

22. Last question, we ask every guest this on Share Your Bitcoin Journey. If
a $500 to $2,000 Bitcoin micro-grant from Bitcoin for the Arts landed in your
wallet tomorrow, what would it unlock for you as a creator?
