=== DESCRIPTION ===

In this conversation, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Nadia Vaeh, an award-winning recording artist and filmmaker whose music has always been about more than entertainment.

An Atlanta native building a career across songwriting, touring, and her label Galactic Cat Records, Nadia has spent more than a decade living the independent artist grind: writing, performing, building businesses, and stacking the side hustles most creatives never put on the poster. That lived experience became the spark for her first featured documentary, One Poop Scoop Away (directed by Josh Bedsole), a film that asks the question thousands of artists carry quietly: what does it actually mean to make it in today's music industry?

Rather than chasing celebrity, she is documenting the resilient sacrifice and unconventional paths of artists, producers, managers, and industry voices who keep choosing the work. The feature is still completing interviews, with editing ahead and a teaser already in hand. This September she joins Bitcoin For The Arts at the Midwest Bitcoin Summit in Columbus, presenting that teaser and a live talk inside the Bitcoin Arts Park booth, capturing new footage on site for the film, and standing with us as a working artist in the room.

In this episode we talk about music as a lifeline, what independent artists are really navigating right now, how Bitcoin enters a creative life built on honesty and survival, and what it means to bring this story into a Bitcoin conference audience that needs to meet real artists, not abstracts.

IN THIS EPISODE:
- Growing up as the black sheep and turning to music as nervous-system comfort
- The day at 23 she quit her boyfriend, quit school, and quit her job to become an artist
- Losing her mother at 17, and music as language for grief
- A decade of indie touring, side hustles, burnout, and peeling back the highlight reel
- Why "what does it mean to make it?" got loud enough to become a film
- Documenting the industry from inside as both filmmaker and participant
- Streaming math, broken royalties, and why patronage has to return
- Songwriter rights, splits, PROs, and what nobody teaches at the start
- Pairing songs with causes: Alliance of Hope, Peace Over Violence, and more
- Getting paid in Bitcoin, then going deeper with Ainsley Costello and Julie
- Ownership and independence versus the easier label path
- Bringing One Poop Scoop Away to Midwest Bitcoin Summit (teaser, talk, Q&A, pitch)
- Capturing Summit footage for the documentary
- Five-year vision: music as the main entree, not twelve side dishes
- Advice for indie artists putting a toe in Bitcoin
- What a $500 to $2,000 BFTA Bitcoin grant would unlock in post-production
