**Nobody in financial services is marketing to this customer class.** Not Bitcoin IRA Inc. Not Swan. Not the legacy IRA providers. They don't know how to reach the performing-arts community, they don't have the cultural credibility, and they don't have the union-side relationships.

**BFTA does.** Dion does. ## 4. The macro pension-crisis context (background, not the lead)

Use this only if Frank or Madison ask "why now" or "why is this customer ready." The backdrop:

- **AFM-EPF (Musicians)** received a **$1.527 billion federal bailout** in August 2024 to avoid 2034 insolvency. Without it, benefits would have been cut by ~50%. Plan covers 49,180 participants — every one of them got the "your pension might be at risk" notice.
- **Radio, Television and Recording Arts Plan** (NYC, 516 entertainment-industry participants) received **$63 million** in 2024 to avoid 2027 insolvency.
- **WGA Health Fund** lost $200 million in the past 4 years and was bailed out in the 2026 contract.
- **SAG-AFTRA** is merging the SAG-Producers Pension Plan with the AFTRA Retirement Fund effective Jan 1, 2028 — partly to stabilize the AFTRA side.
- **The federal Special Financial Assistance program** (Butch Lewis Act, 2021) has approved ~$54.6B across 818,000 workers, with a total of ~$94B allocated. Designed to keep recipient plans afloat through 2051. By federal mandate, recipient plans are **classified as "critical status" through 2051 regardless of actual financial health**.

The story this paints: **the fiat pension system for performing artists has materially failed**, and the "fix" is a printed-money stay of execution. The customer class is *primed* for an alternative — they've already been told their plans need rescuing.

## 5. The structural mismatch (deploy if needed)

Performing artists have the worst-shaped retirement need that fiat pensions are equipped to handle:

| Lifecycle factor | Office worker | Performing artist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active career length | ~40 years (25 → 65) | **~10–25 years** (dancers retire 35–45) |
| Income shape | Steady salary | Project-based, irregular |
| Vesting | Usually achieved | **Many never accumulate enough credits in a single plan** |
| Years drawing retirement | ~20 (65 → 85) | **~40–50** |
| Savings window | ~40 years | **~15 years** |

Bitcoin's hard cap and long-term appreciation profile **mathematically matches the artist's lifecycle**. Short savings window + long drawdown window + portable, non-debaseable asset = the right shape.
