=== TITLE ===

Share Your Bitcoin Journey: Episode 9 – Beth Fletcher (Lady Nakamoto): From the X Games to Bitcoin Art


=== DESCRIPTION ===

In this episode of Share Your Bitcoin Journey, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Beth Fletcher, the Alaskan digital artist known to the Bitcoin world as Lady Nakamoto. This is the first time Beth has sat down for a full interview, and the conversation traces a path almost no one else in Bitcoin can claim: from the X Games and snowboarding World Cups, to the Rolf Institute, to a felony charge that nearly broke her, to a vision quest that ultimately led her into Bitcoin and digital art.

Beth represented BFTA on the floor at Bitcoin Is For Everyone in Portland, where she sold her first artwork peer-to-peer with Bitcoin. She has been a supporter of Bitcoin For The Arts from the very beginning. Her work has been featured at the Bitcoin conference in Las Vegas and lives at the intersection of Bitcoin, sovereignty, and Alaskan landscape.

This is an unfiltered conversation about wholeness, sovereignty, and what it actually means to live the peer-to-peer ethos as an artist.

ABOUT BETH FLETCHER (LADY NAKAMOTO):
Beth Fletcher is an Alaskan digital artist, a former X Games and World Cup snowboarder, and a Rolfer trained at the Rolf Institute in 2007. She lives and works in Alaska, where she creates digital art under the name Lady Nakamoto exploring themes of sovereignty, politics, the American experience, and the Bitcoin revolution. Her work has been featured at the Bitcoin conference in Las Vegas, and she represented Bitcoin For The Arts on the floor at Bitcoin Is For Everyone in Portland. She has been a donor and early supporter of BFTA from the beginning.
