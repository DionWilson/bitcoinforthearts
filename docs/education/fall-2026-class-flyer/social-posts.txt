Four classes on sound money. One hour each. Live online, plus one session in New York. Open to artists and everyone else.

Arts schools teach the craft. Almost none of them teach how the money works, why studio rent and tour costs outrun wages, or how a creator gets paid without a platform taking the margin.

This fall, Bitcoin For The Arts is teaching the first four classes in that series. Each one is a live webinar of about an hour, with time for questions. One session will also meet in person in New York. We are still setting the room, and a real headcount is how we book the right space.

Nov 9. What Is Money?
Nov 23. Unlocking New Creative Freedom
Dec 7. Bitcoin in Practice
Dec 14. Protecting Your Savings: Security and Self-Custody

We built these classes for working artists. They are also a plain-English start for anyone who wants to understand sound money. You do not have to be an artist to take a seat.

Reserve a seat: https://www.bitcoinforthearts.org/education/webinar#signup

Bitcoin For The Arts is a 501(c)(3).
