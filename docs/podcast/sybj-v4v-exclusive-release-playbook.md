# Share Your Bitcoin Journey: value-for-value exclusive release

A test of a new release pattern. Each new episode goes on Fanfares first, for 21 sats, for one week. An AQSTR campaign pays Nostr users in sats to spread the word. After seven days the episode goes free on YouTube and the RSS feed as usual.

Two goals:

1. Get paid for the content and grow listeners.
2. Show other creators that the value-for-value rails work, so they can use them too.

## How the pieces fit

| Rail | What it does | Who earns |
|---|---|---|
| Fanfares | Sells the episode for 21 sats | BFTA, per the payment split |
| Fanfares referrals | People who share a referral link earn a share of the sales they drive, if that setting is on | Listeners who share |
| AQSTR | Pays sats for reposts, quotes, replies, and follows on our Nostr post | Nostr users who engage |
| YouTube and RSS | Free release after the exclusive week | Reach, not revenue |

AQSTR pays for attention. Fanfares pays for listening. Together they reward both sharing the episode and buying it.

## Release timeline

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day -1 | Fanfares listing ready, 21 sats, with the 60 to 90 second public preview. Write the AQSTR post and fund the campaign. |
| Day 0 | Publish the Fanfares listing. Publish the AQSTR post. Dion reposts it from his own account and tags the guest. Post a short clip on Instagram and TikTok pointing to Fanfares. |
| Days 1 to 6 | Reply to everyone who engages. Zap back thoughtful quotes. Post one more clip midweek. Post the "Why 21 sats?" note. |
| Day 7 | Close the campaign. Record the numbers below. |
| Day 8 | Publish on YouTube and RSS. Post the "now free" note thanking everyone who paid. |
| Day 14 | Record YouTube views and RSS downloads for the free week. |

## AQSTR campaign settings

Start small. This is a test.

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Nostr event | Create New Post (copy below) | |
| Include campaign URL in post | On | Lets people find the campaign and earn sats |
| Like | 0 (off) | Likes do not spread the post |
| Repost | 50 sats | Puts the post in front of their followers |
| Quote | 100 sats | A quote with a comment is the strongest endorsement |
| Reply | 21 sats | Builds conversation under the post |
| Follow (new) | 50 sats | Builds the audience that sees the next episode |
| Budget | 10,000 sats | Plus the 20% platform fee: 12,000 sats total |
| Require NIP-05 | On | Cuts spam and bot accounts |
| Duration | 7 days, if an end date can be set | Matches the exclusive week |
| Eligibility | Trust score 50 or higher (default) | |

As a rough guide, 10,000 sats could cover something like 40 reposts, 30 quotes, 100 replies, and 50 new follows.

## Fanfares listing

Use the existing upload-pack format (see the Episode 13 pack), with these changes:

- **Price:** 21 sats
- **Preview audio:** always include a 60 to 90 second public preview. It is the free taste that makes people pay.
- **Referrals:** turn on the referral share if Fanfares offers it for the episode, so people who share the link earn sats.
- **Description, last line:** "Exclusive on Fanfares for one week. Free on YouTube and RSS from [date]."

## What to measure

Record these on Day 7 and Day 14 so each episode can be compared with the last.

| Measure | Where | Day 7 | Day 14 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sats spent (campaign plus fee) | AQSTR | | |
| Reposts, quotes, replies, new follows | AQSTR | | |
| Fanfares purchases, and sats earned | Fanfares | | |
| Zaps on the campaign post | Nostr client | | |
| New Nostr followers | Nostr client | | |
| YouTube views, first 7 days free | YouTube Studio | n/a | |
| RSS downloads, first 7 days free | RSS.com | n/a | |

Useful ratios:

- **Cost per paid listen:** AQSTR sats spent divided by Fanfares purchases.
- **Earned versus spent:** Fanfares sats earned divided by AQSTR sats spent.
- **Free-week lift:** YouTube and RSS numbers compared with the previous episode's first week.

At 21 sats per listen, the campaign will not pay for itself in sales at first. The test is whether paid sharing brings new listeners and followers who come back, and whether the free release does better after a week of buzz.

## Bookkeeping

- Record AQSTR spending as a marketing expense, with the dollar value on the day the sats were sent.
- Record Fanfares income as program revenue, with the dollar value on the day it was received.
- Keep the AQSTR receipt and a Fanfares sales screenshot for each episode.

## After the first two episodes

Review the numbers. Options:

- Keep 21 sats, or test a different price.
- Change the reward mix. For example, if quotes bring purchases, raise the quote reward and drop the reply reward.
- Raise the budget for guests with large Nostr audiences.
- Write up the results as a public guide for other artists, along with the guest.
