## HALLOWEEN SKIT SCRIPT

**"The Ghosts of Central Banking"**
Runtime: about 5 minutes. Dion as Jerome Powell. Pete as Christine Lagarde.

**Why it's funny right now (true as of October 31, 2026):**
- Powell's term as Fed chair ended in May 2026. Kevin Warsh is the chair now.
  Powell is still on the Board as a governor until 2028.
- Lagarde announced on October 29, 2026 that she's stepping down from the
  ECB on February 7, 2027, months before her term ends.
- The digital euro pilot starts in the second half of 2027. First issuance
  is possible in 2029, and only if EU lawmakers pass the regulation.
- Real quotes the skit plays on: Powell called 2021 inflation "transitory."
  In December 2024 he said bitcoin is "just like gold, only it's virtual,
  it's digital," and "a competitor for gold." In 2023 Lagarde said that in
  her "very humble assessment" bitcoin "is worth nothing."

**Opening title card (on screen, 3 seconds):**
"PARODY. The following characters are played by Bitcoiners in a van. Any
resemblance to actual central bankers is entirely intentional."

**Props**
- Powell: gray suit, navy tie, short silver wig.
- Lagarde: short white wig, silk scarf, pearls.
- Flashlight (for the under-the-chin scary lighting).
- Bowl of fun-size candy bars, plus one full-size bar.
- A "money printer": any cardboard box with a crank and a sign that says
  MONEY PRINTER. Fun-size bars come out of a slot.
- A phone with a big euro sign sticker taped on it.
- One of Pete's branding irons. **Cold.** Plus an already-branded leather
  coaster hidden under the table.
- Sound effects added in editing: thunder, door knocks, a creaking door,
  spooky organ sting, scream.

**Accent note:** keep Lagarde's French accent light and warm. The joke is
the policy, not the accent.

## FILMING IN THE VAN (production notes)

- **Engine off for the whole shoot.** Never record with the van idling.
  Carbon monoxide builds up fast in a closed van, and the engine hum ruins
  the audio anyway. Crack a window.
- **Park legally** in a lot or pull-off where you're allowed to stay for an
  hour or two. Check before you start setting up.
- **Sound:** a van is a metal box and it echoes. Hang a moving blanket or a
  couple of jackets on the walls behind the camera. Use lav mics, not the
  camera mic. Record 30 seconds of silence ("room tone") for the editor.
- **Light:** sit facing the open side door or back doors so the daylight
  hits your faces. Don't put a bright window behind either of you.
- **Scenic background:** frame the camera so the open door shows the view
  behind Pete, or shoot an exterior establishing shot of the van first.
- **Skit lighting:** for the dark cold open, close the doors and use only
  the flashlights. Do the skit in one block so the wigs go on once.
- **Phones on airplane mode.**
