===============================================================
TONE NOTES
===============================================================


- Lead with the ask, not the throat-clearing. "We need" beats "We are pleased to announce".
- Specific numbers beat vague qualifiers. "$13.8M to under $2M" beats "huge cuts".
- The Vegas dates (April 27-29) should appear in every Vegas-related post — short subject lines tend to get truncated and the dates need to be above the fold.
- For Nostr specifically, lean into the V4V ethos: receiving zaps on these posts is itself a small fundraising action. Don't overthink the call to action.
- For LinkedIn, slightly more professional but still direct - LinkedIn is where the corporate sponsors and their decision-makers actually read.
- For Instagram, the caption can be longer because IG users actually read captions; visual is the hook (suggest a photo of you packing for Vegas, the conference logo, or a pre-made graphic with the key dates and asks).
- "We don't make it without you" is a true statement, not a marketing line. Use it sparingly so it lands.
