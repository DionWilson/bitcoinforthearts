## Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. Expands Bitcoin-Native Support Model for Independent Creators

**New York, NY** - Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. (BFTA), a New York-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit, is advancing a Bitcoin-native patronage model designed to support independent artists through micro-grants, education, and community programming.

BFTA serves creators across visual arts, theater, dance, music, writing, storytelling, and film. The organization publishes a clear allocation framework that directs 55% of funds to artist grants, 30% to programs, 10% to operations, and 5% to a long-term Bitcoin reserve strategy.

"We are building a practical system where artists can receive support more directly and create with greater sovereignty," said Dion Wilson, Founder & Executive Director of Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. "Our work is about giving artists real tools, real funding pathways, and a long-term foundation."

Wilson brings a long career in the performing arts to this effort. He spent 16 years as a principal dancer and has performed with Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater, Dance Theatre of Harlem, and Philadelphia Dance Company. He toured nationally with *The Lion King* and contributed to the creation of *Fela! The Musical*. Through BFTA, he is now focused on artist-first funding infrastructure at the intersection of arts and Bitcoin.

The nonprofit's current public activities include artist storytelling, educational resources, and event-driven community engagement in New York City. Its stories platform highlights creators working at the edge of artistic and financial independence, including:

- **Matt Finlay**, a Melbourne-based dissident musician whose independent, value-for-value music model aligns with censorship-resistant publishing;
- **A13MW (Zap Animations)**, an animator developing hand-drawn Bitcoin narratives, with her short *Crying Baby, Dancing Bee* slated for the Bitcoin Film Festival in June 2026;
- **Hitomi Matsui**, an Osaka-born, New York-based visual artist exploring sovereignty and digital-age identity through contemplative illustration.

BFTA is also developing education programming for artists seeking practical Bitcoin fluency, including self-custody basics, receiving BTC, and creator-focused workflow training. Public materials are being published in open formats to increase accessibility.

As part of broader community participation, BFTA promotes fundraising formats such as "Bitcoin Art Night," a DIY model that helps supporters host local or virtual events to direct more resources toward artists.
