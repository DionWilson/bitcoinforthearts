# Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. - Fact Sheet

## At a glance

- **Legal name:** Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. (BFTA)
- **Entity type:** 501(c)(3) nonprofit
- **Headquarters:** New York, NY
- **Website:** https://bitcoinforthearts.org
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/Bitcoin-For-The-Arts
- **Primary contact:** hello@bitcoinforthearts.org
- **Press contact:** dionwilson@bitcoinforthearts.org

## Mission

Bitcoin For The Arts supports artists across visual arts, theater, dance, music, writing, storytelling, and film through Bitcoin micro-grants, workshops, residencies, and productions. The organization emphasizes artist sovereignty, transparent stewardship, and long-term support infrastructure.

## Positioning statement

BFTA is building a Bitcoin-native patronage model designed to help independent artists reduce dependence on gatekeepers, receive support more directly, and preserve value over time.

## Funding model (public allocation policy)

- **55%** Artist grants
- **30%** Programs (workshops, residencies, co-productions)
- **10%** Operations
- **5%** Bitcoin reserve ("HODL Vault")

Notes:
- BFTA publicly communicates an allocation framework and governance materials.
- The reserve address is intentionally not published for security reasons.

## Core programs

1. **Bitcoin micro-grants**
   - Small, fast grants for working artists.
   - Current website status notes applications are being collected, with grant processing planned to begin in **Q3 2026**.

2. **Education for artists**
   - Workshops and open materials on self-custody, receiving BTC, and artist workflows.
   - Public education resources are available under open licensing.

3. **Programming and community activation**
   - Workshops, events, and collaboration formats that connect artists and Bitcoin communities.
   - Includes DIY fundraising concepts such as "Bitcoin Art Night."

## Geographic scope

- Public-facing events and activity are currently centered in New York City.
- Grant eligibility language on the site indicates applicants can be global, while funded project activities must occur in the United States.

## Founder

**Dion Wilson**  
Founder & Executive Director, Interim Board Chair

Wilson is a performing artist and arts advocate with 16 years as a principal dancer. He has performed with Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater, Dance Theatre of Harlem, and Philadelphia Dance Company, toured nationally with *The Lion King*, and contributed to *Fela! The Musical*. He has mentored over 10,000 students and now leads BFTA's Bitcoin-native patronage strategy.
