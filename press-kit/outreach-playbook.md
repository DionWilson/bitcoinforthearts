# Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. - Outreach Playbook

## Objective

Secure initial arts media coverage by sequencing outreach from easiest-placement channels to higher-selectivity editorial desks.

## Priority sequence

1. **BroadwayWorld submission portal** (first pass, fastest)
2. **BroadwayWorld editorial inbox** (same week, with personalized note)
3. **Playbill editorial pitch** (personalized, founder-led angle)
4. **Additional arts outlets** (after first placement or if no response)

## Step-by-step workflow

### Step 1 - Prepare your send package

Use the files in this folder:

- One-page kit: `one-page-press-kit.md`
- Full fact sheet: `fact-sheet.md`
- Release copy: `press-release-draft.md`
- Pitch templates: `email-pitch-templates.md`
- Image shortlist: `media-assets-and-story-angles.md`

Recommended first attachment bundle:

1. One-page press kit (PDF export)
2. Press release draft (DOC or PDF)
3. 2-4 images + logo (SVG + PNG)

### Step 2 - Submit to BroadwayWorld

Portal: https://www.broadwayworld.com/submitnews.php

Submission guidance:

- Keep release copy around 400-500 words
- Lead with clear "what is new" language
- Include one strong founder image and one artist image
- Include direct links to site pages (About, Stories, Grants)

### Step 3 - Send direct pitch email (BroadwayWorld)

Use template #1 in `email-pitch-templates.md`.

Personalize with:

- Why this matters to BroadwayWorld readers now
- One specific artist story mention (Matt Finlay or A13MW)

### Step 4 - Send Playbill editorial pitch

Email: editorial@playbill.com

Use template #2 in `email-pitch-templates.md` and personalize:

- Subject line tied to artist livelihood + funding model
- Founder performing arts background in first paragraph
- Offer interview + short Q&A format

### Step 5 - Follow-up once

If no reply after 7-10 days, send the follow-up template (#3).

Rules:

- Follow up once per pitch thread
- Keep it short (under 90 words)
- Offer one fresh hook (for example: artist story, upcoming event, or new educational release)

## Editorial angle matrix

Choose one angle per outlet:

1. **Funding model innovation**  
   "A nonprofit testing Bitcoin-native patronage for independent artists"
2. **Artist sovereignty case study**  
   "How creators are using direct support and censorship-resistant rails"
3. **New York community and events**  
   "Workshops and artist/Bitcoiner events building local momentum"
4. **Practical education**  
   "Teaching artists self-custody and Bitcoin payment workflows"

## Outreach quality checklist

Before every send:

- [ ] Subject line is specific and human (not generic PR blast language)
- [ ] First paragraph explains why this outlet's audience should care
- [ ] One-page kit is attached
- [ ] At least two high-quality images are attached
- [ ] All links are live and point to relevant pages
- [ ] Contact information is visible in email signature

## What success looks like

Minimum:

- One published announcement or mention
- One editor relationship started for future stories

Strong:

- One feature + one follow-up interview request
- Repeatable media package ready for future events and grant milestones
