# Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. - One-Page Press Kit

## Organization Snapshot

- **Name:** Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. (BFTA)  
- **Type:** 501(c)(3) nonprofit  
- **Location:** New York, NY  
- **Website:** https://bitcoinforthearts.org  
- **Core thesis:** Build Bitcoin-native patronage so independent artists can create with greater financial and artistic sovereignty.

## Mission (Short Version)

Bitcoin For The Arts supports artists across visual arts, theater, dance, music, writing, storytelling, and film through Bitcoin micro-grants, workshops, residencies, and productions. Our focus is low time preference creative work, transparent stewardship, and long-term support infrastructure.

## What Makes BFTA Distinct

- Public-facing allocation model: **55/30/10/5**
  - **55%** Artist grants  
  - **30%** Programs (workshops, residencies, co-productions)  
  - **10%** Operations  
  - **5%** Bitcoin reserve (HODL Vault; address not public for security)
- Bitcoin-first support model designed to reduce gatekeeping and payment friction.
- Open education resources for artists learning Bitcoin tools and workflows.

## Founder Bio (Press Version)

**Dion Wilson** is Founder & Executive Director of Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. and an accomplished performing artist with 16 years as a principal dancer. He has performed with Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater, Dance Theatre of Harlem, and Philadelphia Dance Company, toured nationally with *The Lion King*, and contributed to the creation of *Fela! The Musical*. Wilson has taught and mentored more than 10,000 students and now focuses on building a Bitcoin-native funding model for independent creators.
