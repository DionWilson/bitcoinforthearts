## 2) Asset packaging checklist

Before sending:

1. Rename files with outlet-friendly names.
2. Include brief captions and credit lines in email body.
3. Keep each image under ~8 MB unless specifically requested.
4. Include one horizontal image and one portrait image when possible.
5. Include logo as both SVG and PNG.

## 4) High-conversion press angles (arts media)

These are tailored to your current public materials and can be pitched without introducing unsupported claims.

### Angle A - New patronage model

**Headline idea:**  
"A New Nonprofit Model Uses Bitcoin to Support Independent Artists"

**Why it works:**  
Clear novelty + mission fit for arts and culture desks.

### Angle B - Artist sovereignty

**Headline idea:**  
"From Platform Dependence to Direct Support: How Artists Are Experimenting with Bitcoin-Native Funding"

**Why it works:**  
Focuses on artist lived experience (Matt Finlay, A13MW, others).

### Angle C - New York arts + community activation

**Headline idea:**  
"New York Nonprofit Brings Bitcoin and Arts Communities Together Through Workshops and Events"

**Why it works:**  
Local relevance with practical community lens.

### Angle D - Education and practical tools

**Headline idea:**  
"Teaching Artists to Get Paid in Bitcoin: Inside BFTA's Education Track"

**Why it works:**  
Service-journalism angle; useful for publication audiences.

### Angle E - DIY fundraising concept

**Headline idea:**  
"Bitcoin Art Night: A Community Fundraising Blueprint for Independent Creators"

**Why it works:**  
Actionable and event-centric for calendar + feature desks.

## 5) Outlet-specific positioning notes

### BroadwayWorld

- Good for announcements and concise releases.
- Keep copy around 400-500 words.
- Lead with practical impact, artist stories, and event/community angle.
- Include at least one strong visual attachment.

### Playbill

- Use a personalized editorial pitch over a generic release.
- Emphasize founder performing arts background + sovereignty funding model.
- Offer interview availability and concise talking points.
